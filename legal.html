LEGAL & PRIVACY

The whole rulebook in plain words. No sneaky clauses, no nine-page nobody-reads-this. If anything here is ever unclear, write to us and we will fix the words.

THE SHORT VERSION
  • Your notes, highlights and progress live on your device. They never leave it.
  • No ads. No trackers. No selling data. There is nothing to sell.
  • If you sign in with Google, we store your name, email and @username - only to make your shelf work.
  • Book summaries here are original study companions, not the books. Please buy the books - every page says so.
  • Audiobooks are public-domain LibriVox recordings, credited to their volunteer readers. Podcasts stream from each show's own official feed, exactly like every podcast app. We host none of it.
  • Gold payments go from your UPI to the creator's UPI, and there is a real 7-day refund promise.
  • Something yours is here and should not be? One message, 48 hours, gone.

  • Personal use. Read, listen, learn, print cheat-sheets for your own study. Do not scrape, resell, re-host or republish the app or its original content without written permission.

2 · PRIVACY

What we collect: if you sign in with Google - your name, email and the @username you pick, plus the reviews and stories you choose to publish. That is the entire list. Everything else - your notes, highlights, reading progress, saved shows, daily streak - is stored only on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

What we never do: no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking pixels, no location data, no contact scraping, no selling or sharing of user data with anyone. Sign out and wipe from Settings removes your synced data. Reading without signing in needs no account at all.

Where data lives: on your phone, and for signed-in readers, on our Supabase (Postgres) database protected by row-level security. Children under 13 (or your country's minimum age) should not create accounts; the library reads fine without one.
