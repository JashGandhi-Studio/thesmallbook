LEGAL & PRIVACY

The whole rulebook in plain words. No sneaky clauses, no nine-page nobody-reads-this. If anything here is ever unclear, write to us and we will fix the words.

THE SHORT VERSION
  • Without an account, everything stays on your phone. Notes, highlights, progress and streaks are saved in your browser only.
  • With an account, we keep what makes it work on every device: your name, email, @username, birth year (private to you), progress, notes, and anything you choose to post.
  • No ads. No trackers. No selling data.
  • Accounts are 13+. Under 18? Use the app with your parent or guardian knowing. Younger kids use Kids mode from a parent's phone.
  • Book summaries here are original study companions, not the books. Please buy the books.
  • Gold is paid by UPI straight to the creator, checked by our server, with a 7-day refund promise.
  • Want your data gone, or something of yours taken down? One email does it.

2 · Privacy and the DPDP Act

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025. In plain words: we collect only what a feature needs, use it only for that feature, and delete it when you ask.

If you never sign in: we receive nothing about you. Your reading data lives in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing site data removes it.

If you sign in with Google: Google confirms who you are and shares your name, email and photo with us. We then keep, in our Supabase database:

  • your name, @username and photo (these are public on your profile);
  • your birth year, kept inside your private account record and never shown to anyone;
  • your reading progress and notes, so they follow you across devices;
  • what you choose to publish or send: stories, comments, reviews, likes, follows and messages;
  • if you buy Gold: the plan, amount, order number, UPI reference (UTR) and expiry date. We never see your bank account, card or UPI PIN.

Services that help run the app, each receiving only what its job needs:

  • Supabase (database and sign-in). Every table is locked so that you can read only your own private rows.
  • Google for sign-in, web fonts, and translation when you switch a book's language (the text being translated is sent).
  • Web3Forms delivers book requests: the title you type, and your email only if you add it.
  • Puter produces the natural reading voices: the passage being read aloud is sent to it.
  • Podcast hosts, Apple's podcast search, rss2json and Archive.org deliver episodes, show artwork and audiobooks. Like any podcast app, your device asks them directly, so they see a normal web request.
  • YouPI (UPI payment matching) and, if offered, Razorpay process Gold payments.

Children: under the DPDP Act anyone under 18 is a child. The app asks for your birth year after sign-in. Accounts are for readers 13 and older, and if you are under 18 please use the app with your parent or guardian knowing. Children under 13 use Kids mode, opened by a parent from their own account, which needs no sign-in and stores nothing about the child. We do not track children's behaviour, do not show ads to anyone, and keep children out of the public Stories space. Reading without an account needs no consent at all.

Your rights: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, name someone to act for you, or withdraw consent. Email thesmallbook.in@gmail.com from your sign-in address. We act within 30 days, well inside the 90-day limit the Rules allow. Deleting your account removes your profile, progress, notes and posts. Payment records are kept only as long as tax law requires.

Grievance officer: Jash Gandhi, Mumbai, Maharashtra, thesmallbook.in@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

If something goes wrong: if a data breach affects you, we will tell you without delay and report it to the Data Protection Board as the Rules require.

6 · Payments, Gold and refunds

Gold is optional. You pay by UPI to the creator's UPI ID. Your bank's confirmation reaches our server through a signed message, and the server, not your browser, switches Gold on for the plan you paid for, until its end date. The app never sees your bank login, card or PIN. What the Gold page promises is exactly what it delivers, and offers never use fake countdowns. If Gold does not work for you, write within 7 days of paying for a full refund, no forms.
