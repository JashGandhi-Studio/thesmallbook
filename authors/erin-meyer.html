The Culture Map

2014 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Erin Meyer's eight-scale framework for decoding cultural differences - so you can communicate, lead and negotiate across borders without stepping on invisible landmines.

Misunderstandings between cultures aren't personality clashes - they're predictable patterns that fall on eight scales: communicating, evaluating, persuading, leading, deciding, trusting, disagreeing and scheduling. Meyer's framework lets you map any culture (including your own) on each scale, so you can translate intentions instead of judging them. The book's promise: culture is not a mystery - it's a map you can learn to read.

Lessons in the summary

  • High-Context vs Low-Context Communication
  • Evaluating: Direct Negative Feedback vs. Indirect
  • Persuading: Principles-First vs Applications-First
  • Leading: Egalitarian vs Hierarchical
  • Deciding: Consensual vs Top-Down
  • Trusting: Task-Based vs Relationship-Based

Lines worth keeping

  • In business, as in life, culture is the silent language we all speak without knowing it.
  • What is obvious to you will be obvious only to you.
  • The best global managers are not those who are culturally blind, but those who can see clearly across cultures.
