How to Talk to Anyone

2003 · 8 min · 6 lessons

People decide who you are in seconds - and every second is coachable: the smile, the eyes, the posture, the first words.

Where most communication books give philosophy, Lowndes gives TECHNIQUES - 92 named micro-tricks drawn from sales, politics, and psychology, organized by situation: first impressions (the Flooding Smile, Sticky Eyes, the Big-Baby Pivot), small talk (matching mood, Prosaic with Passion, wearing a Whatzit), sounding like a somebody (Comm-YOU-nication, the Exclusive Smile), being an insider in any crowd (Scramble Therapy, learning the jobbledygook), praise that works (the Killer Compliment, Little Strokes, the...

Lessons in the summary

  • The First Ten Seconds: Smile, Eyes, Posture
  • Small Talk Is Music, Not Content
  • Comm-YOU-nication: Make Every Sentence About Them
  • Praise, Parties & Phones: The Advanced Rooms
  • The Art of the Opener: Names, Current Events and Icebreakers That Work
  • The Compliment That Lands: Praise the Unseen, Not the Obvious

Lines worth keeping

  • The way you look and the way you move is more than 80 percent of someone's first impression of you.
  • People don't care how much you know until they know how much you care - about them.
  • The success of a party, a friendship, or a business deal often hinges on the first few moments.
