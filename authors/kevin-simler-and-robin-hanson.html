The Elephant in the Brain

2018 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Human behaviour has hidden motives, status, signalling, self interest, and our conscious explanations are usually a flattering after the fact story.

Kevin Simler and Robin Hanson take an uncomfortable position: much of what we do is driven by motives we do not admit to, and the mind hides them from us on purpose because a sincere performance is more convincing. We signal intelligence, kindness, fitness and loyalty, and we narrate it as virtue. Seeing the elephant does not make you cynical, it makes your good acts cheaper to actually do.
