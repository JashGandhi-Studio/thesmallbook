The Practice of Groundedness

2021 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Brad Stulberg argues that presence, patience, acceptance and community make sustained achievement possible without self destruction.

The people who chase peak performance using the standard playbook end up exhausted and no further forward. Stulberg's alternative, groundedness, is a set of practices rather than a state: be where you are, accept what you cannot control, keep a long time horizon, judge the process rather than each session, and stay connected to people who are not impressed by you. It is ambition with a floor.
