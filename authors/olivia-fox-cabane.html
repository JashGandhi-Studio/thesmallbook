The Charisma Myth

2012 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Charisma isn't magic or genetics - it's three trainable signals: presence, power, and warmth. And it starts in your mind, not your mouth.

Cabane's demolition of the born-with-it myth: charisma is a set of BEHAVIORS - and since people read your body language, and body language leaks your mental state, charisma training is primarily INTERNAL: manage the mind and the magnetism follows.
