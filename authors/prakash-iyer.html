The Habit of Winning

2013 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Prakash Iyer shows through Indian success stories that winning is a set of repeatable habits - practice, attitude, effort and finishing.

Prakash Iyer - a former Xerox and Pepsi executive, and captain of the India hockey team as a boy - distills winning into something ordinary: habits, not magic. The book is a collection of true stories - from cricketers to corporate leaders to everyday Indians - each illustrating one small principle. His core message: winning is not a rare talent that some are born with; it is a set of repeatable habits - showing up early, doing the extra rep, choosing attitude over circumstance, finishing what you start.
