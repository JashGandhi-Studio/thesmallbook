This Is Going to Hurt

2017 · 8 min · 8 lessons

Adam Kay kept a diary through six years as a junior doctor in the NHS: hilarious, horrifying, and finally unbearable. It is the funniest book ever written about why doctors quit.

Adam Kay's diaries from his years as an obstetrics and gynaecology trainee record the reality of a modern health system from the bottom of its hierarchy: hundred hour weeks, life and death decisions taken by the exhausted and unsupervised, patients absurd and heartbreaking, and a bureaucracy that thanks its staff in posters.
