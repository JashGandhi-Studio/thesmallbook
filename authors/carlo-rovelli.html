Seven Brief Lessons on Physics

2014 · 7 min · 10 lessons

The whole of modern physics, relativity, quantum, cosmos, time, heat and what we are, in seven lyrical essays a curious teenager could read on a train. Physics as poetry, and poetry as physics.

Rovelli, a loop-quantum-gravity physicist, writes science like an Italian love letter: Einstein's general relativity as a map of curved spacetime where mass bends time itself; quantum mechanics as a granular, relational world where reality is interaction; the cosmos as knots of quanta; time as something that may not exist fundamentally; heat as the author of time's arrow; and finally us, humans, as brief arrangements of matter that got curious.
