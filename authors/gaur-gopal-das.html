Life's Amazing Secrets

2018 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Life is a car with four wheels: personal, relationships, work, and contribution. Ignore any wheel and the whole ride wobbles.

Structured as a single conversation - a drive through Mumbai traffic between the monk and his wealthy, restless friend Harry - the book maps life as a car with four wheels: PERSONAL LIFE (gratitude, mindset, spiritual practice - the driver's own condition), RELATIONSHIPS (forgiveness, sensitive speech, correcting others with care), WORK LIFE (finding your ikigai-like purpose, integrity, handling competition), and CONTRIBUTION (the final wheel that gives the other three meaning - 'the highest purpose'). Lessons in the summary

  • The First Wheel: Gratitude Is the Starting Engine
  • The Second Wheel: Speak Sensitively, Correct Carefully
  • The Third Wheel: Purpose at Work - the Four Quadrants
  • The Fourth Wheel: Contribution - the Rent We Pay
  • The First Wheel Revisited: Gratitude as a Daily Practice, Not a Feeling
  • The Fifth Wheel: Balance - Work, Family, Health and Spirit

Lines worth keeping

  • When we speak about others, their absence should not mean their absence from our sensitivity.
  • Gratitude is not only the greatest of virtues, but the parent of all others.
  • Seva, or selfless service, is the rent we pay for living on this planet.
