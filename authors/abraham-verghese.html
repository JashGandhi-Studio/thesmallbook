The Covenant of Water

2023 · 10 min · 8 lessons

In every generation of one Kerala family, someone drowns. Abraham Verghese's epic follows Big Ammachi and her descendants across the twentieth century as medicine, faith and India itself change around their house by the water.

Spanning 1900 to 1977 in Kerala's backwaters, Abraham Verghese's novel follows a family shadowed by a condition: in each generation, at least one person dies by drowning. Around that mystery he builds a cathedral of a book: Big Ammachi, married at twelve and widowed into matriarchy; a leper doctor, an artist, revolutionaries; the coming of modern medicine; and a final revelation that joins the family's curse to science. It is about how families carry affliction and grace in the same blood.
