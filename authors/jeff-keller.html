Attitude Is Everything

1999 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Your attitude is your window on the world - and success is a three-step chain: think, speak, act. Keep the window clean.

Jeff Keller was an unhappy lawyer until a 3 a.m. infomercial purchase (a self-development course) began the turnaround that became his life's work.
