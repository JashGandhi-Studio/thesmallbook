The Total Money Makeover

2003 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Debt is dumb, cash is king. The 7 Baby Steps - from ₹ emergency fund to wealth building - are the plan.

Dave Ramsey's battle plan for money: get out of debt (except the house) and stay out, using his 7 Baby Steps: (1) save ₹1,000 starter emergency fund, (2) pay off all debt with the debt snowball, (3) save 3-6 months of expenses, (4) invest 15% for retirement, (5) save for kids' college, (6) pay off the house early, (7) build wealth and give.
