Karmayogi: A Biography of E. Sreedharan

2014 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Grand projects are won not by genius but by ruthless discipline: clear targets, honest reporting and refusal to compromise standards.

E. Sreedharan, the 'Metro Man', delivered the Konkan Railway and Delhi Metro - projects the world said couldn't be done on time or budget - through an unfashionable formula: absolute punctuality, personal integrity, meticulous planning, and leading from the front. His career proves that in a system famous for delays and corruption, one man's standard can move mountains.

Lessons in the summary

  • Deadlines Are Sacred - Treat Them Like Contracts
  • Lead From the Front - Be Seen in the Mud
  • Integrity Is a System, Not a Slogan
  • Planning Is 90% of Execution
  • Standards Are Non-Negotiable - Even Under Pressure
  • The Nation's Progress Is Built by Quiet Bureaucrats

Lines worth keeping

  • Work is worship. If you do your duty, everything else follows.
  • The secret of the Metro's success is simple: we never compromised on quality, and we never missed a deadline.
  • Integrity is not a virtue to be praised; it is a system to be practiced.
