Superforecasting

2015 · 11 min · 10 lessons

Forecasting is a skill, not a gift. People who break problems down, think in precise probabilities and update often can predict the future far better than pundits.

Philip Tetlock spent decades studying expert predictions and found that many experts did little better than chance. But in a large forecasting tournament funded by a US intelligence research agency, his Good Judgment Project discovered a group of ordinary people, retired workers, students, engineers, who consistently made remarkably accurate predictions about world events.
