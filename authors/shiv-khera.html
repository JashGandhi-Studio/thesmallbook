You Can Win

1998 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Success is not an accident: attitude, positive thinking, goal-setting, and character - built step by step, the Indian classic's way.

Khera's blockbuster - one of India's best-selling English books ever - is deliberately old-school: success as CONSTRUCTION, assembled from attitude (the foundation: his famous calculation that attitude, not aptitude, determines altitude), motivation that's internal and renewable (the fire from within versus the flash from outside), self-esteem built through integrity and contribution, interpersonal skills grounded in genuine care, subconscious programming through auto-suggestion, goal-setting with the SMART...

Lessons in the summary

  • Attitude: The Foundation Everything Sits On
  • Motivation From Within: Fireproof Fuel
  • Goals: The SMART Discipline Plus the Burning WHY
  • Winners Do Things Differently: The Character Close
  • The Success Mindset: Winners Do Different Things Differently
  • Service and Success: The Paradox of the Giving Hand

Lines worth keeping

  • Winners don't do different things. They do things differently.
  • Your positive action combined with positive thinking results in success.
  • Under adverse conditions - some people break down, some break records.
