Tiny Experiments

2025 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Anne-Laure Le Cunff turns uncertainty from a source of paralysis into a practice: small, time boxed experiments with a clear ending.

The book argues that the desire for certainty produces inaction, and that a better response is to treat life as a series of small trials. Each experiment has a hypothesis, a duration and a review, which turns vague anxiety into data. The practice is not about optimising yourself, it is about learning what is actually true for you.
