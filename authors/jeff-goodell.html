The Heat Will Kill You First

2023 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Jeff Goodell traces what rising heat does to the human body, to cities, to food and to the poor, and why the danger is underestimated.

Goodell's subject is thermodynamic rather than political. A human body survives within a narrow band, and beyond a certain combination of heat and humidity no amount of adaptation helps, because sweat stops working. Heat is unequal, it kills people who cannot afford cooling, and it is reshaping where and how people can live. The book is a warning written as reporting: heat is the first thing that will kill us, and it is already here.

Lines worth keeping

  • Heat kills the poor first, then the old, then everyone else.
  • The climate is not a future problem. It is a present body problem.
