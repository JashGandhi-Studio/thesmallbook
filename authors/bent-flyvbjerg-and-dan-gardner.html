How Big Things Get Done

2023 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Flyvbjerg and Gardner show that the way to finish big things is to think slow, act fast, and use the numbers from everyone else's mistakes.

Most large projects go over budget, over time or both, and the pattern is so regular that it can be predicted in advance. The authors argue for a discipline that feels backwards: slow the planning down until the design is genuinely settled, then build fast with modules that have already worked elsewhere. Optimism is not the enemy. Unmeasured optimism is.
