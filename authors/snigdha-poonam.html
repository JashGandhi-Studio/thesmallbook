Dreamers: How Young Indians Are Changing the World

2018 · 11 min · 8 lessons

A journalist's field report from tier-2 and tier-3 India: the ambition economy of young strivers, its hustle culture, its heroes and its dark side.

Poonam spent years traveling through towns like Ranchi, Indore and Gorakhpur, meeting the generation that refuses to inherit small lives. They join skills academies, practice motivational monologues, run YouTube channels, enter politics young, and treat self-invention as a full-time project. The book is neither mockery nor fan letter: it documents the hustle, the sincerity and the exploitation (of hope, by coaching factories and political machines) that define the world's largest young workforce.
