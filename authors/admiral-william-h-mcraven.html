Make Your Bed

2017 · 8 min · 6 lessons

If you want to change the world, start off by making your bed - small disciplines cascade, and you can't do it alone.

Expanded from the University of Texas commencement speech viewed tens of millions of times, McRaven's ten lessons compress 37 years of Navy service - and six months of SEAL training's deliberate crucible - into a manual for ordinary adversity.
