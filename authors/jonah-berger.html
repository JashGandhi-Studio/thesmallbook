Contagious

2013 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Virality isn't luck - it's engineering: Social currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical value, Stories. Build sharing into the thing itself.

Only 7% of word of mouth happens online - but ALL of it follows the same psychology. Berger's Wharton research distilled six principles (STEPPS): SOCIAL CURRENCY (we share what makes us look good - remarkable facts, insider access, visible achievement); TRIGGERS (top of mind = tip of tongue: link your product to frequent environmental cues - Kit Kat wired itself to coffee breaks and revived a dying brand); EMOTION (high-arousal feelings - awe, anger, excitement, anxiety - drive sharing; sadness and contentment...

Lessons in the summary

  • Social Currency + Triggers: Make Them Look Good, Then Keep Reminding Them
  • Emotion + Public: High Arousal, High Visibility
  • Practical Value + Stories: Useful Trojan Horses
  • Running the STEPPS Audit: Engineering Word of Mouth for Boring Products
  • The Triggers: Keep Reminding Them - and Fit Into Their Lives
  • The Story: Make Your Message a Trojan Horse

Lines worth keeping

  • Virality isn't born, it's made.
  • Top of mind means tip of tongue.
  • People don't share information, they share stories that carry it.
