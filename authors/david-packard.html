The HP Way: How Bill Hewlett and I Built Our Company

1995 · 10 min · 8 lessons

David Packard's account of HP's founding and culture: trust-based management (open labs, no time clocks, management by objectives), and the values that made 'the HP Way' the template.

Packard tells the founding story (Stanford friends, $538 in 1939, the Palo Alto garage, the audio oscillator Disney bought for Fantasia) and the management invention that outlived the products: the HP Way.
