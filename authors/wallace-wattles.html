The Science of Getting Rich

1910 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Wealth comes from thinking in a Certain Way + acting in a Certain Way - the original 'think and grow rich' blueprint.

Written in 1910, Wattles' tiny book argues wealth is not a zero-sum game: there is a thinking stuff from which all things are made, and by thinking in a 'Certain Way' - clear vision, gratitude, and action in the present - anyone can attract the formless substance into their lives. Its practical core: do all you can do today, in your current place, in the most efficient way - and wealth follows as a natural result of a certain habit of mind plus consistent action.

Lessons in the summary

  • Wealth Is Your Right - and It's Not Zero-Sum
  • Thinking in a Certain Way
  • Act in the Present, in the Certain Way
  • Gratitude: The Receiver's Frequency
  • The Advancing Life: Grow or Stagnate
  • Gratitude and Vision Compound Into Creation

Lines worth keeping

  • There is a thinking stuff from which all things are made, and which, in its original state, permeates, penetrates, and fills the interspaces of the universe.
  • Get rich without compromise - that is what you must do.
  • You must relate to the formless mind of the universe in a way that brings you into harmony with it.
