The Fatherhood Field Guide

2020 · 8 min · 8 lessons

Babies do not need a hero. They need a present, competent, boringly reliable man who can change a nappy at 3 a.m. and apologise at 3 p.m. Marcus Webb wrote the manual he was handed nowhere.

Marcus Webb, a father of four and former infantry officer, wrote the field guide he needed: pregnancy from the passenger seat, the hospital, the first year shifts, discipline without shouting, talking so sons open up and daughters stay close, providing without disappearing, and staying married to the children's mother on purpose. Its tone is its thesis: fatherhood is a craft with skills, not an instinct other men were issued.
