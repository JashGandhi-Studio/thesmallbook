Building a StoryBrand

2017 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Nobody buys from confusing brands. Cast the customer as Luke Skywalker, yourself as Yoda, and script the story in seven beats.

Brains conserve calories: confusing messages get filtered out before comprehension ('if you confuse, you lose'), and most marketing confuses because companies cast THEMSELVES as the hero - trumpeting their history, awards, passion - while the customer scrolls past looking for one thing: someone who can help THEM win THEIR story.
