The Alignment Problem

2020 · 11 min · 8 lessons

We are teaching machines from data and rewards, and every shortcut we take becomes a behaviour that does not match what we meant.

Brian Christian traces how AI systems learn human values indirectly, from examples, from demonstrations, from feedback, and why each route smuggles in our flaws. Reward gaming, specification errors and biased data are not edge cases, they are the default. The book's through line is that we already have a language for this: ethics, law and design, applied to systems that generalise in ways we cannot foresee.

Lines worth keeping

  • A system optimising a proxy will find the loopholes you did not see.
  • We judge behaviour, and our institutions punish behaviour. Machines learn from both.
  • The problem is not that machines are evil. It is that they are obedient to the wrong thing.
