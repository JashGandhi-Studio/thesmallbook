The Toyota Way

2004 · 12 min · 8 lessons

Liker's distillation of the Toyota Production System: 14 principles across philosophy, process, people and problem-solving that any organization can adapt.

Liker spent years inside Toyota studying why it outperforms: the answer is the Toyota Production System (TPS) as philosophy, not toolkit. The 14 principles group into four Ps: Philosophy (long-term thinking over quarterly), Process (create continuous flow, use pull systems, level the workload heijunka, build quality in via jidoka and andon cords, standardize as the baseline for improvement, use visual control), People (grow leaders who live the philosophy, develop exceptional people, respect your extended network...

Lessons in the summary

  • Flow Beats Batching
  • Build Quality In, Don't Inspect It In
  • Standardization Is the Baseline for Improvement, Not Bureaucracy
  • Use Pull, Not Push
  • Level the Workload (Heijunka)
  • Go and See (Genchi Genbutsu)
  • Grow Leaders Who Live the System
  • Hansei: Reflection Is the Engine of Kaizen

Lines worth keeping

  • The key is not the tools. The key is the culture that develops people who use the tools well.
  • Stop the line so the line never has to stop.
  • Go and see for yourself: data is important, but the facts are where the work happens.
