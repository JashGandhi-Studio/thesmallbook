The Hype Machine

2020 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Sinan Aral shows how the same networks that spread news also spread misinformation, and how the design of the machine decides which one wins.

Aral, a network scientist, separates the hype from the evidence. Social media does change behaviour at scale, and its incentives select for engagement over accuracy. False news travels further and faster, not because people are stupid but because the machine rewards what holds attention. His fix is not nostalgia, it is redesign: incentives, friction and accountability.

Lines worth keeping

  • The engine does not care whether the story is true. It cares whether it spreads.
  • False news spreads faster because it is engineered to.
  • We do not have a misinformation problem. We have an incentive problem.
