Crossing the Chasm

1991 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Early adopters and mainstream buyers are different species. The gap between them is where startups die - cross it by dominating one beachhead niche at a time.

The technology adoption curve looks smooth in textbooks: innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, laggards. Moore's career-defining insight: there's a CHASM hiding between early adopters and the early majority - and it's where most innovations die. Early adopters are VISIONARIES: they buy dreams, tolerate bugs, want revolutionary change, and don't need references.
