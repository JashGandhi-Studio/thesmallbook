Working Identity

2003 · 11 min · 8 lessons

You find out what you want to become by trying it, not by thinking harder about what you want to be.

Herminia Ibarra studied people who actually changed careers and found the opposite of the standard advice. Nobody worked out the answer first. They acted, borrowed identities, changed their company, and only afterwards told a coherent story about the change. Career change is a series of cheap experiments with real people, not a single decision made at a desk.

Lessons in the summary

  • Act First, Explain Later
  • Test a Possible Self
  • Change Your Company
  • Live With the In Between
  • Ask Who, Not What
  • Rewrite the Story
  • The Sunk Cost Self
  • Build a Bridge, Not a Bonfire

Lines worth keeping

  • We learn who we are by doing, not by introspection.
  • Career change is a process of trial and error, not a decision.
  • The story comes after the move, not before it.
