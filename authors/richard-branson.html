Losing My Virginity

1998 · 12 min · 8 lessons

Richard Branson's memoir of Student magazine, Virgin Records, the airline war against British Airways and the branded-venture empire, plus the rule that made it survivable.

Branson's autobiography covers the first 48 years: the dyslexic schoolboy publishing Student magazine (1968), the mail-order records side that became Virgin Music (with the Manor studio, Mike Oldfield's Tubular Bells, the Sex Pistols), the desperate sale of Virgin Records to Thorn EMI in 1992 (he ran out crying), and the bet-everything launch of Virgin Atlantic, including the famous lawsuit against British Airways' dirty-tricks campaign (won out of court in 1993).
