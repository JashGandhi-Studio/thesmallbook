The 10X Rule

2011 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Wrong targets, not wrong ambition, kill most people: aim 10X higher, expect 10X obstacles, and act at levels others call obsessive.

Cardone's thesis is a double correction: people set targets too LOW (average goals produce average effort - and 'average' in Cardone's math is a failing formula because everyone underestimates the effort required), and then take too little ACTION (the real 10X is the effort side: whatever you think a goal requires, budget ten times the calls, attempts, follow-ups, and years). His framework: success is your DUTY (not a preference - treating it as optional is why it stays optional), there is no shortage of success...

Lessons in the summary

  • The 10X Target: Aim Higher Because Low Aims Lie
  • Success Is Your Duty - and There's No Shortage of It
  • The Four Degrees of Action - Choose Massive
  • Obsession, Omnipresence & Burning the Excuse List
  • Massive Action: The Only Force That Overcomes Fear
  • The 10X Commitment: Quitting Is Not an Option on the List

Lines worth keeping

  • Success is your duty, obligation, and responsibility.
  • Never reduce a target. Instead, increase actions.
  • Average is a failing formula.
