Make It Stick

2014 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Learning sticks when it feels hard: retrieval, spacing and interleaving beat re-reading, cramming and highlighting every time.

The most popular study habits - re-reading, highlighting, cramming - are the least effective because they feel productive while creating 'illusions of competence'. Durable learning comes from effortful retrieval: testing yourself, spacing practice over time, and mixing different problems. Difficulty that slows you down today is the very thing that makes knowledge stick tomorrow.

Lessons in the summary

  • Re-reading Feels Good and Does Little
  • Retrieval Practice Is the Best Investment
  • Spacing Beats Cramming Every Time
  • Interleaving Trains Judgment, Not Just Recall
  • Struggle Is a Signal, Not a Stop Sign
  • Mastery Requires Calibration - Check Yourself Honestly
