All About Love: New Visions

2000 · 8 min · 8 lessons

Love is not a feeling that happens to you. It is the will to nurture your own and another person's growth, and it cannot coexist with abuse.

bell hooks argues that a culture obsessed with love has no shared definition of it, so people call attachment, possession and even harm by love's name. Borrowing M. Scott Peck definition, she calls love the will to extend yourself for another's spiritual growth, built from care, honesty, responsibility and respect. Where there is lying, domination or abuse, there is no love, whatever the feelings involved. That single standard rearranges everything.

Lessons in the summary

  • Define Love or Be Defined by Confusion
  • Honesty Is the Foundation, Not a Feature
  • Abuse and Love Cannot Share a House
  • Self-Love Is Learned Against Resistance
  • Love and Domination Cannot Coexist
  • Greed Is the Enemy Dressed as Ambition
  • Forgiveness Is Not Forgetting, It Is Refusing Revenge
  • Love Is a Practice for Communities, Not Just Couples

Lines worth keeping

  • Love is an action, never simply a feeling.
  • There can be no love without justice.
  • To love well is the most difficult and important work there is.
