The Midnight Library

2020 · 8 min · 8 lessons

Nora Seed, having decided to die, wakes in a library where each book lets her live one unlived life: rock star, glaciologist, mother, Olympian. Matt Haig's fable asks what regret is actually made of.

Nora Seed's life has contracted to a point: job lost, cat dead, brother distant, and she decides to end it. Instead she finds the Midnight Library, run by her old school librarian, where infinite shelves hold her parallel lives. Trying them, she discovers that the glamorous versions carry their own griefs, the swimming medal life holds the same father shaped weather, fame holds addiction, and even the life where she kept the cat is not the rescue she imagined.
