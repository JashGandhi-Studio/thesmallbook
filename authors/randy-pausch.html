The Last Lecture

2008 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Randy Pausch, given months to live, gave a lecture about achieving childhood dreams and the lessons behind them, mostly for his children.

Pausch was a computer scientist dying of pancreatic cancer, and he chose to talk about the brick walls, the help he received and the difference between a head fake and the real lesson. The book's value is its clarity: he separates what he wanted people to know from the situation he was in, and the separation is the discipline.
