Who Moved My Cheese?

1998 · 8 min · 6 lessons

The cheese keeps moving. The question is whether you sniff the air and run - or sit in the empty station demanding it back.

Two mice (Sniff and Scurry) and two little-people (Hem and Haw) live in a maze, hunting cheese - a metaphor for whatever you chase: the job, the relationship, the market, the identity. When Cheese Station C empties, the mice - simple creatures - immediately move on; the little-people, over-thinking and entitled, stay and starve on analysis and outrage. Haw's eventual journey through fear into the maze, and the lessons he writes on the walls, form the whole curriculum: change happens; anticipate it; monitor it;...
