The Coming Wave

2023 · 11 min · 10 lessons

AI and biotechnology will spread faster and cheaper than any technology before. Containing them, keeping them under meaningful human control, is the central challenge of our time.

Mustafa Suleyman, co-founder of DeepMind and later head of AI at Microsoft, argues that a coming wave of technologies, especially artificial intelligence and synthetic biology, will transform society. These technologies are general-purpose, spread quickly, become cheaper and can be used for both good and harm. History shows that technologies proliferate; they rarely stay contained.
