Who Is Michael Ovitz?

2018 · 9 min · 8 lessons

Michael Ovitz built CAA into the agency that ran Hollywood, package deals, superstar clients, total control, then left for Disney's number two job and was fired in fourteen months with a severance that became a scandal. His memoir is the rare confession from a man the town called ruthless.

Michael Ovitz co-founded Creative Artists Agency in 1975 and rewrote Hollywood's power structure, shifting leverage from studios to talent through packaging and relentless information control. The memoir traces the ascent, the martial arts discipline behind his method, and the catastrophic Disney chapter under Michael Eisner that ended in a record payout and public disgrace. It is candid about ambition's machinery: secrecy, loyalty tests, and the loneliness at the top he engineered for himself.
