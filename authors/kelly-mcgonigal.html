The Joy of Movement

2019 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Kelly McGonigal argues that exercise changes mood, memory and social connection, and that the joy is the point rather than a bonus.

McGonigal reframes movement away from calories and towards the body's own chemistry. Exercise produces endocannabinoids, improves mood, sharpens memory and, when done with others, builds belonging. The book's practical claim is that the kind of movement you will keep doing is the one that gives you something back, which is usually not the treadmill.

Lines worth keeping

  • The best exercise is the one you will do again tomorrow.
  • Moving together is one of the oldest ways humans bond.
