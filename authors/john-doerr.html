Measure What Matters

2018 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Ideas are easy; execution is everything. OKRs - Objectives and Key Results - are how the best organizations focus, align, stretch, and actually ship.

John Doerr walked into a 40-person startup called Google in 1999 and taught them the system he'd learned from Andy Grove at Intel: OKRs. An OBJECTIVE is WHAT you want accomplished - significant, concrete, inspirational ('win the developer market'). KEY RESULTS are HOW you'll know you got there - 3-5 specific, measurable, time-bound, verifiable outcomes ('ship v2 by June 30,' '10,000 weekly active developers by Q3') - you either hit the number or you don't; no judgment calls, no 'basically done.'
