Nudge

2008 · 7 min · 9 lessons

A nudge is any small change to the choice environment that predictably improves decisions, without banning anything.

Humans are not Econs - we are Humans: lazy, present-biased, loss-averse and easily confused. Thaler and Sunstein show that the same insights that explain our errors can be used to fix them. The nudge toolbox: smart defaults, good feedback, mapping options simply, and gentle reminders. The goal is 'libertarian paternalism' - steer choices toward welfare while leaving the door open.

Lines worth keeping

  • A nudge is any aspect of the choice architecture that alters people's behavior in a predictable way without forbidding any options.
  • Choices are never merely presented; they are always framed - the question is whether the framing is wise.
  • If you want people to do something, make it easy.
