Chanakya's Chant

2010 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Ashwin Sanghi's thriller pairs ancient Chanakya with a modern strategist - proving that statecraft, intelligence and the long game work in any era.

Sanghi's novel weaves two timelines: the ancient strategist Chanakya engineering Chandragupta's rise, and a modern-day historian who discovers a chant that brings Chanakya's spirit of strategy into contemporary Indian politics. The book is a thriller built on statecraft: alliances, betrayals, information warfare and the patient construction of power.
