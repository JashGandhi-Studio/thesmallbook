Meditations

180 · 8 min · 6 lessons

You have power over your mind - not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength.

Marcus Aurelius ruled Rome at its height - through plague, war, betrayal, and the deaths of most of his children - while writing private notes to himself that he never intended anyone to read. Meditations is that notebook: the most powerful man alive coaching himself daily on the same struggles you have - anger at difficult people, fear of death, distraction, vanity, despair.
