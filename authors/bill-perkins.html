Die With Zero

2020 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Dying with money is dying with unlived life. Spend on experiences at the right age, give while alive, and aim to bounce the last check.

Most personal finance optimizes for the wrong endpoint: maximum money at death. Perkins - energy trader and poker player - argues the real game is maximum LIFE: money is stored life energy, and every rupee unspent at death is hours of your life you traded and never redeemed.
