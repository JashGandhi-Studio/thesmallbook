Braiding Sweetgrass

2013 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Robin Wall Kimmerer braids botany, indigenous knowledge and personal history to argue that gift economies and gratitude are better models of our place in the living world than ownership.

Kimmerer is a trained botanist and an enrolled member of the Citizen Potawatomi Nation, and the book lives in both. Sweetgrass resists harvesting that takes without giving back, food grows best inside relationship, and knowledge is carried by people who were treated as though they had none. Her claim is not that science is wrong, it is that a science without reciprocity can tell you how a plant works and still miss how to live with it.
