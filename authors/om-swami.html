If Truth Be Told

2015 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Om Swami tells the truth about trading success for the Himalayas - corporate emptiness, monastic hardship and the slow practice that built real peace.

Om Swami - who left a successful software career, an Australian lifestyle and a marriage to become a monk in the Himalayas - writes the story of his own search: from a restless corporate life to the austerities of the mountains, from the trauma of his mother's death to the discipline of meditation that eventually grounded him.
