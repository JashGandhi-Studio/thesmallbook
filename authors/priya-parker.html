The Art of Gathering

2018 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Priya Parker argues that most gatherings fail because nobody decided what they were for, and that hosting is a design discipline.

Parker's claim is that the ordinary meeting, dinner and conference are under designed. A host's job is to decide the purpose, choose who is in the room, give the gathering a shape and a set of temporary rules, and end it well. Hospitality is not logistics, it is the deliberate creation of a temporary world with its own physics.

Lines worth keeping

  • The purpose is the why, and most gatherings never state it.
  • A good host populates the room with the right mix, not the biggest list.
  • People remember the shape of a gathering, not its catering.
