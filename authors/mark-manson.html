The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck

2016 · 9 min · 7 lessons

The desire for more positive experience is itself a negative experience. Choose your struggles instead of avoiding them.

Self-help's obsession with positivity backfires: constantly needing to feel good reminds you that you don't. Manson's alternative is subtractive - stop giving attention (f*cks) to things that don't matter so you can give them fully to the few that do. Life is suffering either way; the meaningful question isn't 'what do I want to enjoy?' but 'what pain am I willing to sustain?'
