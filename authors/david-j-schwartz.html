The Magic of Thinking Big

1959 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Believe it can be done. The size of your success is set by the size of your belief - and excusitis is the failure disease.

Schwartz, a Georgia State professor who studied thousands of executives, found the gap between the successful and the mediocre wasn't intelligence, education, or luck - it was the scale of their thinking. Belief triggers the mind to find ways; disbelief triggers it to find excuses. The book is a field manual: cure 'excusitis' (the failure disease in its health, intelligence, age, and luck strains), build confidence through action (action cures fear), think and dream creatively, make your attitudes your allies,...

Lessons in the summary

  • Believe You Can Succeed - and You Will
  • Vaccinate Yourself Against Excusitis
  • Action Cures Fear - Build Confidence by Doing
  • Think Big: Language, Vision, and the Long View
  • Turn Defeat Into Victory & Put Goals to Work
  • How to Think Big: Ask Bigger Questions, See Bigger Pictures

Lines worth keeping

  • Believe it can be done. When you believe something can be done, really believe, your mind will find the ways to do it.
  • Action cures fear. Indecision, postponement, on the other hand, fertilize fear.
  • The thinking that guides your intelligence is much more important than how much intelligence you have.
