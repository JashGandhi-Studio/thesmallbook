Good Energy

2024 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Casey Means argues that the way to feel better is to treat blood sugar, muscle, light and sleep as one system rather than four habits.

Means starts from the body's own signals rather than from a diagnosis. Energy crashes, cravings and poor sleep are the visible surface of metabolic health, and metabolic health responds to a small set of levers: what and when you eat, how much muscle you carry, light in the morning, and sleep. The book asks readers to run their own experiment with measurements rather than accept a rule.

Lessons in the summary

  • Fatigue Is Information
  • The Spike and the Crash
  • Ultra Processed Food
  • Muscle Is a Metabolic Organ
  • Sleep and Blood Sugar
  • Light and Time
  • Stress and Metabolism
  • Running Your Own Test

Lines worth keeping

  • Fatigue is not a character flaw. It is data.
  • A spike is followed by a crash, and the crash is what you feel.
  • The body is a system, not a list of separate habits.
