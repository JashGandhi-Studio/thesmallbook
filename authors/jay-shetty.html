Think Like a Monk

2020 · 8 min · 6 lessons

You don't need to shave your head to think like a monk: detach from noise, find your dharma, and serve your way to peace.

Shetty spent three years as a monk in a Mumbai-area ashram before returning to build a media career - and the book is that curriculum translated: LET GO (strip away the identity built from others' opinions - the 'dust on the mirror'; audit values; starve negativity; transform fear into fuel), GROW (find your dharma - the intersection of passion, expertise, and service; redesign your routine around intentionality; train the monkey mind with the charioteer's discipline; master ego before it masters you), and GIVE...

Lessons in the summary

  • Wipe the Dust Off the Mirror: Identity Detox
  • Fear Is a Signal, Breath Is the Switch
  • Find Your Dharma: Passion + Expertise + Service
  • Routine: Location Has Energy, Time Has Memory
  • Give: Gratitude, Trust Circles & Service as the Cure
  • Detachment Is Not Distance - It's Clarity

Lines worth keeping

  • Your identity is a mirror covered with dust. When you first look, the truth of who you are is hidden.
  • Location has energy; time has memory.
  • When you learn to navigate and manage your breath, you can navigate any situation in life.
