The Maruti Story: How a Public Sector Company Put India on Wheels

2010 · 13 min · 8 lessons

Maruti 800 proved Indian manufacturing could be world class, but only after a public-sector dream was rescued by a Japanese partner, strikes, and ten years of hard learning.

In 1971 Indira Gandhi's government dreamed of a people's car; by 1981 the state-owned Maruti Udyog had produced nothing but files. V. Krishnamurthy rebuilt it with Suzuki as a 26 percent partner, and the Maruti 800 launched in 1983 was cheaper, cleaner and more reliable than the Ambassador and Premier Padmini combined.
