Secrets of the Millionaire Mind

2005 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Give me five minutes and I'll predict your financial future: it's your money blueprint - installed young, running silently, and fully rewritable.

Eker's premise: everyone carries a MONEY BLUEPRINT - a subconscious thermostat set in childhood through verbal programming ('money doesn't grow on trees', 'rich people are greedy'), modeling (how your parents handled money), and specific emotional incidents - and the blueprint trumps everything: knowledge, opportunity, even windfalls (lottery winners revert to their setting; self-made millionaires who lose everything rebuild to theirs). Part one is the rewiring protocol: awareness (trace the file), understanding...

  • The Jars & Acting Despite Fear: The Files That Make It Stick
  • The Envy Files: What You Envy Reveals What You're Not
  • The 'Both' Mindset: You Can Have It All - If You Think So
