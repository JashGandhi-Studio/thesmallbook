The Art of Possibility

2000 · 11 min · 8 lessons

The Zanders offer practices for moving from a world of measurement and scarcity to one of possibility and contribution.

A conductor makes no sound, and the whole practice of leadership follows from that fact. The book is a set of practices, giving an A before the performance, being a contribution rather than a success, lighting a spark, enrolling rather than instructing, that change what a person or an organisation is capable of. It is not positive thinking. It is a discipline of choosing a frame and holding it.
