The Defining Decade

2012 · 9 min · 10 lessons

Eighty percent of life's most significant moments happen by 35. The twenties are not a waiting room: they are the decade when career capital, personality, the brain's last rewiring and partner choice compound fastest.

Clinical psychologist Meg Jay pushes back against the culture of extended adolescence: treating the twenties as a throwaway decade costs real compounding.
