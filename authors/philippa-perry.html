The Book You Wish Your Parents Had Read

2019 · 11 min · 8 lessons

Philippa Perry's rule is simple: what matters is not what happened in your childhood, but how you understand it and repair it with your own child.

This is a book about the relationship, not the routine. Perry, a psychotherapist, argues that most parenting advice is behaviour management, and most of what children need is a parent who can notice their own feelings, stay connected through conflict, and apologise when they get it wrong. Repair matters more than perfection.

Lessons in the summary

  • The Relationship Is the Work
  • Your Childhood Comes With You
  • Repair Beats Perfection
  • All Feelings, Some Behaviour
  • Listen Past the Behaviour
  • The Containing Parent
  • Children Who Feel Too Much
  • The Long Conversation

Lines worth keeping

  • What matters most is not what happened to you but how you made sense of it.
  • The child needs a parent who repairs, not a parent who never fails.
  • All feelings are permissible. Behaviour is where the limits live.
