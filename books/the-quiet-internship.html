✅ 5-Step Action Plan

  1. Write five questions that would help you understand your team’s real work.
  2. Choose one small deliverable and state exactly when it will be ready.
  3. Keep a private log with three lines after each workday.
  4. Ask a manager for one example of excellent work in your role.
  5. Send one update this week with status, risk and next step.

⚠️ When This Doesn't Work

A book can offer a useful lens, not a universal rule. Your money, health, family duties, workplace power and access to support change what is possible. Keep the principle, adapt the practice, and ask for expert help where the stakes are high.
