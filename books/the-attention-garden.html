✅ 5-Step Action Plan

  1. For three days, note one physical condition present when attention slips.
  2. Remove one notification or visual cue for a week.
  3. Set up one cue that tells your brain exactly what begins now.
  4. Reserve a small repeatable block for one thing you want more of.
  5. Turn one vague task into a finishable 20-minute edge.

⚠️ When This Doesn't Work

A book can offer a useful lens, not a universal rule. Your money, health, family duties, workplace power and access to support change what is possible. Keep the principle, adapt the practice, and ask for expert help where the stakes are high.
