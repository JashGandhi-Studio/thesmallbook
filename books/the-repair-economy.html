✅ 5-Step Action Plan

  1. Before a purchase, ask what it will cost to keep working for five years.
  2. Identify one system you can make easier to repair rather than replace.
  3. Ask a repairer one question about how to extend the life of something you own.
  4. Make a maintenance note for one important item today.
  5. When repairing something, ask what standard or warranty the part carries.

⚠️ When This Doesn't Work

A book can offer a useful lens, not a universal rule. Your money, health, family duties, workplace power and access to support change what is possible. Keep the principle, adapt the practice, and ask for expert help where the stakes are high.
