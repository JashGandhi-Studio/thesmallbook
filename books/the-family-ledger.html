✅ 5-Step Action Plan

  1. Create one simple monthly page: money in, essentials, debt, savings and flexible spending.
  2. Name one safety fund and one future goal, even if both begin small.
  3. Schedule a 20-minute money conversation before the next major due date.
  4. List one unpaid responsibility that should be included in a money decision.
  5. Set your household’s pause rule for purchases above a number you choose.

⚠️ When This Doesn't Work

A book can offer a useful lens, not a universal rule. Your money, health, family duties, workplace power and access to support change what is possible. Keep the principle, adapt the practice, and ask for expert help where the stakes are high.
