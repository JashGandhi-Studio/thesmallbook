How do I find my purpose?

Answered from 5 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Purpose is not found in a vision quest, it's detected in patterns. Notice what you do for free, what makes time vanish, and what problems you can't stop noticing. Then test it in small service: purpose is a hypothesis you confirm by helping one person.

A failure that proves it

Why did Gerald Ratner fail?

1991 · EGO · Loss: £500M and his own name

The CEO who called his own products 'crap' forgot the why behind his brand, and lost £500 million in a day.

Never make your customer the punchline. Your brand is what people feel owning your product - and one sentence can reprice that feeling to zero.
