How do I deal with loneliness?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Loneliness is a signal, not a sentence, it points at connection you need to build. Meaning, belonging and small regular contact are the medicine, not a big event.
