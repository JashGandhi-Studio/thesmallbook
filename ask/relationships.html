How do I handle fights in my relationship?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Every fight is really two fights: the topic and the fear underneath. Separate observation from judgment, make it safe for the other person to tell the truth, and remember, you can't win an argument with someone you love.

The books behind the answer

Nonviolent Communication

Marshall B. Rosenberg · 2003 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Behind every criticism is an unmet need trying to speak. Learn the OFNR grammar - observe, feel, need, request - and watch arguments dissolve.

  • Requests, Not Demands, and Empathy as First Aid: Say what you observe, what you feel, what you need, then make a request, not a demand.

The Let Them Theory

Mel Robbins · 2024 · 9 min · 7 lessons

Let them do what they do. Then let ME decide what I do about it. That's the whole theory - and it changes everything.

  • Let Me: The Half Everyone Skips: You control your side: set the boundary, then let them choose. Trying to control their half is how fights never end.

Supercommunicators

Charles Duhigg · 2024 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Every discussion is secretly one of three conversations - practical, emotional, or social. Supercommunicators detect which, match it, and loop until the other side feels heard.

  • Hard Conversations: Safety, Identity, and the Why Beneath the Want: Make the conversation safe first, when people feel safe, they tell the truth instead of defending.

Dale Carnegie · 1936 · 11 min · 8 lessons

You can make more friends in two months by becoming interested in other people than in two years trying to get people interested in you.

A failure that proves it

Why did Elvis Presley fail?

1967-77 · TRUST · Loss: Half of everything, and the world

Elvis handed one man total control of his life, and it ended in isolation and ruin. In love and business, never surrender your whole self.

Audit the people you trust most - precisely because you trust them most. Any advisor who can't survive scrutiny is charging you for the absence of it.
