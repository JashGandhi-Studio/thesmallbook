How can I read more books?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Start with 5 pages a day, not 50, the streak matters more than the session. Keep a book where you usually scroll. And summaries count: finishing one small read builds the identity of a reader faster than abandoning a big one.
