What does the future of AI mean for me?

Answered from 2 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

The old agenda, famine, plague, war, is mostly won. The new frontier is data and algorithms that may know you better than you know yourself. Your edge: understand your own mind, keep your data sovereign, and hold one fully human skill.

The books behind the answer

Homo Deus

Yuval Noah Harari · 2015 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Famine, plague and war are being beaten - next, humans will try to upgrade into gods. The future belongs to whoever understands the data.

  • The Algorithm Knows You Better Than You Do: Authority is shifting from human experience to data processing. Know thyself, before the algorithm does.
  • Dataism: The New Religion: Information flow becomes the supreme value, and your data becomes your contribution.
  • Meaning Is a Story, So Choose It Wisely: Humans run on shared stories. Since meaning is a story, you can edit yours.

Range

The Tiger Woods path (specialize at 2) wins in golf. Almost everywhere else, the Roger Federer path (sample, quit, switch, integrate) wins - because life is a wicked game.

A failure that proves it

Why did Blockbuster fail?

2000 · BUSINESS · Loss: 9,000 stores → 1

Blockbuster ignored the future until the future ignored it. The question isn't whether AI changes things, it's whether you prepare.

If your profits come from customer pain, you've already written the invitation for your disruptor. What annoys your customers is someone else's business plan.
