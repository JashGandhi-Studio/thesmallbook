How do I make new friends as an adult?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Friendship is frequency plus vulnerability: repeated low-stakes contact, then a moment where you show something real. Join a recurring group, sport, book club, volunteer, where you'll see the same people weekly. Then be the one who invites first. Adult friendship is a practice, not a luck event.
