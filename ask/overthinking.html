How do I stop overthinking everything?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Overthinking is a motion without movement. Set a deadline for the decision, take the smallest test step, and let action answer what thinking can't.

The 5 Second Rule

Mel Robbins · 2017 · 8 min · 6 lessons

You'll never feel like it. Count 5-4-3-2-1 and move before your brain kills the impulse - a launch code for the life you keep postponing.

  • The Activation Energy Problem: Your brain stalls to protect you. Count down and launch, action breaks the loop.

A failure that proves it

Why did Digg fail?

2010 · STARTUP · Loss: $175M valuation → $500k sale

Digg deliberated for years, then shipped a redesign that destroyed it in a weekend. Decide, ship, learn, in that order.

Your power users are the product. Change their rituals gradually and with them, never at them - communities don't complain twice; they leave once.
