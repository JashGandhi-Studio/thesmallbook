How do I focus without distractions?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Focus is environmental before it's mental. Phone in another room, one tab open, 45-minute blocks with a visible timer. Distraction is a cue problem, not a willpower problem, remove the cue and the craving dies. Train the muscle daily; it grows like any other.
