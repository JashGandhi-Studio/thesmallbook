How do I give feedback without hurting people?

Answered from 2 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Praise in public, criticize in private, specifically, kindly, immediately. Radical candor means caring personally AND challenging directly; anything less is ruinous empathy.

The books behind the answer

Radical Candor

Kim Scott · 2017 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Care personally + challenge directly. Anything less is ruinous empathy, obnoxious aggression, or manipulative insincerity.

  • Challenge Directly: Praise in Public, Criticize in Private: Specific praise publicly; private, kind, specific correction. Never let problems fester for the annual review.
  • The 2×2: Four Ways to Be a Boss: Care personally + challenge directly. Ruinous empathy (care without challenge) quietly destroys teams.
  • The Team Culture: Make Feedback a Habit: Run plus/delta debriefs after every meeting, feedback stops being scary when it's normal.

No Rules Rules

Reed Hastings & Erin Meyer · 2020 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Hire the best, tell them the truth, and let them make decisions - the Netflix culture machine, explained by its founder.

  • Radical Candor: Feedback Is Love: At Netflix, hiding problems to be polite is treated as disloyalty. Feedback is a gift, give it daily.

A failure that proves it

Why did Boeing's Culture Trade fail?

2019 · EGO · Loss: $20B+, 346 lives, a century of trust

Boeing's culture made honest feedback impossible, and planes fell. The feedback you avoid is the cost you pay.

Culture isn't a poster; it's who wins arguments. The day your cost-cutters can overrule your craftsmen on safety-critical calls, the countdown starts - you just don't know the number.
