How do I network without feeling fake?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Networking is not collecting contacts, it's being genuinely interested. Give before you ask, and the connections follow naturally.

The books behind the answer

How to Win Friends & Influence People

Dale Carnegie · 1936 · 11 min · 8 lessons

You can make more friends in two months by becoming interested in other people than in two years trying to get people interested in you.

  • Six Ways to Make People Like You: Be interested, not interesting. Ask about them and the conversation takes care of itself.

How to Talk to Anyone

Leil Lowndes · 2003 · 8 min · 6 lessons

People decide who you are in seconds - and every second is coachable: the smile, the eyes, the posture, the first words.

  • Small Talk Is Music: Small talk is the bridge to big talk. Master the rhythm: listen, echo, ask one more.

The Charisma Myth

Olivia Fox Cabane · 2012 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Charisma isn't magic or genetics - it's three trainable signals: presence, power, and warmth. And it starts in your mind, not your mouth.

  • Presence Drills: Charisma is mostly presence: put the phone down, look at people, be where you are.

Supercommunicators

Charles Duhigg · 2024 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Every discussion is secretly one of three conversations - practical, emotional, or social. Supercommunicators detect which, match it, and loop until the other side feels heard.

  • The Three Conversations: Ask what kind of conversation they want, then match it. Depth beats volume.
