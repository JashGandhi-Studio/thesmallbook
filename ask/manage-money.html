How do I manage my money properly?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Pay yourself first, automate a fixed percentage out of every income before spending. Track where money goes for a month; awareness alone cuts waste. Build a 3-6 month emergency fund before any investing. Boring beats brilliant.
