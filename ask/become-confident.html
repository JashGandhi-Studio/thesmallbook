How do I become more confident?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Confidence is built from evidence, not affirmations. Keep small promises to yourself, stack visible wins, and rehearse the uncomfortable thing until it's boring. The person who has done the thing 50 times doesn't need to feel confident, they know.
