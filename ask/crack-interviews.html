How do I crack job interviews?

Answered from 3 books · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Interviews are won before the room: research the company's real problems, rehearse your stories out loud, and frame answers as evidence, not claims. Confidence is preparation you can't see. End every answer with what you learned and what you'd do next.
