How do I manage a team effectively?

Answered from 2 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

A manager's output is the team's output. Maximize leverage, run meetings that decide things, give honest reviews, and adapt your style to each person's maturity.

The books behind the answer

High Output Management

Andy Grove · 1983 · 8 min · 6 lessons

A manager's output = the output of their team. Leverage, meetings, and performance reviews done right - the Grove way.

  • Your Output = Your Team's Output: You're not managing if the team isn't producing more because of you.
  • Leverage: The Manager's Force Multiplier: Training, 1:1s and direction-setting are high leverage; doing their work is not.
  • Meetings Are Where Work Happens, Run Them Right: A meeting without a decision is a waste of everyone's leverage.

The Five Dysfunctions of a Team

Patrick Lencioni · 2002 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Every team failure traces to one of five dysfunctions - and they cascade. Fix trust first; everything else follows.

  • Dysfunction 1: Absence of Trust: Every team failure cascades from missing trust. Build vulnerability first.

A failure that proves it

Why did Enron fail?

2001 · FRAUD · Loss: $74B shareholder value, 20,000 jobs

Enron hired the smartest people and rewarded the worst behavior. Culture eats strategy, manage the culture.

Culture is what you tolerate - a workplace where bad news is career death guarantees you'll hear it last, when it's fatal. And never hold your salary AND savings in the same company's fate.
