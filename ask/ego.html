How do I keep my ego in check?

Answered from 4 books and 2 failure cases · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Ego is the enemy of learning: it makes you defend instead of improve. Talk less, listen more, and compare yourself to yesterday, not to the room.

A failure that proves it

Why did Elizabeth Holmes fail?

2018 · FRAUD · Loss: $9B valuation → 11 years prison

Theranos believed its own hype until a $9 billion empire evaporated. Ego makes you stop checking the facts.

Vision without truth is fraud with better branding. And prestige is not due diligence: nobody on that legendary board asked to see the machine work.

Why did Adam Neumann fail?

2019 · EGO · Loss: $47B → $8B in six weeks

WeWork's founder built a $47 billion valuation on ego, and lost control of it in weeks.

Charisma is a multiplier on the underlying business - and the underlying business was renting desks. When the founder's mystique IS the moat, the IPO prospectus is the exorcism.
