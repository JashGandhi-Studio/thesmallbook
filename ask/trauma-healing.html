How do I heal from past trauma?

Answered from 2 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Trauma lives in the body, not just the mind, so healing must include the body: safety, connection, breath and movement. Your brain can rewire at any age; the alarm can learn the danger is over.

The Body Keeps the Score

Bessel van der Kolk · 2014 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Trauma lives in the body, not just the mind. Heal it through the body - movement, breath, connection, and safe re-experiencing.

  • Trauma Lives in the Body: Trauma is a physical imprint, the body keeps reliving danger the mind believes is over.
  • Connection Is the Antidote: The single most protective factor in recovery is one safe person. Connection is medicine.
  • You Can Rewire the Brain, At Any Age: Neuroplasticity means the brain can learn safety even after decades of terror.
