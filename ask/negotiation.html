How do I negotiate a better salary / deal?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Negotiation is not battle, it's discovery. Ask calibrated questions, name their fears, and let silence do the heavy lifting.

The books behind the answer

Never Split the Difference

Chris Voss · 2016 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Negotiation isn't logic or compromise - it's emotional intelligence weaponized: tactical empathy, calibrated questions, and 'No.'

  • Tactical Empathy & Labeling: Label their fear out loud: 'It sounds like the budget is fixed.' The other side opens up when they feel heard.

Getting to Yes

Roger Fisher & William Ury · 1981 · 8 min · 6 lessons

Don't bargain over positions: separate people from problems, focus on interests, invent options, insist on objective criteria - and know your BATNA.

  • Invent Options for Mutual Gain: Grow the Pie Before Cutting It: Don't argue positions, dig for the interest underneath. There's always a deal that serves both sides.

Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion

Robert B. Cialdini · 1984 · 9 min · 7 lessons

Persuasion runs on fixed psychological triggers: reciprocity, commitment, social proof, liking, authority, scarcity, unity. Know them or be played by them.

  • Reciprocity: Give first, and the other side feels a pull to return it. Small genuine gifts open big doors.

Read the summary · Open the interactive book

The 48 Laws of Power

Robert Greene · 1998 · 22 min · 16 lessons

Power is a social game. Learn its laws - to play them or to defend against those who play them on you.

  • Never Outshine the Master: In salary talks, make the other side feel like the winner, people pay more when they feel powerful.

A failure that proves it

Why did Nicolas Fouquet fail?

1661 · EGO · Loss: Freedom - died in prison

Nicolas Fouquet threw the most spectacular party in France to impress the king, and got jailed for life. Read the room before you flex.

Never outshine the master. Making your boss feel inferior is not networking - the brighter you shine upward, the darker the response comes down.
