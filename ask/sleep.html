How do I sleep better at night?

Answered from 4 books and 1 failure case · simple English · Hindi and Hinglish in the reader

The short answer

Sleep is the foundation every other performance sits on. Guard it like a salary: fixed schedule, deep rest cycles, and a morning routine that sets your body clock.

A failure that proves it

Why did Chernobyl fail?

1986 · EGO · Loss: Uninhabitable zone, countless lives

Chernobyl's fatal test was run by sleep-deprived operators pushing through a night shift. Never make big calls on no rest.

Cultures that punish bad news manufacture catastrophes, then are surprised by them. The truth was available at every step; the hierarchy just made it unspeakable. What you don't allow said, you'll eventually see exploded.
