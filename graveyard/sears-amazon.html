Why Did Sears Fail?

🏬 The Original 'Everything Store' That Forgot Itself · 2018 · 💸 132 years → bankruptcy

📜 What Happened

Sears WAS Amazon a century early: the catalog that delivered everything to every doorstep in America — even entire houses. It had the brands, the logistics, the customer trust, and (in its Discover card and Prodigy network!) the raw pieces of e-commerce before e-commerce. Instead, hedge-fund management pitted internal divisions against each other in Ayn Rand-style internal markets, starved stores of investment, and financial-engineered the giant to death. Bankruptcy: 2018.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

It abandoned its own founding insight (delivery-to-everyone) exactly when technology finally made that insight omnipotent.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Companies rarely die of old age — they die of forgetting why customers loved them. Your origin story often contains your future strategy; Sears sold its own playbook to Bezos for free.
