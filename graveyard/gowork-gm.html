Why Did General Magic Fail?

🪄 The iPhone, Built 15 Years Too Early by the Wrong Decade · 2002 · 💸 $90M+; the future, mistimed

📜 What Happened

Spun out of Apple in 1990 with its most brilliant engineers, General Magic built... the smartphone: touchscreen, apps, emoji, e-commerce, cloud sync — in 1994. Partners (Sony, Motorola, AT&T) shipped devices; consumers, who barely had email, shrugged. The web arrived and ate its proprietary network. It died quietly — while alumni Tony Fadell (iPod/iPhone), Andy Rubin (Android), and Megan Smith went on to build the exact future it prophesied.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Right vision, wrong decade — built for infrastructure (wireless data, consumer readiness) that didn't exist, with no bridge product to survive until it did.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Being early is indistinguishable from being wrong — unless you build the boring bridge that keeps you alive until the world catches up. Timing isn't luck; it's a survival design problem.
