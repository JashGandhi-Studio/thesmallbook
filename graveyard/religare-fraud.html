Why Did Religare Enterprises Fail?

The Singh Brothers Looted Their Own Financial Flagship

2018YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 2,300+ crore INR diverted; promoters arrestedTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Billionaire heirs Malvinder and Shivinder Mohan Singh controlled one of India's most respected corporate groups after selling pharmaceutical giant Ranbaxy to Daiichi Sankyo for $4.6 billion. They channeled funds into Fortis Healthcare and NBFC powerhouse Religare Enterprises. But behind the scenes, the brothers systematically routed thousands of crores from Religare's lending arm (Religare Finvest) through dummy shell companies back to their private holdings to cover personal debt and spiritual commune investments. In 2018, independent directors blew the whistle. Both brothers were arrested by the Delhi Police Economic Offences Wing in 2019, losing control of the entire empire.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treating a public financial services company as a personal family treasury through fraudulent circular loan structures.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Inherited wealth and corporate pedigree are zero guarantee of fiduciary ethics. Circular lending to affiliated shell companies is always detected when market liquidity tightens.
