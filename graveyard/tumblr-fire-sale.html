Why Did Tumblr Fail?

🩺 From $1.1 Billion to $3 Million: The Internet's Greatest Fire Sale · 2019 · 💸 Bought for $1.1B, sold for $3M — a 99.7% loss

📜 What Happened

Tumblr was the cultural internet of the 2010s — 500 million blogs, the home of fandoms, art and internet culture. Yahoo bought it in 2013 for $1.1 billion. Then the mismanagement classic: Yahoo forced ad-riddled redesigns that alienated users, and in 2018 the parent company (Verizon by then) banned adult content — the platform's most distinctive community — causing traffic to crash 30% in weeks. In 2019, Verizon sold Tumblr for $3 million — 0.3% of what Yahoo paid. The internet's cultural home became corporate real estate that got flipped into a loss.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Buying a beloved community and optimizing it like a spreadsheet — every 'growth' move (ads, bans) destroyed the very culture that made it valuable.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Community is the asset. When you buy a community, you're buying trust — and trust dies the moment you treat users like inventory. Monetize communities by serving them, not squeezing them.
