Why Did Volta Trucks Fail?

The Electric Truck Stopped by Its Supply Chain

2023YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Production plans halted as the company entered bankruptcyTHE BURN

What happened

Volta Trucks designed a city delivery truck around the driver, raised substantial venture funding and took orders from major logistics groups. Its path to volume depended on outside suppliers, including battery supplier Proterra. When Proterra failed and other production problems mounted, Volta could not finance the long gap between prototypes, certification and paid deliveries. The Swedish group filed for bankruptcy in October 2023.

The fatal mistake

Building a vehicle company with too little control over the components and cash reserves needed to survive supplier failure.

The lesson

A preorder book is not resilience. If one supplier can stop the line, the supplier is part of the product and part of the risk register.
