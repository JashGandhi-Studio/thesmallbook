Why Did Party City Fail?

A Global Helium Shortage Deflated the Balloon Empire

2023YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $1B+ in debt; Chapter 11 restructuringTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Party City operated over 800 party-supply stores across North America, deriving a huge chunk of its high-margin revenue from custom foil and latex balloon bouquets for birthdays and graduations. But in 2019 and 2022, global helium shortages hit the industrial gas market, driving wholesale helium prices up over 100 percent and leaving stores with empty tanks. Meanwhile, digital discounters like Amazon and party sellers on Temu undercut its paper plates, costumes, and plastic decor. Saddled with $1 billion in debt, Party City filed Chapter 11 bankruptcy in January 2023.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Relying on a single volatile scarce industrial commodity (helium) for store foot traffic without alternative margin drivers.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If your primary customer draw depends on an unhedged natural resource, supply shocks will strangle your foot traffic while e-commerce eats your remaining commodity inventory.
