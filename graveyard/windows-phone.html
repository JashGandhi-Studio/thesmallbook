Why Did Windows Phone Fail?

📱 Microsoft's Mobile OS — 0.1% Share and a Forgotten Tombstone · 2017 · 💸 Billions spent; a market Microsoft owned and then lost

📜 What Happened

In 2007, Microsoft owned the mobile OS market — Windows Mobile had over 50% share before the iPhone. Then a decade of missteps: a botched transition, an app ecosystem developers ignored, and a strategy that kept changing direction. Windows Phone launched to critical praise (the tile UI was genuinely good) but never cracked 3% — and by 2017 Microsoft had killed it, exiting mobile entirely. The company that invented the smartphone platform category watched Apple and Google take everything.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Failing to commit — changing mobile strategy repeatedly while developers and users waited to see if Microsoft would stay the course.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

In platform markets, hesitation is death: developers build for winners, and users follow apps. A good product with a scared parent loses to a decent product with a committed one.
