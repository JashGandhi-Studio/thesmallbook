Why Did IRL App Fail?

🤖 The Unicorn Whose Users Were 95% Bots · 2023 · 💸 $170M+ raised on fake users

📜 What Happened

Social app IRL ('In Real Life') raised $170M+ — SoftBank led a round valuing it at $1.17B — on the strength of 20 million claimed monthly users chatting about events. After employees raised doubts and the board investigated, the finding was surreal: roughly 95% of IRL's 'users' were bots or automated accounts. The real user base of the billion-dollar social network was a rounding error. The company shut down in 2023; the SEC later charged the founder with fraud.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Metrics manufactured to impress investors instead of a product built to attract humans — the growth chart was the product.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Fake growth is a loan from reality at loan-shark interest. Every vanity metric you inflate must eventually be repaid in truth, publicly, at the worst moment. Build for users, not for the slide deck.
