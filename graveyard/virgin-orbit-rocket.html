Why Did Virgin Orbit Fail?

Launching Rockets From the Belly of a Boeing 747

2023YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $3.7B SPAC valuation; total Chapter 11 bankruptcyTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Richard Branson's Virgin Orbit promised responsive, flexible space launch by air-launching its LauncherOne rocket from under the wing of a modified Boeing 747 named Cosmic Girl, operating out of commercial airport runways worldwide. It went public via a $3.7 billion SPAC in late 2021. But air-launching rockets introduced severe payload weight penalties and complex mechanical aerodynamics compared to standard ground-based vertical rockets. In January 2023, its historic inaugural orbital mission from Cornwall, UK, suffered a fuel filter dislodgement that caused the rocket to tumble into the ocean. Running out of operating funds, Virgin Orbit shut down and filed Chapter 11 in April 2023.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Pursuing an operationally complex air-launch architecture that carried inferior payload economics compared to SpaceX's reusable Falcon 9.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Novelty in delivery architecture cannot compensate for poor cost-per-kilogram unit economics. In launch services, payload capacity and launch cadence beat theatrical runway takeoffs every time.
