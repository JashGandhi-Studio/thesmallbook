Why Did Air Namibia Fail?

The desert airline is grounded

2021YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Liquidated in 2021THE BURN

What happened

Air Namibia connected a vast, sparse country to the world and carried national pride far beyond its passenger numbers. Governments kept funding the flag because the map argued for it. Debt, aircraft leases and the pandemic pushed the state to stop paying. The airline was liquidated in 2021, leaving routes, staff and a national symbol to be argued over in court and parliament.

The fatal mistake

Treating a public service as an airline and an airline as a public service without funding either honestly.

The lesson

If the mission is national connection, price it as policy. If it is a business, run it as one.
