Why Did AOL–Time Warner Fail?

💽 The Worst Merger in History · 2000 · 💸 $99B write-down (one year)

📜 What Happened

At the dot-com peak, AOL used its inflated stock to buy Time Warner — $350 billion combined, the largest merger ever. The cultures despised each other from day one; the dot-com crash vaporized AOL's value; broadband made AOL's dial-up business a museum piece mid-merger. In 2002 the company posted a $99 billion loss — still among the largest annual corporate losses ever recorded. Both sides spent a decade undoing it.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Merging at the exact top of a bubble, using bubble currency, on a thesis ('synergy') no one could define operationally.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When the deal's logic is a buzzword, the price is the confession. Timing plus ego plus 'synergy' is how two strong companies make one weak one.
