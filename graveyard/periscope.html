Why Did Periscope Fail?

The live pioneer logs off

2021YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
App shut in 2021THE BURN

What happened

Periscope made phone livestreaming feel instant and public just as Meerkat created the moment. Twitter bought it before launch and gave it a huge audience door. Live video became a feature everywhere rather than a destination. Usage and economics weakened, spam and moderation costs stayed high, and Twitter closed the standalone app in 2021 while absorbing live tools into the main service.

The fatal mistake

Mistaking a new behaviour for a durable standalone network.

The lesson

When platforms copy the feature and own the audience, a pioneer needs a second moat fast.
