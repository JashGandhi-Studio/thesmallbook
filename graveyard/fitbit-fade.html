Why Did Fitbit Fail?

The Wrist King Squeezed From Both Ends

2021YEAR
☠️ TECHCAUSE
💸 Over 90% of its peak value before selling to GoogleTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Fitbit created the fitness tracker, sold 100 million devices and reached the New York Stock Exchange in 2015 worth billions. Then the squeeze came from both ends: the Apple Watch took the premium wrist with a real platform and app store, while Xiaomi took the cheap wrist for a tenth of the price. Fitbit's answer, its own smartwatch and subscription, arrived late and thin. The stock fell over 90% from its peak, and Google bought what was left in 2021 for $2.1 billion.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Owning a feature, not a platform: a step counter is one sensor in someone else's computer, and features get absorbed.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When your whole product can become one icon on a bigger device, your lead is a countdown. Move up into services and software before the platform arrives.
