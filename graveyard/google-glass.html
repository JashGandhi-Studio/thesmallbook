Why Did Google Glass Fail?

🥽 $1,500 to Be Called a 'Glasshole' · 2015 · 💸 Consumer launch dead in 2 years

📜 What Happened

Google launched Glass with skydivers livestreaming into its I/O keynote — the most spectacular product demo ever. Then reality: $1,500 for a beta product, battery dying in hours, and a face-mounted camera that made everyone around the wearer feel recorded. Bars banned it; 'Glasshole' entered the dictionary; wearers reported strangers' open hostility. The consumer product retreated in 2015 — the lesson being social, not technical.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Solved every engineering problem and zero social ones — nobody asked how the people NOT wearing it would feel.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Products live in society, not in specs. If your product makes bystanders uncomfortable, the bystanders are also your market — and they voted.
