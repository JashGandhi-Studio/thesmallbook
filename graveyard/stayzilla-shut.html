Why Did Stayzilla Fail?

🛏️ Burned Cash for Growth in 900 Cities, Profitable in None · 2017 · 💸 $34M raised; shut down mid-fight

📜 What Happened

Stayzilla was India's homestay pioneer — Airbnb before Airbnb mattered in India — live in 900 cities with 55,000 stays. Its playbook was the era's: discounts and cashbacks to buy growth, expansion everywhere at once, unit economics postponed. When the 2016 funding winter arrived, there was no profitable core to retreat to — 900 shallow markets instead of 10 deep ones. The founder honestly admitted the model 'was ahead of its market' and shut it down, then faced a criminal complaint from a vendor over dues — a founder's worst month, in public.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Spreading capital across 900 cities to impress the next investor instead of making 10 cities actually work.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Depth beats breadth when the money stops. A business that works in one city can be copied to a hundred; a business that half-works in 900 cities can only be shut down.
