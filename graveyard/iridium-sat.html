Why Did Iridium Fail?

📡 $5 Billion for Phones That Didn't Work Indoors · 1999 · 💸 $5B — sold for $25M

📜 What Happened

Motorola's Iridium launched 66 satellites for a global phone — engineering's Everest, conceived in 1987 when cellular was patchy. By its 1998 launch, GSM roaming covered the profitable world. The Iridium handset: $3,000, brick-sized, $7/minute — and needing line-of-sight to sky, it FAILED INDOORS AND IN CARS, where businesspeople live. It won 20,000 subscribers against a 500,000 break-even. Bankruptcy in nine months; the $5B constellation sold for $25M.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

An 11-year build with no checkpoint asking 'is the problem still unsolved?' — the market moved; the plan, magnificently, didn't.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Long projects need scheduled kill-reviews against the CURRENT world, not the founding memo. Engineering achievement is not exempt from the question 'who still needs this?'
