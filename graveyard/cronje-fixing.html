📜 What Happened

South Africa's beloved cricket captain — the born-again Christian, the leader of leaders — was caught on Delhi police wiretaps fixing matches for bookmakers. His initial confession: he'd taken money but 'never fixed a game'; the King Commission revealed years of payments, and teammates he'd tried to recruit into throwing matches. Banned for life at 30. 'An unfortunate love of money,' he called it himself.
