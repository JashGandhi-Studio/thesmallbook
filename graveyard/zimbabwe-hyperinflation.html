Why Did Zimbabwe Hyperinflation Fail?

The 100 Trillion Dollar Note Was Worth About Forty Cents

2008YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
Official inflation of 89.7 sextillion percent; prices doubling every day; the currency abandoned mid-sentenceTHE BURN

What happened

Land reform wrecked farm output, the government printed to cover spending, and by November 2008 Zimbabwe's inflation annualised at 89.7 sextillion percent - prices doubling every day or so. The central bank issued a 100 trillion dollar note that bought less than a bus ticket. In 2009 the currency was simply abandoned: shops priced in US dollars and rand, and the trillions became souvenirs. The lesson survived the currency.

The fatal mistake

Printing to spend while production collapsed - each new note taxed the previous ones until money stopped functioning as money, one zero at a time.

The lesson

A currency is a claim on what an economy makes. When the claim grows and the output shrinks, the exchange rate finds the truth no matter what the printing press says.
