Why Did AltaVista Fail?

The search pioneer is switched off

2013YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Closed by Yahoo in 2013THE BURN

What happened

AltaVista once indexed the web with a power that felt miraculous and helped a generation learn to search. It passed through owners while Google rewrote expectations around speed and relevance. Portal strategies distracted it and the brand became a Yahoo side door. Yahoo closed AltaVista in 2013, ending a pioneer whose name had once been a synonym for finding things online.

The fatal mistake

Treating search as a feature of portals while a rival treated it as the product.

The lesson

Focus is not a slogan when the market rewards one job done relentlessly.
