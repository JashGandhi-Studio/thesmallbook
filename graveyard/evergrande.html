Why Did China Evergrande Fail?

🏗️ The $300 Billion Property Empire That Drowned in Debt · 2021 · 💸 $300B+ debt — the largest default in history

📜 What Happened

Evergrande was China's largest property developer — a symbol of the boom that built cities overnight. Its model: borrow endlessly, build endlessly, sell presale apartments, borrow more. When Beijing cracked down on developer debt and home sales froze, the music stopped: Evergrande owed over $300 billion, missed its bond payments, and began the largest default in financial history. Construction sites across China went silent with half-sold towers; the company that once topped the world's developers became a monument to leverage.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treating debt as free fuel — growth funded by borrowing that assumed prices would rise forever, with no room for a single bad year.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Leverage is a loan on the future — and the future always arrives. If your survival depends on never having a bad year, you don't have a business; you have a countdown. Debt should fund what you can survive without.
