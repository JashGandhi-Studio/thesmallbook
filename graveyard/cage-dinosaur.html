Why Did Nicolas Cage Fail?

🦖 Castles, Cobras & a Stolen Dinosaur Skull · 2009 · 💸 $150M spent to tax debt

📜 What Happened

Cage earned over $150 million and spent it on: two European castles, an island in the Bahamas, 15 mansions, a fleet of yachts, shrunken pygmy heads, two king cobras, and a $276,000 Tarbosaurus skull — which turned out to be stolen from Mongolia and had to be returned. By 2009 he owed the IRS $6.3 million and spent the next decade doing back-to-back movies to dig out.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treating income as infinite and collectibles as investments. (The dinosaur skull wasn't even legally his.)

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Lifestyle inflation has no ceiling — there is always a weirder thing to buy. Wealth is what you DON'T see, and Cage made all of it visible.
