Why Did DeLorean Fail?

The Stainless Steel Dream That Ran Out of Road

1982YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 About $175M of other people's money, and the company in three yearsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

John DeLorean walked out of General Motors to build the car of the future: stainless steel, gullwing doors, a factory in Northern Ireland paid for by the British government. The DMC-12 arrived late, underpowered and expensive to fix. DeLorean needed to sell about 25,000 cars a year and sold half that. With cash gone in 1982, John DeLorean was arrested in an FBI drug sting, acquitted later, but the company was already dead. The car became a star in Back to the Future in 1985, three years too late to save its maker.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Building the factory and the legend before proving that enough people would buy the car at the price it cost to make.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A famous founder and a beautiful product cannot outrun unit economics. Prove people will pay, at volume, before you build the cathedral.
