Why Did Lido Learning Fail?

The class ends mid term

2022YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Operations shut in 2022THE BURN

What happened

Lido Learning sold small group online classes to Indian parents who wanted school support with more attention. It grew during the edtech boom and promised quality over noisy large batches. High teacher and acquisition costs met falling demand as schools reopened. In 2022 classes stopped abruptly and parents sought refunds, showing how fragile trust becomes when prepaid education meets weak cash.

The fatal mistake

Selling long promises while funding operations month to month.

The lesson

Prepaid revenue is a liability to teach, not a trophy to spend.

The antidote book

Profit FirstMike Michalowicz · Money & Finance · 6 lessons

Sales − Profit = Expenses. Take profit FIRST, hide it, and let the business survive on what remains - because it will.
