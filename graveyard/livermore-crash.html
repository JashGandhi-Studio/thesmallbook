Why Did Jesse Livermore Fail?

🐻 The Man Who Won 1929 and Lost Everything Anyway · 1934 · 💸 $100M (≈ $1.5B today)

📜 What Happened

Livermore shorted the 1929 crash and made $100 million in days — perhaps history's greatest single trade — while the world burned. By 1934 he was bankrupt: bigger bets, broken rules (his OWN published rules), and the emotional spiral of chasing past glory. The greatest trader alive was destroyed by the only opponent he couldn't read: himself.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

He broke every rule he'd written once the wins made him feel exempt from them. Genius at reading markets, bankrupt at reading himself.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Getting money and keeping money are opposite skills. Your rules exist precisely for the moments you feel too good to need them.
