Why Did John Law Fail?

🎴 The Gambler Who Broke France · 1720 · 💸 France's entire financial system

📜 What Happened

A Scottish gambler-economist convinced France to let him print paper money and merge it with his Mississippi Company — shares in the 'boundless riches' of Louisiana swamps. Shares rose 20x; the word 'millionaire' was coined for the winners. Law printed money to prop up shares, shares to prop up money — a doom loop wearing genius's clothes. The 1720 collapse ruined a generation, made 'banque' a dirty word in France for a century, and helped set the fuse for revolution.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Backing the currency with the stock and the stock with the currency — a perpetual motion machine of confidence, which is to say, nothing.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When the system's collateral is belief in the system, one doubt is a bank run. Complexity that no one can explain isn't sophistication — it's the hiding place of the flaw.
