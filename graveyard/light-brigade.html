Why Did The Charge of the Light Brigade Fail?

🐴 Six Words That Killed 110 Men · 1854 · 💸 278 casualties in 20 minutes

📜 What Happened

At Balaclava, Lord Raglan ordered cavalry to stop Russians removing captured guns from a hillside — but his vague written order ('advance rapidly to the front... try to prevent the enemy carrying away the guns') never specified WHICH guns. The messenger, when asked, gestured angrily at the wrong valley — one lined on three sides with Russian artillery. Six hundred horsemen charged into the guns; a third fell in twenty minutes. A French marshal watching: 'It is magnificent, but it is not war. It is madness.'

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

An ambiguous order, an assumption at every relay, and a chain of command where no one felt able to ask 'which guns, exactly?'

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Vague instructions + hierarchy + fear of clarifying questions = catastrophe, in every century. The 30 seconds it takes to confirm understanding is the cheapest insurance ever invented.
