Why Did SmileDirectClub Fail?

Mail-Order Teeth Straightening Sued Into Bankruptcy

2023YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $8.9B IPO valuation; liquidated with zero customer supportTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

SmileDirectClub promised to democratize orthodontic care by sending clear plastic aligners directly to customers through the mail for $1,900, bypassing expensive orthodontist visits with at-home putty impression kits and 3D scans. Going public in 2019 at an $8.9 billion valuation, the company spent hundreds of millions on aggressive television advertising. But dental boards sued over patient safety, customers reported permanent nerve damage and broken bites, and non-disclosure agreements gagged complaining patients. Drowning under $900 million in debt and regulatory injunctions, SmileDirectClub abruptly filed Chapter 11 in late 2023, shutting down immediately and stranding thousands of mid-treatment customers.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Prioritizing aggressive direct-to-consumer customer acquisition over clinical medical oversight and patient safety.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Healthcare is not a traditional e-commerce DTC funnel. Bypassing clinical supervision to goose gross margins creates toxic regulatory liability that crushes marketing moats.
