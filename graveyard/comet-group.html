Why Did Comet Group Fail?

Plugged out by thin margins

2012YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Administration in 2012THE BURN

What happened

Comet sold televisions, appliances and computers through big British sheds. Extended warranties and scale were meant to protect a business where the products themselves kept getting cheaper. Consumer electronics became a showroom for online baskets. Comet changed owners for a token sum, still could not fix cash and credit insurance, and collapsed into administration in 2012.

The fatal mistake

Relying on add ons to rescue a core offer customers could price in seconds.

The lesson

Transparent prices punish any model that needs confusion to make margin.

The antidote book

Profit FirstMike Michalowicz · Money & Finance · 6 lessons

Sales − Profit = Expenses. Take profit FIRST, hide it, and let the business survive on what remains - because it will.
