Why Did Republic First Bank Fail?

The smaller echo of 2023

2024YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
Seized in 2024THE BURN

What happened

Republic First Bank in Philadelphia shared a confusingly similar name with the much larger First Republic but lived a quieter regional life. It struggled with costs, governance fights and the post 2023 deposit climate. Regulators closed Republic First in April 2024 and sold most deposits and assets to Fulton Bank. It became the first US bank failure of 2024 and a reminder that confidence problems did not end with the famous names.

The fatal mistake

Letting governance noise meet a nervous deposit era.

The lesson

Small banks do not need a national panic to fail. They need doubt plus delay.
