Why Did Café Coffee Day Fail?

Lots Can Happen Over Coffee, Including Debt Trap

2019YEAR
☠️ MONEYCAUSE
💸 7,000 crore INR hidden debt; tragic founder deathTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

V. G. Siddhartha built Café Coffee Day from a single café in Bengaluru in 1996 into an Indian cultural icon with over 1,700 locations, beating Starbucks across the country. CCD was where a generation of young Indians had first dates, business meetings, and Wi-Fi access. But Siddhartha harbored an insatiable appetite for debt, leveraging his café empire to fund real estate, technology parks, and heavy logistics ventures. In July 2019, Siddhartha tragically took his own life, leaving a letter revealing hidden transactions and intense pressure from private equity lenders and tax authorities. The company was forced to sell off assets and close hundreds of cafés.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Using short-term leverage and structured debt to finance long-gestation unrelated speculative businesses.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never leverage a beloved consumer brand to fund unrelated capital-intensive speculative ventures. When debt payments compound, financial strain consumes both the business and personal sanity.
