Why Did Dunzo Fail?

🛵 Everyone's Favorite App, Nobody's Profitable Delivery · 2024 · 💸 $450M+ raised — app went dark

📜 What Happened

Dunzo was Bangalore's beloved verb — 'just Dunzo it.' Google invested; Reliance put in $200M at a $775M valuation. Then quick-commerce arrived and Dunzo chose to fight Zomato-Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart simultaneously — dark stores, ads everywhere, deep discounts — a capital war against opponents with 10x the ammunition. When the next round didn't come: unpaid salaries for months, vendor lawsuits, cofounders exiting, and by 2024-25 the app effectively went dark. Being loved, it turned out, was not a moat.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Entering a burn war against the deepest pockets in the country with the shallowest treasury in the fight.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never fight a war of attrition against someone whose losses are your entire net worth and their rounding error. Pick fights where YOUR constraint is the advantage — or don't pick that fight.
