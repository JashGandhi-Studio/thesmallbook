Why Did V.G. Siddhartha Fail?

☕ The Coffee King Who Carried It Alone · 2019 · 💸 A founder India mourned

📜 What Happened

The founder of Café Coffee Day built 1,700 cafés and gave a generation of Indians their third place — 'a lot can happen over coffee.' Behind the empire: mounting debt (₹7,000+ crore across entities), pledged shares, tax pressures, and a founder who confided in almost no one. His final letter apologized to his team and spoke of pressure he could no longer bear. India lost him in July 2019; the business, restructured, survived — the debts were solvable. He never let anyone help him solve them.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Not the debt — businesses survive debt. It was carrying the full weight in silence, ashamed to ask for the help that existed.
