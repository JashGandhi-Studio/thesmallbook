Why Did Coolest Cooler Fail?

🧊 Kickstarter's Biggest Hit Became Its Biggest Lesson · 2019 · 💸 $13M raised; 20,000 backers never got coolers

📜 What Happened

The Coolest Cooler — blender, speakers, USB charger in one cooler — raised $13M from 62,000 backers, then Kickstarter's record. But the $185 pledge price was BELOW the actual build cost, the blender motor supplier fell through, tariffs hit, and the company started selling coolers on Amazon at $400 to fund backer units — enraging the very people who'd funded it. It died in 2019 with 20,000 backers empty-handed.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Priced the reward before pricing the manufacturing — every record-breaking sale deepened the loss, and the crowd that funded them got served last.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A pre-order below cost is a debt, not a triumph. Know your true unit cost before you celebrate demand — viral sales of a money-losing SKU is just viral bankruptcy.
