Why Did The Limited Fail?

The store becomes a website, then a memory

2017YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
All stores closed in 2017THE BURN

What happened

The Limited helped invent modern American mall fashion and launched or nurtured brands that defined shopping for a generation. Its name once meant taste at scale. The parent empire moved on, malls weakened, and the chain lost a clear reason to exist beside faster rivals. In 2017 it closed all remaining stores and briefly pointed shoppers online before the brand faded.

The fatal mistake

Keeping a famous name after the operating magic had left the building.

The lesson

Names keep value only when the experience keeps proving them.
