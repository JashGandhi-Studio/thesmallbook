Why Did Voyager Digital Fail?

The Crypto Lender That Gave a Hedge Fund Its Customers' Coins

2022YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
A $1+ billion hole; 1.7 million customers locked out; unsecured loans to Three Arrows Capital never repaidTHE BURN

What happened

Voyager paid eye-watering yields on deposits and lent the other side of the trade to hedge funds. Its largest borrower was Three Arrows Capital: $655 million in bitcoin and stablecoins, unsecured, at a moment when 3AC was already drowning. When 3AC defaulted in June 2022, Voyager issued a notice of default nobody could collect on and entered Chapter 11 within days - while its CEO's classic-car collection made the filings. Customers queued for years to recover cents.

The fatal mistake

Rehypothecating retail deposits into unsecured, concentrated counterparty risk - the yield paid to users was literally the credit risk nobody priced.

The lesson

Yield is someone else's borrowing. Ask what backs the interest and who the counterparty is; in crypto lending of that era the honest answer was 'the next hurricane'.
