Why Did The Concorde Fail?

✈️ The Plane So Beautiful Nobody Could Stop Paying · 1976–2003 · 💸 £1.3B+ (never recovered)

📜 What Happened

Britain and France knew by the late 1960s that supersonic passenger flight couldn't be profitable — fuel costs, tiny cabins, sonic-boom bans over land. They finished it anyway: too much invested, too much national pride. It flew 27 years, a magnificent machine losing money on champagne-service for 100 passengers, and gave economics its most famous term: the 'Concorde fallacy' — the textbook name for throwing good money after bad.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Both governments knew the answer and kept paying to avoid admitting it — prestige became the budget line that couldn't be questioned.
