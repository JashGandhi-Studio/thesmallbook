Why Did Pontiac Fail?

The performance badge is parked

2010YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Brand retired in 2010THE BURN

What happened

Pontiac sold excitement inside General Motors: GTO, Firebird and a promise that driving should feel louder than commuting. For decades the badge gave GM a clear personality. Badge engineering diluted the promise until many Pontiacs felt like other GM cars with different trim. During GM bankruptcy restructuring, Pontiac was chosen for execution and the brand ended in 2010.

The fatal mistake

Keeping the badge while draining the difference.

The lesson

A sub brand dies when customers can no longer say what it stands for in one sentence.
