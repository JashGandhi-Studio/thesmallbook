Why Did Johnny Manziel Fail?

🏈 The First-Round Talent Who Didn't Study the Playbook · 2016 · 💸 An NFL career in 2 years

📜 What Happened

Johnny 'Football' Manziel won the Heisman as a freshman — improvisational genius, drafted in the first round by Cleveland in 2014. Then the work stopped mattering to him: he later admitted he barely studied the playbook, partied through preparation weeks (once reportedly wearing a disguise in Vegas the weekend before a game), and relied on the talent that had always been enough. In college, it was. Against professionals who study 60 hours a week, it wasn't even close. Out of the NFL by 2016, at age 23.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Believing talent was a permanent balance instead of a head start that others erase with work.
