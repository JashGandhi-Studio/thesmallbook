Why Did Johnny Depp Fail?

$30,000 a Month on Wine

2017YEAR
☠️ FAMECAUSE
💸 $650M earned, sued his managersTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Depp earned $650 million and reached the edge of ruin: 14 residences, a 156-foot yacht, 45 luxury cars, $200,000/month in private jets, a $3M cannon to fire Hunter S. Thompson's ashes, and - per his own managers' court filings - $30,000 a month on wine. When he sued his management for mishandling money, their defense was an itemized list of his spending. Both sides were, in a sense, right.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A money machine with no owner: he never read statements, signed what was handed to him, and treated income as infinite while delegating everything including attention.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

You can delegate execution, never awareness. The monthly statement-reading habit costs one hour and would have saved nine figures - nobody guards your money like you.
