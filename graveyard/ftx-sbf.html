Why Did Sam Bankman-Fried Fail?

🪙 The $32B Empire on a Spreadsheet Nobody Kept · 2022 · 💸 $32B → zero in 10 days

📜 What Happened

FTX was crypto's most trusted exchange — Super Bowl ads, stadium names, congressional testimony. Behind it: customer deposits secretly funneled to sister fund Alameda, accounting done partly on QuickBooks, and a company that didn't know how many employees it had. One leaked balance sheet triggered a bank run; $32 billion evaporated in ten days. SBF got 25 years.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

No board, no CFO, no controls — genius-branding substituted for basic accounting while customer money paid for the empire.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Competence in one thing (trading) is not competence in everything. When a company's controls are 'trust me,' the correct amount to deposit is zero.
