📜 What Happened

Vikram Kothari's Rotomac pens were in every Indian student's pencil box in the 1990s and 2000s, popularized by catchy advertising and celebrity endorsements. But the writing instrument business was just a front for massive commodity trade financing fraud. Kothari borrowed thousands of crores from a consortium of public sector banks against fake letters of credit, ostensibly to import and export wheat, gems, and fertilizer. In reality, the goods were fictitious and the funds were routed through shell companies in Dubai and Hong Kong for interest arbitrage and personal real estate. In February 2018, the CBI and Enforcement Directorate arrested Kothari for a 3,700 crore INR default.
