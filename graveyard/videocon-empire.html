Why Did Videocon Fail?

📺 From India's Electronics King to ₹80,000 Crore in Debt · 2018 · 💸 ₹80,000+ crore debt; the dhoti-to-empire story ends in insolvency

📜 What Happened

Venugopal Dhoot built Videocon from a small TV maker into India's most iconic consumer-electronics brand — the name in every middle-class living room. Then ambition outran the business: he borrowed globally (including a controversial $800M loan from a subsidiary of another Dhoot firm) to expand into oil fields, telecom, and everything. When commodity prices fell and repayments came due, the empire — levered to the eyeballs — collapsed into India's insolvency courts in 2018. The brand that defined Indian electronics died under the debt that was supposed to make it bigger.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Using a profitable core business as collateral for unrelated, giant bets — the electronics cash flow was sacrificed to feed oil and telecom dreams.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Your core business is not an ATM for every dream. Bets should be sized so the core survives the worst case — the empire that bets its foundation loses both the bet and the foundation.
