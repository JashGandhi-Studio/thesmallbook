Why Did Stein Mart Fail?

Off price meets a closed world

2020YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Stein Mart promised department store brands at discount prices to loyal middle market shoppers. It survived cycles by serving customers who liked the hunt without luxury theatre. Online off price rivals grew, stores aged, and the pandemic removed the in person hunt at the worst moment. Stein Mart filed for bankruptcy in 2020 and liquidated its stores.

The fatal mistake

Serving loyal shoppers while the channel moved under them.

The lesson

Loyalty delays change. It rarely cancels it.
