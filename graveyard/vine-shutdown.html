Why Did Vine Fail?

Killed the App That Invented Short Video

2017YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 The TikTok-sized opportunityTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Vine's 6-second looping videos created modern short-form culture and its first stars. But Twitter (which bought it pre-launch for $30M) never built creator monetization - top Viners asked for $1.2M collectively to stay; Twitter declined, and the stars left for YouTube and Instagram, taking audiences with them. Shut down in 2017 as 'unprofitable.' A year later, TikTok scaled the exact format with creator funds into a $300B+ phenomenon.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treated creators as free content instead of the product itself - the platform monetized nothing and paid nobody while sitting on the format of the decade.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

In creator economies, the talent IS the moat: pay them or lose the platform. Owning the format means nothing if everyone who makes it valuable can leave.
