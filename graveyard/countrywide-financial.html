Why Did Countrywide Financial Fail?

The mortgage machine is sold in distress

2008YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
Sold to Bank of America in 2008THE BURN

What happened

Countrywide became America's largest mortgage lender by making home loans feel easy in a rising market. Its sales engine and political influence made it central to the housing boom. The loans soured, investigations grew and funding markets panicked. Bank of America bought Countrywide in 2008 in what looked like rescue and became years of legal and mortgage costs.

The fatal mistake

Optimising for origination volume while someone else was meant to hold the risk.

The lesson

If you are paid for making the loan and not for whether it survives, volume will eventually invent danger.
