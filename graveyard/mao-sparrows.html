Why Did The Four Pests Campaign Fail?

🐦 They Killed the Sparrows; the Locusts Sent Thanks · 1960 · 💸 A man-made contribution to history's deadliest famine

📜 What Happened

In 1958 China declared war on four pests — rats, flies, mosquitoes, and sparrows, accused of eating grain. Hundreds of millions of people banged pots so sparrows couldn't land until they fell dead from exhaustion; nests were destroyed nationwide. Sparrows, it turned out, mostly ate INSECTS. With their predator erased, locust populations exploded and devoured the harvests, deepening (alongside disastrous farm policies) the Great Famine. In 1960 sparrows were quietly removed from the list — replaced by bed bugs.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Attacking a complex system at one visible node, at national scale, with zero pilot test — and no channel for biologists to say 'wait.'

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Second-order effects don't care about your intentions. Before removing something annoying, ask what it was quietly holding back — and never scale an irreversible fix you haven't tested small.
