Why Did Napoleon's Egypt Campaign Fail?

🐫 Conquered the Pyramids, Lost the Fleet, Abandoned the Army · 1799 · 💸 An army, a fleet, and the plan

📜 What Happened

Napoleon invaded Egypt to cut Britain's route to India — dazzling battles, scholars discovering the Rosetta Stone, glory dispatches home. Then Admiral Nelson found the French fleet anchored at Aboukir Bay and annihilated it, stranding 35,000 soldiers with no way home. Plague, desert sieges, and a failed march into Syria followed. Napoleon's response: he slipped away on a fast ship, leaving his army behind (it surrendered years later), and spun the catastrophe into a hero's return that carried him to power.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A brilliant land plan with a single point of failure — the fleet — left exposed; when it sank, the whole campaign had no plan B.

Audit your dependencies: the weakest link you don't control decides your fate. And notice — the story told about a failure can matter more than the failure. Control the narrative or it controls you.
