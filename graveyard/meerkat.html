Why Did Meerkat Fail?

The SXSW Darling That Twitter Switched Off Mid-Flight

2015YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
The hottest app of SXSW 2015, dead as a brand within a yearTHE BURN

What happened

Meerkat let anyone stream live video from their phone, and it detonated at SXSW 2015 on the strength of one brilliant loop: broadcast to your Twitter followers in one tap. Twitter - which had just bought Periscope - cut off Meerkat's social graph access within weeks. The startup pivoted, renamed, and its team eventually built Houseparty instead. The lesson arrived before the corpse was cold: your distribution is someone else's API.

The fatal mistake

Building the product on a rival's graph with no ownership of the audience - the entire growth engine was a firehose the landlord could (and did) close.

The lesson

Rented distribution is a feature request away from extinction. If your growth depends on one platform's tap, own the relationship or accept you are their R&D.
