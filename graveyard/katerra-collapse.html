Why Did Katerra Fail?

🏗️ $3B to 'Disrupt Construction' — Bankrupt Anyway · 2021 · 💸 $3B — SoftBank's construction bonfire

📜 What Happened

Katerra raised almost $3B (mostly SoftBank) to make buildings the way Toyota makes cars: factories, standard parts, vertical integration. It bought dozens of construction firms, built massive factories, and promised revolution. But construction isn't cars: every site, code, climate, and client is different. Katerra took on huge fixed costs before proving its process actually saved money on real projects, absorbed acquisitions faster than it could integrate them, and bled cash on every 'revolutionary' building. Bankrupt in 2021.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Scaling the factory before proving the unit economics of a single building — buying the revolution retail before checking if it worked wholesale.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Prove the math on ONE unit before you industrialize a million. Capital lets you skip the proving stage — which just means you discover the flaw after buying the factories instead of before.
