Why Did Hailo Fail?

The taxi app takes the wrong road

2016YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
North America exit and mergerTHE BURN

What happened

Hailo made licensed taxis bookable by app when Uber still felt insurgent. London black cab drivers gave it supply and trust in its home city. Expansion into North America burned cash against ridehail economics, and the company retreated. Hailo merged into Daimler's MyTaxi in 2016, ending the independent global taxi app dream.

The fatal mistake

Fighting a subsidised insurgent city by city with regulated supply.

The lesson

A trusted niche can survive. A global war chest contest needs a global war chest.
