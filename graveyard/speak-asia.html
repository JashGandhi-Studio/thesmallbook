Why Did Speak Asia Fail?

📋 24 Lakh Indians Paid to Fill Surveys That Nobody Wanted · 2011 · 💸 ₹2,200 crore from 24 lakh 'panelists'

📜 What Happened

Speak Asia ran full-page newspaper ads and prime-time TV spots: pay ₹11,000, fill two online surveys a week, earn ₹52,000 a year. 'Market research,' they called it. 24 lakh Indians joined — many borrowing to pay entry fees, whole families signing up. The truth: there were almost no real corporate clients wanting surveys; payouts came from new members' fees, the oldest trick wearing a laptop. When payments froze in 2011, ₹2,200 crore was gone and the promoters were untraceable abroad.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

For victims: not asking the only question that kills every scheme — WHO is paying this money, and what are they getting for it?

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If the product is vague but the recruitment is aggressive, the recruits ARE the product. Any income that depends on people joining after you is a queue for a cliff.
