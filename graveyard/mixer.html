Why Did Mixer Fail?

Big streamers, small crowd

2020YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Closed in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Microsoft bought Beam, renamed it Mixer, and tried to buy attention in game streaming with exclusive deals for stars such as Ninja and Shroud. The technology was fast and interactive. Viewers did not follow in enough numbers. Twitch and YouTube already held the communities, clips and habits, so expensive exclusives rented fame without creating a crowd. Microsoft shut Mixer in 2020 and sent partners toward Facebook Gaming.

The fatal mistake

Trying to purchase a network effect one celebrity at a time.

The lesson

An audience is not a pile of followers. It is the place people already gather.
