Why Did BitConnect Fail?

The $2.4 Billion Crypto Ponzi That Screamed 'Ponzi'

2018YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 $2.4B from retail investors worldwideTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

BitConnect promised investors up to 40% monthly returns through a 'trading bot' — a return that would turn ₹100 into a crore in two years. The pitch was so absurd that the crypto community mocked it endlessly ('BitConnect!') while millions still poured money in, especially in India where it ran massive seminars. The inevitable happened: in 2018 the price crashed 92% in a day, the exchange shut down, and the founders vanished. The US SEC later charged it as a Ponzi scheme. The joke was on everyone who ignored the joke.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Chasing returns that math cannot support — 40% monthly returns are impossible by definition, but greed converted the impossible into 'opportunity'.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If a return is mathematically impossible, it's a fraud — no exceptions. When you hear 'guaranteed high returns', the guarantee is that someone is taking your money. Math is the best fraud detector.
