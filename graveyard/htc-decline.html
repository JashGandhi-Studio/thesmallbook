Why Did HTC Fail?

One in Six Smartphones on Earth, Then Barely One

2011YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
From ~11% global share to under 1%; the phone team sold to Google for $1.1 billionTHE BURN

What happened

HTC made the first Android phone and by 2011 shipped one in six smartphones worldwide, beating Apple to market features and carrying the Android flag. Then Samsung out-spent it in marketing and components, Chinese brands undercut it in price, and HTC answered with an annual parade of near-identical models. By 2017 it sold its design team to Google and retreated to VR - a company that once defined Android, now a case study in being squeezed from above and below.

The fatal mistake

Competing against Samsung's marketing budget and Xiaomi's costs with neither - a decade of hardware iterations while the brand, the channels and the supply chain lost their seats.

The lesson

In commodity hardware, position must be bought (brand) or earned (cost). If you hold neither, scale is a treadmill that funds your own replacement.
