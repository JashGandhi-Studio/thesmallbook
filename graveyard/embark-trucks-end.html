Why Did Embark Trucks Fail?

Autonomy Arrived Later Than the Cash

2023YEAR
TECHCAUSE
An independent public company sold after mass layoffsTHE BURN

What happened

Embark Trucks was one of the best known self driving truck companies and reached the public markets through a SPAC merger. Commercial driverless freight took longer to certify and scale than investors had funded, while public market appetite for pre revenue autonomy companies collapsed. Embark cut most of its workforce in 2023 and agreed to be acquired by Applied Intuition, ending its run as an independent company.

The fatal mistake

Financing a long research timeline as if a market window and a stock listing could shorten the engineering and safety timeline.

The lesson

When the technology date is uncertain, the balance sheet must assume delay. A company can be directionally right and still run out of time.
