Why Did Isaac Newton Fail?

🍎 The Genius Who Bought the Top · 1720 · 💸 £20,000 (his life savings)

📜 What Happened

The greatest scientific mind in history invested in the South Sea Company, wisely sold early with a 100% profit — then watched friends keep getting richer as the bubble inflated. Consumed by FOMO, he bought back in near the absolute peak with far more money. The bubble collapsed within weeks and Newton lost around £20,000 — a fortune, roughly his entire life savings.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

He beat the market, then let envy drag him back in at the top. FOMO overrode the finest analytical brain on Earth.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Intelligence doesn't protect you from greed — Newton himself said it: 'I can calculate the motion of heavenly bodies, but not the madness of people.' If the smartest man alive couldn't time a bubble, you can't either.
