Why Did Village Voice Fail?

The alt weekly falls silent

2018YEAR
HISTORYCAUSE
Publication suspended in 2018THE BURN

What happened

The Village Voice invented much of the American alternative weekly: fierce politics, arts coverage, classifieds and a city arguing with itself in print. For decades it was both a newspaper and a New York institution. The internet took classifieds, then ads, then the weekly habit. The paper ended print in 2017 and suspended publication in 2018 after ownership changes and staff cuts hollowed the thing readers loved.

The fatal mistake

Assuming cultural importance would somehow pay the printer.

The lesson

Influence is not revenue unless someone builds the bridge between the two.
