Why Did Jabong Fail?

Folded into the sister closet

2020YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Closed and folded into Myntra in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Jabong helped teach India to buy fashion online, with brands, sales events and a young urban customer. Flipkart acquired it after buying Myntra, giving one parent two fashion doors. Running both doors cost more than the extra reach earned. Flipkart shut Jabong in 2020 and pushed users to Myntra, accepting focus over a famous second label.

The fatal mistake

Owning two answers to the same customer and paying twice for the question.

The lesson

Portfolio logic beats sibling rivalry when acquisition costs rise.
