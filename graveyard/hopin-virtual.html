Why Did Hopin Fail?

The $7.8B Lockdown Wonder Sold for $15M

2023YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE

📜 What Happened

Johnny Boufarhat built Hopin from his bedroom into the fastest-growing software startup in European history when pandemic lockdowns forced every conference on earth to cancel in 2020. Hopin raised $1 billion from Andreessen Horowitz and Tiger Global, surging to a $7.8 billion valuation in mid-2021. But when in-person conferences returned, demand for virtual stage software cratered. Heavy enterprise churn was accompanied by massive acquisitions that failed to integrate. In August 2023, Hopin sold its core events and webinars business to RingCentral for an initial $15 million.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Mistaking a temporary global lockdown crisis for permanent secular software demand.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Distinguish between structural secular growth and black-swan windfalls. Pricing a business at a 100x multiple on peak pandemic revenue guarantees catastrophe when normal life resumes.
