Why Did Germania Fail?

The green tails disappear

2019YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Insolvent in 2019THE BURN

What happened

Germania flew German holidaymakers to sun and family routes for decades, independent beside giants and tour brands. Its green tails were familiar at regional airports. Fuel prices, aircraft delays, a strong euro squeeze and failed financing talks hit together. Germania filed for insolvency in February 2019 and grounded flights overnight.

The fatal mistake

Carrying independent scale in a market of giants and tour operator power.

The lesson

Mid sized airlines need either protection, niche or cost advantage. Germania's cushion ran out.
