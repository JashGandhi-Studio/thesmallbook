Why Did Evander Holyfield's Ear Aside — Tyson's Entourage Fail?

$400M Earned; Bankrupt With 200 People on Payroll

2003YEAR
☠️ FAMECAUSE
💸 $400M career → $23M in debtTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Mike Tyson earned over $400M — and filed for bankruptcy $23M in debt. The autopsy: an entourage of dozens living on his dime, two Bengal tigers ($70k each plus a trainer), mansions he forgot he owned, $2M in one jewelry visit, and promoters (he sued Don King for $100M) treating his fortune as their float. Nobody in the room was paid to say no; everyone was paid to say yes. Tyson later rebuilt modestly — and said losing it all was the beginning of actually owning his life.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Outsourcing financial control to people whose income depended on the spending continuing.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If everyone around you profits when you say yes, you don't have advisors — you have overhead. Wealth needs at least one person in the room whose only job is the word 'no.'
