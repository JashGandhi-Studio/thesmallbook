Why Did Juicero Fail?

🥤 The $400 Wi-Fi Juicer Beaten by Human Hands · 2017 · 💸 $120M in funding

📜 What Happened

Silicon Valley's finest invested $120M in a $699 (later $399) Wi-Fi-connected machine that squeezed proprietary juice packets. Then Bloomberg reporters discovered you could squeeze the packets BY HAND — faster than the machine. The demonstration video ended the company within months. The juice packets, it turned out, were the product; the machine was $400 of ceremony.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Engineering in search of a problem — nobody making the machine asked whether hands existed.
