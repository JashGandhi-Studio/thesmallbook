Why Did Billy McFarland Fail?

🏝️ Fyre: Luxury Festival, FEMA Tents · 2017 · 💸 $26M investor money, 6 years prison

📜 What Happened

Fyre Festival sold $12,000 luxury packages via supermodel Instagram posts — private island, villas, gourmet chefs. Reality on arrival: disaster-relief tents, cheese sandwiches in foam boxes, no water, no acts, no way home. McFarland had known for months it couldn't be delivered and kept selling — then launched a NEW ticket scam while awaiting sentencing for the first one.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Marketing genius pointed at a product that didn't exist — and doubling down on the lie at every decision point where honesty could have limited the damage.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Hype is a loan against delivery, repaid with interest or with handcuffs. The influencer post is not the product. (Also: if the cheese sandwich photo teaches anything — the truth always posts itself eventually.)
