Why Did GainBitcoin Fail?

India's 'Crypto Guru' and the ₹1 Lakh Crore Mirage

2018YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 Est. up to ₹1 lakh crore in BitcoinTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Amit Bhardwaj toured India as a crypto visionary — books, seminars, Bollywood connections — selling 'cloud mining contracts': give me your Bitcoin, get 10% MONTHLY returns for 18 months. At Bitcoin's 2017 peak mania, tens of thousands handed over coins. There was little real mining; payouts were recycled deposits, later paid in his own worthless token when Bitcoin ran short. Investigators estimated the Bitcoin collected could be worth up to ₹1 lakh crore at later prices. Bhardwaj died in 2022 with cases still crawling; most victims never saw a satoshi.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

For victims: believing 10% GUARANTEED monthly returns could coexist with reality — 120%+ a year, promised in an asset whose whole point is volatility.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Returns above ~15% a YEAR come with risk; returns 'guaranteed' above that come with handcuffs — usually for someone else, paid for by you. The guru selling certainty in a casino owns the casino.
