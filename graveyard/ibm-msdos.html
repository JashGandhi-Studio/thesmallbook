Why Did IBM's PC Deal Fail?

Handed Microsoft the Throne for Pocket Change

1981YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 The most valuable industry in historyTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Racing to launch the IBM PC, IBM licensed an operating system from a tiny company called Microsoft — and let Bill Gates keep the right to sell that same DOS to other manufacturers. IBM believed the money was in hardware; software was an accessory. Clone makers copied the hardware, licensed the same DOS, and undercut IBM. The 'IBM-compatible' market exploded — with Microsoft collecting a toll on every machine. IBM eventually sold off its own PC business; Microsoft became one of the most valuable companies on Earth.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Keeping the commodity (hardware) and giving away the monopoly (the software standard everyone would depend on).

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

In every deal, ask: which side of this becomes the standard everyone must pay? Own the chokepoint, not the commodity. The accessory you're ignoring may be the whole kingdom.
