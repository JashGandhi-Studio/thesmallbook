Why Did Quibi Fail?

$1.75 Billion, Six Months, Gone

2020YEAR
☠️ EGOCAUSE
💸 $1.75B in 6 monthsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Hollywood legend Jeffrey Katzenberg and tech royalty Meg Whitman raised $1.75 billion for 'quick bites' — premium 10-minute shows for phones — before a single user validated the idea. Launched April 2020 (lockdowns: nobody commuting), no screenshots allowed (killing all social sharing), couldn't cast to TVs, competing against free TikTok. Six months later: shut down. Possibly the fastest ever destruction of that much startup capital.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Two icons so credentialed nobody made them test the premise. $1.75B raised on résumés; zero spent on finding out if anyone wanted it.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The market doesn't care who you are. A weekend of Mom-Test conversations would have saved $1.75 billion — validation is cheaper than legend-status, and legends skip it at their own expense.
