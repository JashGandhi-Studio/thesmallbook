Why Did Parmalat Fail?

Europe's Enron Kept a €3.95 Billion Bank Account That Never Existed

2003YEAR
FRAUDCAUSE
A €14 billion hole; Europe's largest bankruptcy of its era; founder Calisto Tanzi convictedTHE BURN

What happened

Parmalat, the global dairy giant from Parma, spent two decades reporting cash it did not have. The masterpiece was a purported €3.95 billion Bank of America account - authenticated by a forged fax - that auditors finally called the bank about directly in December 2003. There was no account. The group collapsed within weeks, investigators found a forged letterhead factory, and the 'milk empire' turned out to be a paper company with cows printed on it.

The fatal mistake

Auditing by fax - confirmation letters were typed on forged letterhead and filed, while tens of billions in claimed cash was never once verified at source.

The lesson

Confirm balances directly with the counterparty, always. The single most basic audit procedure remains the one that catches the biggest frauds.
