Why Did New Age Meats Fail?

The cultivated bite never reaches menu scale

2023YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Closed in 2023THE BURN

What happened

New Age Meats pursued cultivated pork and sausages that could sell taste without slaughter. It raised money while alternative protein still enjoyed generous climate and technology optimism. Costs, regulation, funding winter and the long road to commercial plants closed in. The company shut in 2023 before proving cultivated meat could be made at food prices rather than demonstration prices.

The fatal mistake

Funding a science timeline with venture patience measured in quarters.

The lesson

Deep tech needs milestones investors can taste, literally and financially.

The antidote book

The Black SwanNassim Nicholas Taleb · Money & Finance · 6 lessons

The biggest events are unpredicted by construction. Stop forecasting; start building antifragility - and position for the swans instead.
