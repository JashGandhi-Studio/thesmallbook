Why Did Hyperloop One Fail?

$450M for a Tube That Never Carried Anyone... Except Once

2023YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $450M+; assets sold for scrapTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Born from Elon Musk's white paper, Hyperloop One raised $450M+ to shoot passenger pods through vacuum tubes at 700 mph. It built a Nevada test track, signed glossy MoUs with governments, and rode a decade of hype. Total passengers ever carried: two employees, once, at 100 mph in 2020. The physics were possible; the economics — building hundreds of miles of near-perfect vacuum tube through earthquakes, land rights, and thermal expansion — never closed. It quietly shut down in 2023, selling the tubes for scrap.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A decade spent marketing the dream to governments and investors while the core question — can this ever be cheaper than a train? — stayed unanswered.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

'Technically possible' and 'economically survivable' are different planets. If the business only works in the render, you don't have a company — you have an expensive screensaver.
