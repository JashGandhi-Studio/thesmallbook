Why Did A.C. Gilbert Fail?

🧲 The Toy Giant That Chased Cool and Lost Its Soul · 1967 · 💸 58-year institution, gone in 5 years

📜 What Happened

A.C. Gilbert made Erector Sets and chemistry kits — the century's iconic educational toys, a company with a 58-year reputation for quality. When 1960s kids turned to TV-advertised toys, new management panicked: slashed quality to cut prices, chased fads (spy kits, slot cars) far from its identity, flooded discount channels that cheapened the brand, and churned through strategies yearly. Each pivot alienated loyalists without winning the fad market. Dead by 1967 — a Harvard case study in panic-driven identity loss.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Answered disruption by abandoning everything customers actually valued about them — quality and identity — to imitate competitors badly.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When the market shifts, double down on why customers loved you while adapting the wrapper — panic-pivoting into someone else's identity gets you neither market.
