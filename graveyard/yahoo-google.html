📜 What Happened

The full menu of missed forever-fortunes: 1998 — Page and Brin offer Google's search algorithm for $1M; Yahoo passes. 2002 — Yahoo could buy Google for ~$3B; balks at $5B. 2006 — agrees to buy Facebook for $1B, then lowers the offer to $850M; Zuckerberg walks. 2008 — Microsoft offers $44.6B for Yahoo itself; Yahoo refuses. 2017 — sold to Verizon for $4.8B.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Not one bad decision — a decades-long pattern of valuing what Yahoo was over what the future obviously was.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

History gives most companies one great door; it gave Yahoo four, and it priced each one like a used car. When the future knocks holding a discount, the expensive thing is the haggling.
