Why Did Oldsmobile Fail?

Your father's brand problem

2004YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Phased out in 2004THE BURN

What happened

Oldsmobile was once a technology flagship inside GM and one of America's oldest car names. It sold millions by standing for attainable engineering and comfort. Its buyers aged and younger drivers chose imports or other GM badges. The famous attempt to say it was not your father's Oldsmobile admitted the problem without solving it, and GM phased the brand out in 2004.

The fatal mistake

Marketing around an ageing customer base instead of earning a new one.

The lesson

A loyal base can hide a missing future for years. Then both disappear together.
