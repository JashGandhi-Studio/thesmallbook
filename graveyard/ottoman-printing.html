Why Did The Ottoman Printing Ban Fail?

📜 The Empire That Said No to the Printing Press for 250 Years · 1727 · 💸 Centuries of compounding knowledge

📜 What Happened

Within decades of Gutenberg, presses covered Europe, multiplying books, literacy, science, and dissent. The Ottoman Empire — then the superpower — restricted printing in Arabic script for nearly 250 years, protecting the prestige (and income) of its calligrapher guilds and religious authorities' control over texts. Europe compounded knowledge at print speed; the empire copied by hand. By the time printing spread freely, the scientific and industrial gap had become the story of the next two centuries.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Letting the people who profited from the old way vote on the new way — protecting scribes' income at the cost of civilization's compounding.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Blocking a technology doesn't stop it — it just decides you won't be the one benefiting. Incumbents who tax the future to protect the present always pay compound interest on the delay.
