Why Did TruJet Fail?

The regional promise stalls

2022YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Operations halted by 2022THE BURN

What happened

TruJet built an Indian regional network under the UDAN push for smaller city connectivity, at one point serving routes the big airlines ignored. Subsidy and scarcity made expansion look plausible. Fleet problems, funding stress and the pandemic broke continuity. Operations stopped and revival attempts faded by 2022, another regional carrier learning that small routes can carry large fixed costs.

The fatal mistake

Expanding a thin margin network before the balance sheet could survive interruptions.

The lesson

In aviation, one grounded plane can be a strategy event.
