Why Did Mark Twain Fail?

✒️ America's Funniest Bankruptcy · 1894 · 💸 $300,000 (≈ $9M today)

📜 What Happened

The world's most famous author poured today's-equivalent of $9 million into the Paige Compositor, a typesetting machine with 18,000 parts that was perpetually 'almost ready.' For 14 years he kept funding it — while turning DOWN the chance to invest in Alexander Graham Bell's telephone. The machine was obsolete on arrival; Twain went bankrupt and spent years on lecture tours repaying debts he wasn't legally required to repay.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Sunk-cost fallacy for 14 straight years — every dollar in became a reason to put another dollar in. And he passed on the telephone.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Being world-class in one field gives you zero edge in another. The money you've already lost is not a reason to lose more.
