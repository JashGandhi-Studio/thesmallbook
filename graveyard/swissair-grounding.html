Why Did Swissair Fail?

🇨🇭 The 'Flying Bank' That Bought Its Way to Bankruptcy · 2001 · 💸 National icon grounded overnight

📜 What Happened

Swissair was so financially solid it was called 'the Flying Bank' — a symbol of Swiss reliability. Consultants sold it the 'Hunter Strategy': buy stakes in half of Europe's weakest airlines (Sabena, LOT, LTU, Air Littoral...) to build an alliance by acquisition. Each stake bled cash; each rescue demanded more. In October 2001, out of cash, the fleet was grounded overnight — planes full of passengers, stranded worldwide. Switzerland treated it as a national humiliation; executives faced (unsuccessful) criminal trials.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Bought market position with cash instead of earning it with product — a portfolio of other people's problems marketed as a strategy.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

You can't acquire your way around a weak strategy. Buying losers doesn't average into a winner — it compounds into a funeral.
