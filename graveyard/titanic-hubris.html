📜 What Happened

The 'unsinkable' ship carried lifeboats for barely half its passengers — why waste deck space on the impossible? It received six ice warnings on April 14th and maintained near-full speed through a known ice field, chasing a crossing record. The nearest ship's radio operator had been told to shut up by Titanic's overworked operators earlier that night — and had gone to bed.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The belief in unsinkability CAUSED the sinking: it justified the missing lifeboats, the ignored warnings, and the speed.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The moment you believe you can't fail is the moment you've removed every safeguard that prevents failure. Confidence builds ships; arrogance removes the lifeboats.
