Why Did Eduardo Saverin Fail?

The Co-Founder Who Stayed in New York

2005YEAR
☠️ TRUSTCAUSE
💸 Diluted from 34% toward 0.03%THE BURN

📜 What Happened

Facebook's co-founder and first funder stayed in New York doing ad-sales internships while Zuckerberg moved to Palo Alto, met Sean Parker, and rebuilt the company around new investors. A restructuring issued new shares that diluted Saverin's 34% stake to near-nothing while others were protected. He'd signed the papers without fully understanding them. The lawsuit settled (he remained a billionaire), but the seat at the table — and the friendship — were gone forever.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Physical and legal absence at the formation moment — and signing dilution documents on trust instead of on counsel.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Show up where the company is being built, and never sign what you haven't had reviewed. Equity isn't what you were promised — it's what the paperwork says after the lawyers finish.
