Why Did Avon Fail?

138 Years of 'Ding-Dong, Avon Calling' Ends in Bankruptcy

2024YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $12B peak revenue → bankruptcy; $1.2B+ debtTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Avon was the pioneer of door-to-door selling — 6 million independent representatives worldwide, famous for the 'Ding-Dong, Avon Calling' jingle, and by the 1970s the world's largest cosmetics company with over $12 billion in peak revenue. But the direct-selling model — door-to-door reps, printed brochures, home parties — was the 20th century's answer to distribution. The 21st century brought Instagram, e-commerce and beauty influencers who could reach more customers in a day than a rep could in a year. Avon's US operations suffered scandal over bribing foreign officials (a $135 million SEC settlement) and a failed merger with rival Nu Skin. Sold to Natura, then split off, its US arm filed for bankruptcy in 2024 with over $1.2 billion in debt — the iconic brand reduced to a cautionary tale about distribution models that outlive their era.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Doubling down on door-to-door selling while the channel itself was dying — pouring money into a distribution model that had been structurally replaced by digital.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When the channel dies, the brand dies with it. No amount of heritage protects you from a cheaper, faster distribution model — the only question is whether you cannibalize yourself before the market does.
