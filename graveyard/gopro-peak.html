Why Did GoPro Fail?

The Action Camera That Tried to Be Everything

2016YEAR
☠️ TECHCAUSE
💸 Roughly 90% of its market value in two yearsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Nick Woodman printed money with HERO cameras: surfers, skiers and skydivers filmed themselves, and the footage sold the next camera. GoPro's 2014 IPO valued it near $10 billion. Then phones learned to swim, every new HERO looked like the last one, and GoPro spent its profit chasing a media empire and a drone, Karma, which was recalled in 2016 after drones fell from the sky. The stock lost about 90% in two years. GoPro survived, smaller and humbler, as a camera company again.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Believing the brand entitled it to new categories (media, drones) while its one great product quietly commoditised.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A beloved niche product is a business, not a launchpad by default. Defend the core first; expansion spends the trust the core earned.
