Why Did Enron Fail?

⚡ The Smartest Guys in the Room · 2001 · 💸 $74B shareholder value, 20,000 jobs

📜 What Happened

America's 'most innovative company' six years running was a hallucination of accounting: hidden debts in shell partnerships, fake revenue, and a 'rank and yank' culture that fired the bottom 15% yearly — ensuring nobody dared report the truth. Employees were encouraged to load retirement accounts with Enron stock; when it collapsed from $90 to under $1, thousands lost jobs and life savings simultaneously.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A culture that punished truth-telling and worshipped 'talent' created a company where lying was the only way to survive internally.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Culture is what you tolerate — a workplace where bad news is career death guarantees you'll hear it last, when it's fatal. And never hold your salary AND savings in the same company's fate.
