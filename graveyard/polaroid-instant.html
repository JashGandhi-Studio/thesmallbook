Why Did Polaroid Fail?

📸 Died Guarding the Film That Funded It · 2001 · 💸 Bankruptcy; brand sold for parts

📜 What Happened

Polaroid didn't miss digital — it helped invent it, with quality digital prototypes in the 1980s and deep sensor patents. But the business model was razor-and-blades: cameras cheap, film at fat margins — and digital had no film. Executives couldn't approve a future without the blade revenue, so digital projects were starved while film cash flowed. When digital arrived anyway (without them), the collapse was total: bankruptcy in 2001, the beloved brand license-sold to strangers.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

They didn't fail to see the future — they refused to fund any future that didn't include their favorite revenue line.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Business models die harder than technologies. Ask the terrifying question early: 'what would we do if our most profitable line item disappeared?' — because someone else is building exactly that world.
