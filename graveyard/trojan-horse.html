Why Did The City of Troy Fail?

The Original Free Gift

~1180 BCYEAR
☠️ TRUSTCAUSE
💸 The city, the war, everythingTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Ten years of siege had failed. Then one morning the Greeks were gone - leaving only a giant wooden horse, an 'offering to Athena.' Priest Laocoön warned: 'I fear Greeks, even bearing gifts' (and was promptly eaten by sea serpents, which the Trojans read as divine disapproval of his doubt). Cassandra warned too - cursed to be never believed. Troy dragged the horse inside, partied, slept. The soldiers inside opened the gates. Troy burned.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Wanting the war to be over so badly that the convenient story beat the suspicious facts - and punishing the doubters instead of the doubt.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Beware the deal that arrives exactly when you're most tired of fighting. The urge to believe is the vulnerability - free gifts from adversaries are invoices with delayed billing.
