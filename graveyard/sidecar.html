Why Did Sidecar Fail?

The pioneer gets passed

2015YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Shut in 2015, assets to GMTHE BURN

What happened

Sidecar helped invent peer to peer ride sharing and experimented with deliveries and shared rides early. It had ideas the market later rewarded in larger companies. Uber and Lyft raised more, priced harder and won driver and rider liquidity city by city. Sidecar shut service at the end of 2015 and sold assets to GM.

The fatal mistake

Being right about the future while underfunded in the present.

The lesson

In network markets, second place can be a research department for the winner.
