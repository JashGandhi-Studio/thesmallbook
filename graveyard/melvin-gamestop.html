Why Did Melvin Capital Fail?

🎮 Shorted GameStop, Got Squeezed by Reddit · 2021 · 💸 53% in one month; fund dead by 2022

📜 What Happened

Melvin Capital was Wall Street royalty — until Reddit noticed its massive short position in GameStop, visible in public filings, exceeded 100% of the stock's float. Millions of retail traders on r/WallStreetBets bought in unison, partly for profit, partly for vengeance. The stock went from $17 to $483; Melvin lost 53% in a month, needed a $2.75B emergency rescue, and never recovered. The fund shut down in 2022.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A crowded, public, oversized short with unlimited downside — assuming retail investors were noise, not a coordinated force with memes and margin accounts.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never take a position whose maximum loss is infinite and whose existence is public. Markets can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent — especially when 'irrational' is having fun and hates you personally.
