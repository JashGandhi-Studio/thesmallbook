Why Did Q-Cells Fail?

The solar champion in the price war

2012YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Insolvent in 2012THE BURN

What happened

German Q-Cells helped make solar industrial and became one of the world's largest cell makers when panels still carried premium margins. Policy support and engineering pride built factories fast. Chinese scale and falling panel prices crushed margins while European subsidies cooled. Q-Cells filed for insolvency in 2012 and was sold to Hanwha, a champion absorbed by the cost curve it helped create.

The fatal mistake

Assuming technology leadership would protect commodity manufacturing margins.

The lesson

In solar, the learning curve belongs to whoever survives the price war, not whoever started it.
