Why Did Secret Fail?

🤫 The Founder Took Profits While the App Died · 2015 · 💸 $35M raised; shut in 16 months

📜 What Happened

Anonymous-sharing app Secret hit Silicon Valley like gossip lightning — and founders David Byttow and Chrys Bader-Wechseler cashed out $6M PERSONALLY in a funding round months after launch. The app's toxicity problem (harassment behind anonymity) mirrored Yik Yak's; usage cratered; 16 months after launch it shut down. Byttow returned some money — the cash-out remained the era's cautionary symbol.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Taking secondary money before the product proved durable — a signal (and self-fulfilling prophecy) that even the founders wouldn't hold the stock.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Founders selling early tells everyone what the inside view really is. Skin in the game isn't a slogan — its absence is always noticed, and always priced.
