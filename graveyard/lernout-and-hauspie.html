Why Did Lernout and Hauspie Fail?

The voice was ventriloquism

2001YEAR
FRAUDCAUSE
Bankrupt after accounting scandalTHE BURN

What happened

Lernout and Hauspie sold speech recognition when voice interfaces still felt like magic. Belgium celebrated it as a technology champion and investors treated language software as a national future. Revenue in Korea was largely fabricated and accounting unravelled under scrutiny. The company filed for bankruptcy in 2001, dragging reputations and a national tech dream with it.

The fatal mistake

Letting reported growth in distant units escape verification because the story needed heroes.

The lesson

The harder a market is to audit, the more boring your controls must be.
