Why Did Jet Airways Fail?

🛫 The Founder Who Couldn't Let Go · 2019 · 💸 India's premier airline, 20,000 jobs

📜 What Happened

Naresh Goyal built India's best full-service airline — then ran it as a personal fiefdom for 25 years: micromanaged decisions, revolving-door CEOs with no real authority, opaque related-party dealings, and refusal to dilute control even as debt mounted past ₹8,000 crore. Etihad wanted to invest more — with governance conditions. Goyal wouldn't cede control. The airline stopped flying in April 2019 with employees unpaid for months.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Choosing control over survival at every fork — a smaller piece of a flying airline beat 51% of a grounded one, and he chose grounded.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Founder's grip becomes founder's noose. If your company can't function — or be saved — without your signature on everything, you haven't built a business; you've built a hostage.
