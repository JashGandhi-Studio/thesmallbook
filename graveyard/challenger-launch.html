Why Did NASA Challenger Fail?

🚀 The Engineers Said No · 1986 · 💸 7 astronauts, live on television

📜 What Happened

The night before launch, Morton Thiokol engineers begged for a delay: the O-ring seals had never been tested at such cold temperatures and were already showing damage on prior flights. Managers — under schedule pressure with a teacher aboard and the world watching — asked them to 'take off your engineering hat and put on your management hat.' The seals failed 73 seconds after liftoff. The Rogers Commission found the disaster was organizational long before it was mechanical.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Hierarchy overrode physics. The information existed, the experts spoke — and the culture made the truth negotiable under deadline pressure.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Physics doesn't attend your meetings. When the people closest to the problem say stop, seniority is not a counter-argument — deadlines are never worth more than the thing the deadline is for.
