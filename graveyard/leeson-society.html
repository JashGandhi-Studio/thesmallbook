Why Did The Salem Panic Fail?

⚖️ When Fear Became Evidence: 20 Dead on 'Spectral' Testimony · 1692 · 💸 20 executed; a society's shame

📜 What Happened

In Salem, afflicted girls' fits were accepted as proof of witchcraft, and courts admitted 'spectral evidence' — testimony that the accused's SPIRIT had appeared in dreams. Denial was read as guilt; confession (with accusations of others) spared you — a mechanical engine for cascading accusations. Twenty were executed, one pressed to death for refusing to plead. It stopped only when accusations reached the governor's wife — suddenly, standards of evidence mattered. Courts later declared the trials unlawful; apologies took centuries.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The system punished doubt and rewarded accusation — once skepticism itself became suspect, the panic had no brakes until it touched power.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Any process where denial proves guilt and accusing others saves you will manufacture infinite guilt. Watch for it in mobs, offices, and timelines — the structure, not the era, is the danger.
