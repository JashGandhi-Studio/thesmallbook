Why Did Kodak Fail?

They Invented the Thing That Killed Them

2012YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 A 130-year empireTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Kodak engineer Steven Sasson built the world's FIRST digital camera in 1975. Management's response: 'That's cute — but don't tell anyone about it.' Film margins were 70%; digital threatened them. Kodak owned the patents, the brand, the labs — and sat on all of it for two decades while others built the future it had invented. Bankruptcy: 2012. The patents were sold to pay creditors.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Protecting the profitable present from the inevitable future — they saw the disruption FIRST and chose quarterly comfort over survival.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Your cash cow is someone else's target. Cannibalize yourself or be eaten — the choice is only ever WHO does it, never whether.
