Why Did Zano Drone Fail?

Europe's Biggest Kickstarter: 15,000 Drones That Couldn't Fly

2015YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 £2.3M from 12,000 backersTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Zano promised a palm-sized selfie drone with obstacle avoidance and follow-me AI — Europe's biggest Kickstarter (£2.3M). The demo video was largely aspiration; the shipped units (a few hundred of 15,000 owed) could barely hover, drifting into walls with dead batteries in minutes. The company liquidated abruptly; Kickstarter itself commissioned an investigative post-mortem — which found no scam, 'just' catastrophic overpromising by founders who believed their own render.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Sold the render, not the prototype — the campaign video showed capabilities that never existed in any build.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The demo you show is a contract. Crowdfund what your current prototype does on camera in one take — futures sold as presents become refunds, or worse.
