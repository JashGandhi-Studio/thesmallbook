Why Did Aereo Fail?

A Clever Antenna Met the Supreme Court

2014YEAR
TECHCAUSE
Service suspended and bankruptcy followed a copyright rulingTHE BURN

What happened

Aereo rented each subscriber a tiny remote antenna and streamed broadcast television over the internet. The company argued that thousands of individual antennas made the service private rather than a public performance. Broadcasters disagreed. In 2014 the United States Supreme Court ruled against Aereo, removing the legal foundation of the service. Aereo paused operations and filed for bankruptcy later that year.

The fatal mistake

Making one contested legal interpretation the load bearing wall of the entire business model.

The lesson

When regulation or copyright is the moat, test the worst plausible ruling early. Legal cleverness is not the same as legal safety.
