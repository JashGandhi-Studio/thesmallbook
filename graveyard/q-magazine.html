Why Did Q Magazine Fail?

The music monthly closes

2020YEAR
FAMECAUSE
Closed after 34 yearsTHE BURN

What happened

Q was the glossy British music magazine that treated albums as events. It survived the CD boom by selling authority, interviews and a monthly ritual for fans. Streaming made discovery instant and social feeds took the conversation. Print economics thinned for years, and the pandemic pushed the title into closure in 2020 despite its archive and brand love.

The fatal mistake

Selling slow authority in a market that had moved to instant access.

The lesson

When the ritual changes, heritage becomes inventory unless it finds a new ritual.
