Why Did Rajat Gupta Fail?

📞 The $100 Millionaire Who Wanted a Billion · 2012 · 💸 Reputation, freedom, legacy

📜 What Happened

Gupta ran McKinsey — the most trusted position in global business — sat on Goldman Sachs' board, co-founded the Indian School of Business, and was worth $100 million. Then he phoned hedge-fund friend Raj Rajaratnam 16 seconds after a confidential Goldman board call, passing inside information. He earned nothing directly from the tips. He wanted entry into the billionaire circle. He got prison, and his name became a business-school warning.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Social comparison at the summit: worth $100M, he measured himself against billionaires and felt poor.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The envy treadmill has no top floor. A man with everything risked it all for a club membership — if $100M plus global respect isn't 'enough,' the problem was never the number.
