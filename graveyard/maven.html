Why Did Maven Fail?

GM parks its sharing app

2020YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Shut in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Maven was General Motors' car sharing service for city users and gig drivers, part of the era when carmakers promised mobility subscriptions instead of only metal. It had fleet access rivals envied. Utilisation and economics never justified the strategic attention once priorities changed. GM wound Maven down in 2020, keeping pieces for other programmes and leaving the standalone app in the past.

The fatal mistake

Launching a service business inside a product giant without a protected profit logic.

The lesson

Carmakers can supply cars. Supply is not the same as demand.
