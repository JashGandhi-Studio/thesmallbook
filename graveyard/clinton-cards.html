Why Did Clinton Cards Fail?

The card shop that forgot the calendar

2012YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Entered administration in 2012; hundreds of stores closedTHE BURN

What happened

Clinton Cards made buying a birthday card feel like a British high street ritual, with hundreds of shops selling cards, gifts and celebration clutter for every family date. It owned the calendar in physical form. Supermarkets put cards beside the weekly shop, online services personalised and posted them, and Clinton carried too many expensive shops for a product with low baskets and seasonal spikes. The company entered administration in 2012 after its landlord and lenders lost patience; many stores closed and the brand survived only in reduced form.

The fatal mistake

Owning hundreds of shops to sell small, seasonal items while supermarkets and online rivals made the same purchase easier.

The lesson

A ritual product still needs a modern route to the ritual. If the reminder lives on a phone, the shop cannot rely on being walked past.
