Why Did British Home Stores Fail?

Sold for £1 With a £571 Million Pension Hole

2016YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
88 years of retail; 164 stores; 11,000 jobs; a £571M pension scheme left shortTHE BURN

What happened

BHS was a British high-street institution when Sir Philip Green, having taken over £1.2 billion in dividends during ownership, sold it in 2015 for one pound to a twice-bankrupt ex-racing driver with no retail experience and no money. Thirteen months later it entered administration. The pensions regulator pursued Green for years; he eventually contributed £363 million toward the scheme. MPs dubbed him 'the unacceptable face of capitalism' - the stores were gone either way.

The fatal mistake

Extracting the value while transferring the liabilities to a buyer selected for willingness, not capability - the pension was the canary and it was ignored in plain sight.

The lesson

In any sale, follow the pension and the debts, not the headline price. A company sold for £1 is priced exactly at the value of its obligations.
