Why Did Bernie Madoff Fail?

🎩 The $65 Billion Lie That Ran 30 Years · 2008 · 💸 $65B — history's largest Ponzi

📜 What Happened

Madoff was NASDAQ's former chairman, running a legitimate business earning $25M+ a year — and simultaneously the largest Ponzi scheme in history: $65 billion in fake account statements, paying old investors with new investors' money for three decades. The 2008 crisis triggered $7B in withdrawals he couldn't fake. He confessed to his sons; they turned him in within 24 hours. He died in prison; his family was destroyed.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

He was already rich and respected — and risked everything for a lie that could only end one way. Ponzi math has no exit.
