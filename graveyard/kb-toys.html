Why Did KB Toys Fail?

The mall toy store runs out of quarters

2008YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Liquidated after 2008 bankruptcyTHE BURN

What happened

KB Toys lived in malls and sold impulse joy near food courts. It survived the first rise of big box toy aisles by being close, bright and convenient. Walmart, Target and Toys R Us squeezed price while malls weakened. A leveraged structure left little room, and after a second bankruptcy KB liquidated in the 2008 holiday season.

The fatal mistake

Renting convenience in locations whose traffic was no longer guaranteed.

The lesson

Location is a strategy only while the crowd still comes to that location.
