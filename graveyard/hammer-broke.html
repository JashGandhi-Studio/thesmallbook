Why Did MC Hammer Fail?

The 200-Person Entourage

1996YEAR
☠️ MONEYCAUSE
💸 $33M → $13M in debtTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

'U Can't Touch This' made Hammer worth $33 million. He then built a $30M mansion (with gold-plated gates and a bowling alley), bought 19 racehorses, and — the legendary part — kept an entourage of roughly 200 friends and family on payroll costing $500,000 a month. Five years after his peak, he was $13 million in debt.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Fixed monthly costs (payroll, mansion) built on income that was always temporary. Hits end; salaries don't.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never build permanent obligations on peak income. The entourage eats when you feast — and keeps eating when you starve.
