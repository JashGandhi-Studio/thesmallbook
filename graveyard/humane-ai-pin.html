Why Did Humane AI Pin Fail?

The $700 Lapel Laser That Overheated on Every Question

2024YEAR
☠️ TECHCAUSE
💸 $230M venture funding burned; seeking fire-sale acquisitionTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Founded by former Apple design stars Imran Chaudhri and Bethany Bongiorno, Humane raised $230 million from Sam Altman, Microsoft, and Tiger Global to replace the smartphone with a screenless, voice-controlled wearable pin that projected green laser text onto the user's palm. Launched with immense secrecy and a $700 price tag plus a mandatory $24 monthly cellular subscription, the AI Pin proved to be an ergonomic nightmare: the device ran hot against users' chests, took five seconds to answer basic questions, hallucinated information, had weak battery life requiring multiple magnetic battery packs a day, and the green laser projector was completely invisible in daylight. Returns outnumbered sales, leaving Humane seeking an emergency buyer.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Building an expensive screenless hardware device that was strictly worse than the smartphone already in every consumer's pocket.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Do not attempt to replace an indispensable everyday tool with an inferior form factor. If your device requires more steps and delivers worse results than checking a phone screen, consumers will reject it immediately.
