Why Did Sharper Image Fail?

Gadgets without gravity

2008YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2008THE BURN

What happened

The Sharper Image made mall shopping feel like a glimpse of tomorrow: massage chairs, air purifiers and expensive curiosities. Catalogues turned novelty into desire. Its best known air purifier became a legal and reputational problem, novelty proved easy to copy, and the recession hit discretionary gadgets. The company filed for bankruptcy in 2008 and closed its stores.

The fatal mistake

Building a premium novelty store on products that could not stay novel or necessary.

The lesson

Wonder has a short shelf life. A store built on it must reinvent the wonder faster than rent comes due.
