Why Did Olive AI Fail?

$4B Healthcare 'AI' That Hospitals Quietly Returned

2023YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $850M raised → fire-sale shutdownTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Olive AI hit a $4B valuation selling hospitals an 'AI workforce' to automate their brutal admin work. Investigations later found much of the 'AI' was basic screen-scraping automation, sold faster than it could be made to work — customers reported projects that never delivered the promised savings. Olive kept raising and rebranding while quietly losing hospitals, then began mass layoffs, sold off business lines, and shut down completely in 2023.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Selling the AI story at maximum speed while the product underneath delivered a fraction of the pitch — growth outrunning truth.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

You can fundraise on a story, but you can only RENEW on results. Every overpromised deal is a delayed explosion with your logo on it — the gap between demo and delivery always comes due.
