Why Did Toshiba + Westinghouse Fail?

⚛️ A 140-Year Giant Broken by One Acquisition · 2017 · 💸 $6.3B writedown; the company dismembered

📜 What Happened

Toshiba paid $5.4B for nuclear-plant builder Westinghouse in 2006 — triple what rivals bid — betting on a global 'nuclear renaissance.' Fukushima (2011) froze the industry; US construction projects ran billions over budget under fixed-price contracts Westinghouse had guaranteed. Toshiba, meanwhile, was caught inflating profits for years (a $1.2B accounting scandal born of impossible internal 'Challenge' targets). The 2017 reckoning: $6.3B writedown, Westinghouse bankrupt, and Toshiba forced to sell its crown-jewel memory-chip business to survive — then got delisted after 74 years.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Overpaying at auction for a bet on one future, guaranteed with fixed-price promises, papered over by a culture that ordered profits into existence.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Winning an auction usually means you paid the most optimistic price in the room. And 'stretch targets' without honest accounting don't stretch performance — they stretch the truth until it snaps the company.
