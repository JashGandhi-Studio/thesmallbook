Why Did Beepi Fail?

🚙 $150M Used-Car Marketplace, $17M/Month Burn · 2017 · 💸 $150M

📜 What Happened

Beepi promised to reinvent used-car buying: white-glove inspections, purple bows on delivery. Behind the bows: burning $17M a MONTH, ~300 staff paid above-market, luxurious offices — a cost structure from 2021 funding fantasies running on 2016 realities. When markets tightened, rescue merger talks failed twice, and Beepi liquidated — its playbook later executed profitably by leaner rivals.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Spent like the winner of a market they hadn't won — the burn rate assumed infinite future rounds that were never promised.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Runway is strategy. The startup that spends at 'next round' pace dies at the first funding winter — and winters are never announced in advance.
