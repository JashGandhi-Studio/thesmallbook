Why Did InVision Fail?

The $2B Prototyping King Wiped Out by Figma

2024YEAR
☠️ TECHCAUSE
💸 $2B valuation; total shutdown and service discontinuationTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

InVision was the undisputed industry standard for digital product design in the 2010s: every tech company from Netflix to Airbnb used it to review UI mockups and click-through prototypes, powering a $2 billion valuation. But InVision was only a prototyping viewer for static designs created in Sketch or Photoshop. When Figma launched in the browser as a collaborative, real-time multiplayer design tool, designers no longer needed to export screens into InVision. InVision launched a clunky competitor, InVision Studio, years too late. In early 2024, InVision announced it was shutting down its design services permanently.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Relying on a static handoff layer between separate design tools instead of building real-time collaborative creation.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never position your product as a passive export destination between other creative tools. The tool where creators actually do the work will always absorb the review workflow.
