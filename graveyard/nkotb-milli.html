Why Did Milli Vanilli Fail?

🎙️ The Grammy Won by Two Men Who Never Sang · 1990 · 💸 Grammy revoked; careers erased

📜 What Happened

Milli Vanilli sold 30M+ singles and won the 1990 Best New Artist Grammy — and Rob and Fab had sung NONE of it (producer Frank Farian hired them as faces for session singers' vocals). The unraveling began onstage when their backing track skipped, repeating 'Girl you know it's—' like a broken machine. Under pressure, Farian confessed; the Grammy was revoked (unique in history); lawsuits offered refunds to millions of fans. Rob Pilatus spiraled and died at 32.
