Why Did Forever 21 Fail?

Fast Fashion Forgot to Get Faster

2019YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Two bankruptcies in five years; the 2024 filing liquidated the US chainTHE BURN

What happened

Forever 21 filled malls with cheap trends and hit a reported $4 billion revenue run by the mid-2010s. But it carried mall leases as the mall emptied, lost the ultra-fast supply chain race to Shein's on-demand model, and squandered goodwill by suing a blogger and shipping diet pills in jeans orders. The 2019 bankruptcy cut the debt; the 2024 one closed the story, with Shein itself briefly buying the brand it helped kill.

The fatal mistake

Paying 2005 rents to sell 2019 fashion against 2019 competition - the cost base was locked to a retail geography the customer had already left.

The lesson

In fast fashion, supply-chain speed is the moat and the lease book is the anchor. When your competitor iterates in days and you in seasons, every store is a museum.
