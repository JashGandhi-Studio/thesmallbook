Why Did Byju's Fail?

📚 India's $22B Edtech Star That Bought Everything but Discipline · 2024 · 💸 $22B valuation → ~$0

📜 What Happened

India's most valuable startup rode COVID-era edtech mania to $22B: acquisitions (Aakash, WhiteHat Jr, Great Learning — $2.5B+ spree), cricket sponsorships, Messi as ambassador. Underneath: aggressive sales tactics selling costly courses to families who couldn't afford them, audited financials perpetually delayed, an $85M+ FIFA sponsorship as revenue collapsed, and a $1.2B loan in default. Investors wrote holdings to zero; the founder's stake evaporated.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Growth-by-acquisition and image-by-sponsorship while the core product's honesty (sales culture, real learning outcomes, real accounting) rotted.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Valuation is a mood; audited numbers are a fact. When a company delays its accounts and accelerates its sponsorships, the sequence tells you everything.
