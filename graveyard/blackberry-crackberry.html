Why Did DeLorean Motor Company Fail?

🚗 The Car So Iconic It Needed a Time Machine to Sell · 1982 · 💸 $175M + founder's reputation

📜 What Happened

John DeLorean — GM's youngest-ever division head, celebrity executive — left to build his stainless-steel gullwinged dream car. Everything was maximal: a factory in Northern Ireland (for government subsidies) with an untrained workforce, constant redesigns, a 1981 recession launch at nearly double the promised price. Quality was dire, sales a fraction of projections. Desperate for cash, DeLorean was arrested in an FBI cocaine-trafficking sting (later acquitted — entrapment). The company died; the car became famous three years later — in Back to the Future.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Celebrity confidence substituting for manufacturing discipline: every decision optimized for the legend, none for the assembly line.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A great story doesn't survive bad build quality. And desperation is the most expensive state to make decisions in — it turns founders into marks.
