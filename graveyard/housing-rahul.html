Why Did Housing.com's Rahul Yadav Fail?

🏠 India's Most Talented Founder Fired Himself With His Mouth · 2015 · 💸 A $250M-funded rocketship's momentum

📜 What Happened

Rahul Yadav, 26, built Housing.com into India's hottest startup — $100M+ from SoftBank, genuine product brilliance. Then the implosion, in public: a leaked resignation letter calling investors 'intellectually incapable,' Twitter wars with rival CEOs, allotting his entire personal equity to employees in a grand gesture, then un-resigning. The board, exhausted, fired him within months for behavior 'not befitting a CEO.' The company he built merged away its independence; the era's most talented product founder became its cautionary tale.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treating investors, board, and rivals as an audience for dominance displays — burning every bridge while standing on it.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Being right about product buys you nothing if you're impossible to work with. Power in a funded company is borrowed, not owned — the people who gave it can take it back, and 'brilliant but radioactive' always ends the same way.
