Why Did Gitanjali Gems Fail?

💍 The Other Uncle in the PNB Fraud · 2018 · 💸 Part of the ₹14,000 crore PNB hole

📜 What Happened

Mehul Choksi built Gitanjali Gems into a 4,000-store jewelry empire with celebrity endorsements — respectability itself. With nephew Nirav Modi, he ran the same PNB letters-of-undertaking carousel: fake bank guarantees rolling over for years, funding a listed company whose glitter was borrowed. When it broke, Choksi had already taken citizenship in Antigua under its investment program. Lakhs of small shareholders in his listed companies were wiped out; franchisees who'd paid for stores were stranded with worthless stock.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

For investors: mistaking celebrity endorsements, stock listings, and store counts for proof that the underlying money was real.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Scale and fame are not audits. The flashier the empire, the more you should want to see the boring documents — fraud hides behind exactly the glitter that makes questions feel rude.
