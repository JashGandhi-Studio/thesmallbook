Why Did Ronald Wayne Fail?

🍏 The Man Who Sold 10% of Apple for $800 · 1976 · 💸 ~$300 BILLION (today's value)

📜 What Happened

Apple's forgotten third co-founder drew the first logo, wrote the partnership agreement — and 12 days later sold his 10% stake back for $800, fearing liability for the company's debts (he was the only founder with assets to lose). That stake would today be worth around $300 billion. He later sold his original Apple contract copy for $500; it re-sold at auction for $1.6 million.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Optimizing for zero risk at the exact moment asymmetric upside was born. He protected $800 and forfeited history.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The biggest risk is often the safe move. Downside was capped; upside was infinite — when equity is offered at the ground floor of something great, fear is the expensive option.
