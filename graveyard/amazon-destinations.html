Why Did Amazon Destinations Fail?

Amazon Tried to Out-TripAdvisor TripAdvisor. For One Summer.

2015YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Launched April 2015, shut by October - one of Amazon's fastest-ever killsTHE BURN

What happened

Amazon noticed travellers book hotels near the things they buy and do, so it launched Destinations: a travel booking service for getaways near major cities. The problem was that travel needs supply, supply needs inventory, and inventory needs relationships Amazon had not built. The catalogue was thin, the discounts were shallow, and six months later the service vanished as quietly as it arrived.

The fatal mistake

Treating 'people buy things from us' as 'people will buy this thing from us' - entering a supply-constrained business with a demand-side playbook.

The lesson

Marketplace trust does not transfer across categories that need different infrastructure. expansion is a supply problem before it is a demand problem.
