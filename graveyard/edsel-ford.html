Why Did Ford Edsel Fail?

🚗 The $250M Car Nobody Asked For · 1958 · 💸 $250M (≈ $2.5B today)

📜 What Happened

Ford spent a decade and $250M researching, designing, and hyping the Edsel — 'the car of the future,' teased for months in ads that showed everything but the car. Launch day revealed: an odd vertical grille (compared unkindly to a toilet seat), a price straddling markets confusingly, quality problems from rushed production, and a 1958 recession killing mid-market demand. Ford projected 200,000 sales in year one; the entire three-year run sold barely 118,000. 'Edsel' became American for flop.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Ten years of inside-out planning — committees naming, positioning, and hyping — with the actual customer's actual desire never truly tested against the actual car.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Hype raises the cliff, not the bridge. The bigger the launch, the harder the honest question matters beforehand: has anyone outside this building actually asked for this?
