Why Did Decca Records Fail?

🎸 'Guitar Groups Are on the Way Out' · 1962 · 💸 The Beatles

📜 What Happened

On New Year's Day 1962, four boys from Liverpool auditioned for Decca Records — 15 songs in an hour. Decca's verdict, delivered to their manager: 'Guitar groups are on the way out, Mr. Epstein... The Beatles have no future in show business.' They signed a local band called The Tremeloes instead (closer to London — cheaper travel). The Beatles became the best-selling act in the history of recorded music.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Confusing a trend forecast with a talent evaluation — and letting travel expenses influence an A&R decision.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Gatekeepers are guessing too. Every field's experts have rejected that field's biggest phenomenon — so a 'no' is one opinion, not a verdict. (And penny-wise logistics have killed more fortunes than bad taste.)
