Why Did Amazon Fire Phone Fail?

Jeff Bezos's $170 Million Smartphone Flop

2014YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $170M+ written off; 3D gimmick phone discontinued in a yearTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Amazon decided to conquer the smartphone - and the Fire Phone was Bezos's personal project: a device with four cameras and 'Dynamic Perspective' 3D effects, priced higher than the iPhone. The problem: nobody asked for 3D gimmicks, the app store was thin, and the price screamed hubris. It flopped so hard that Amazon wrote off $170 million in unsold inventory and killed the project within a year. The richest company's customer obsession - its core principle - was abandoned for a technology demo.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Building what the company wanted to build (a 3D showcase) instead of what customers wanted - the ultimate violation of Amazon's own 'customer obsession' principle.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Your own principles are only real when they're inconvenient. Features nobody asked for are decorations, not differentiators - fall in love with customer problems, not your own toys.
