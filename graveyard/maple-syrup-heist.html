Why Did Québec's Strategic Syrup Reserve Fail?

🍁 $18M of Maple Syrup Stolen — Barrel by Barrel, for a Year · 2012 · 💸 $18.7M — 3,000 tonnes of syrup

📜 What Happened

Canada's syrup cartel kept a strategic reserve — thousands of barrels in a rented warehouse, audited once a year by counting barrels. Thieves rented space in the SAME warehouse, and for a year quietly siphoned syrup from barrels, refilling many with water, trucking $18.7M of liquid gold to black-market buyers. The heist — the largest agricultural theft in history — was discovered only when an inspector climbed a stack and a 'full' barrel nearly tipped over because it was empty.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

An audit that checked the CONTAINER, not the contents — verification theater that measured the easiest thing instead of the true thing.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Whatever you 'check' will be gamed exactly at the level you check it. Audit the substance, rotate the method, and assume anyone with a year of unsupervised access has used it.
