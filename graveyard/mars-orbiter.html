Why Did NASA Mars Climate Orbiter Fail?

🛰️ The $327M Metric Mix-Up · 1999 · 💸 $327.6M spacecraft

📜 What Happened

After a 9-month journey to Mars, the Climate Orbiter burned up in the Martian atmosphere because Lockheed Martin's software sent thrust data in POUND-FORCE while NASA's navigation expected NEWTONS. Multiple navigators had noticed course anomalies for months; the concerns never made it through the formal reporting process. One unit conversion — the kind checked on high-school homework — ended a $327 million mission.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Not the units error itself — errors happen — but a process where noticed anomalies died in the paperwork instead of triggering alarms.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Catastrophes are rarely exotic; they're small errors that no process caught. The boring checklists ARE the mission — details don't care how brilliant the rest of the project is.
