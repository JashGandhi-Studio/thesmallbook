Why Did Rajneeshpuram Fail?

🕉️ The Spiritual Utopia That Became a Surveillance State · 1984 · 💸 A commune of thousands, 751 poisonings, and the guru's movement exiled from America

📜 What Happened

Rajneeshpuram was built in the Oregon desert in the 1980s: a utopian city for thousands of followers of the Indian guru Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh (Osho), designed to prove that a spiritual community could be self-sufficient, free and enlightened. What it became was the opposite: a fortress run by the guru's personal secretary, Ma Anand Sheela, with surveillance, weapons, and a secret plan to take over the local county by busing in homeless voters. When the plan failed, the commune poisoned salad bars across Oregon — 751 people fell ill, two communities were devastated — to suppress voter turnout. The FBI descended, the commune collapsed in scandal, the guru was deported, and the 'utopia' became a synonym for spiritual authority without accountability. The movement that preached inner freedom had built a police state.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Absolute trust in the leader — followers surrendered every check, and unchecked power did what unchecked power always does.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The Rajneeshpuram test applies to every spiritual movement, startup and political campaign: the moment followers stop questioning the leader, the leader's worst impulses stop being impulses. It is not enough for the mission to be beautiful — the power structure must be accountable. Any organization where 'the guru knows best' is the operating system is already building its own police state; it just hasn't called the FBI yet.
