Why Did Zirtual Fail?

The Startup That Switched Off Overnight

2015YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 About 400 jobs in a day; clients locked out overnightTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Zirtual gave busy founders a US-based virtual assistant for a monthly fee, grew to about 400 staff, and looked like the tidy future of remote work. Then, on August 10 2015, clients got an email: operations were 'paused', effective immediately. The books showed the company was burning far more than its founders believed, payroll could not be met, and years of growth had been financed by hope. Startups.co bought the pieces and relaunched Zirtual smaller, with the same name and a hard lesson attached.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Growing headcount on vanity numbers while nobody reconciled what each assistant actually cost to deliver.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Know your burn multiple every single week. Companies rarely die from one bad month; they die from twelve months nobody added up.
