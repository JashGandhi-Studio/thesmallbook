Why Did Ackman vs. Herbalife Fail?

🥊 A $1B Short Held for 5 Years — Out of Spite · 2018 · 💸 ~$1B and 5 years of focus

📜 What Happened

Bill Ackman publicly declared Herbalife a pyramid scheme and bet $1B on its stock going to ZERO — with a 3-hour presentation, a documentary, and total moral certainty. Rival billionaire Carl Icahn, who Ackman had feuded with for years, took the other side ON LIVE TV and bought 26% of the company, squeezing the short. Regulators fined Herbalife $200M but didn't kill it. Ackman held for FIVE YEARS as losses mounted, unable to walk away from a bet that had become his identity. He finally exited in 2018, near total loss on the position.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Turning a trade into a crusade — once his ego was publicly staked, exiting became psychologically impossible at any price.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The moment a position becomes your identity, you've stopped investing and started defending yourself. Size every bet so you can be wrong quietly — the market charges extra for public certainty.
