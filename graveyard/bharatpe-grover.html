Why Did BharatPe & Ashneer Fail?

The Shark Who Bit His Own Company

2022YEAR
☠️ EGOCAUSE
💸 A $2.8B fintech's reputation + his own seatTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Ashneer Grover co-built BharatPe into a $2.8B fintech and became a household name as Shark Tank India's bluntest judge. Then a leaked audio surfaced of him abusing a bank employee over IPO shares; his defiance ('I'm not apologizing') turned a PR problem into a board war. A governance review alleged family-run expense fraud — luxury trips and personal bills through fake vendors; his wife (head of controls) was implicated; he resigned minutes before the board could fire him, then live-tweeted the war. Criminal cases followed. The company survived; his throne didn't.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Believing the founder IS the company — treating governance as an insult, the board as staff, and the company's account as a family wallet.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Aggression can build a company and still be the exact thing that takes it from you. Power earned publicly gets revoked publicly — and boards forgive missed numbers far more easily than humiliation.
