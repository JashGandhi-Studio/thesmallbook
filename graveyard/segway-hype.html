Why Did Segway Fail?

🛴 'Bigger Than the Internet' — Sold 30k Units in 6 Years · 2009 · 💸 $100M+; predictions off by 99%

📜 What Happened

Pre-launch, Segway ('Project Ginger') was hyped by Steve Jobs ('as big as the PC') and Jeff Bezos; inventor Dean Kamen predicted 10,000 units a WEEK and said cities would be redesigned around it. The reveal: a $5,000 scooter you couldn't ride on sidewalks (banned in many cities), roads (too slow), or anywhere dignity mattered. It sold ~30,000 units in six years — 1% of projections — becoming shorthand for hype-vs-reality. Mall cops and tour groups kept it on life support.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Brilliant engineering aimed at a problem ('walking') that nobody experienced as a problem — plus total blindness to price, regulation, and the dork factor.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Technology impressiveness ≠ customer desire. The question is never 'is it amazing?' but 'what painful thing does it replace, at what price, looking how?'
