Why Did The Metaverse Bet Fail?

🥽 $46B for a World Nobody Moved Into · 2023 · 💸 $46B+ burned; stock -76% at trough

📜 What Happened

In 2021 Mark Zuckerberg renamed Facebook to 'Meta' and redirected the company toward the metaverse — legless avatars, virtual offices, a future he declared inevitable. Reality Labs burned $10-15B per YEAR while flagship world Horizon Worlds struggled to keep even 200,000 monthly users — reports said many were Meta employees, reluctantly. The stock fell 76% before Zuckerberg partially retreated to a 'year of efficiency' (and then to AI). The metaverse didn't die — but $46B+ bought the lesson that you can't schedule a paradigm.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Betting the company's identity on a timeline for OTHER PEOPLE's behavior — renaming the ship before checking if passengers wanted the destination.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

You can fund the future, but you can't invoice it a deadline. Visions need reality checkpoints — when users vote no for two straight years, the vision needs editing, not just more budget.
