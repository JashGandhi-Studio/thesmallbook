Why Did Easter Island Fail?

🗿 They Cut Down the Last Tree · ~1600 · 💸 An entire civilization's future

📜 What Happened

Rapa Nui's civilization built the moai — 900 giant stone heads — in escalating competition between clans, each statue demanding timber for transport. The palm forests that provided canoes (deep-sea fishing), soil protection, and everything else were cut faster than they grew — for monuments. When the last trees fell, no canoes could be built, soil washed away, and the society that carved wonders collapsed into famine. Someone, as the saying goes, cut down the last tree — knowing it was the last.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Status competition consuming the resource base that made ANY status possible — every clan acting rationally, the island dying collectively.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Competing on monuments while the foundation erodes is civilizational lifestyle inflation. Ask what your moai is — and what trees it's costing. Some races are lost by everyone who runs them.
