Why Did Société Générale Fail?

🎰 One Junior Trader, €50B in Secret Bets · 2008 · 💸 €4.9B unwinding one employee's trades

📜 What Happened

Jérôme Kerviel was a junior trader who'd worked in the bank's back office — so he knew exactly which controls checked what, and when. He built €50B in unauthorized positions (more than the bank's entire market value), hiding them with fake offsetting trades timed to vanish before checks ran. Compliance flagged anomalies 74 times; each time his explanations were accepted. When the bank finally found the truth, unwinding the position in a falling market cost €4.9B.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

74 warnings dismissed because the profits looked good — controls existed on paper but every alarm was explainable away while the numbers went up.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A control that can be talked out of is not a control. Alarms exist precisely for the moments when everything looks fine — the more profitable the anomaly, the harder you should look.
