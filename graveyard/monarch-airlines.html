Why Did Monarch Airlines Fail?

Fifty Years of Flying, Grounded by a Monday Announcement

2017YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
2,100 jobs gone; 860,000 bookings cancelled; the UK's largest airline failure until Thomas CookTHE BURN

What happened

Monarch flew for half a century, but the mid-2010s squeezed it from every side: fuel up, sterling down after the Brexit vote, terror-shaken routes, and price wars with package rivals on the beaches it served. Two rescues (including a £165M Graybull turnaround bet in 2014) bought time, not a model. At 4 AM on 2 October 2017 the CAA grounded the fleet, launching the UK's biggest peacetime repatriation before Thomas Cook's.

The fatal mistake

Staying a full-service legacy in a market repricing to bare-bones while refinancing with short-horizon private equity - every rescue added debt without changing the cost base.

The lesson

Turnaround capital fixes the balance sheet, not the business. If the model loses money at today's prices, financing is just a schedule for the funeral.
