Why Did PSINet Fail?

The internet backbone bends

2002YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2002THE BURN

What happened

PSINet helped commercialise the internet and expanded globally by buying providers across continents. It wanted to be the carrier behind the new economy everywhere at once. The debt used to buy growth met falling prices and the telecom crash. PSINet filed for bankruptcy in 2002 and sold its pieces, another backbone bent by the cost of becoming global too fast.

The fatal mistake

Buying geography before proving density economics.

The lesson

Coverage is not advantage if every new country brings more cost than pricing power.
