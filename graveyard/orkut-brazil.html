Why Did Orkut Fail?

Google's Social Network That Won Two Countries and Lost Earth

2014YEAR

📜 What Happened

Orkut launched WEEKS before Facebook in 2004, with Google's infinite resources behind it. It became the #1 site in Brazil and India — then Google's attention wandered: spam overwhelmed communities, performance lagged, redesigns arrived years late, and the A-team moved to other projects (eventually Google+). Facebook methodically localized, shipped weekly, and took both strongholds. Google closed Orkut in 2014 — having held the winning position in two of the world's biggest markets and simply... not pressed it.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Treated a winning product as a side project — victory positions decay by default; nobody at Google woke up owning Orkut's survival.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Markets aren't won once; they're won weekly. An unattended lead is a countdown — if the winning product isn't someone's whole job, it's nobody's.
