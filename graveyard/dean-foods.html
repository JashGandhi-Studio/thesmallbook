Why Did Herbalife of Bre-X Fail?

Bre-X: The Gold Mine That Was Salted Shavings

1997YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 $6B market cap → $0THE BURN

📜 What Happened

Canadian miner Bre-X claimed the century's biggest gold find in Borneo — 70 million ounces. The stock rode from pennies to a $6B valuation; pension funds piled in. The 'gold': core samples salted with shaved jewelry gold and river gold, for YEARS, by the site geologist. When partner Freeport's independent drilling found NOTHING, the geologist fell from a helicopter over the jungle days before questioning. The stock went to zero in weeks; Canadian markets rewrote their mining disclosure rules (NI 43-101) because of it.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A $6B valuation rested on samples that only the company itself ever verified — years of 'independent' analysis of tampered cores.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Whoever controls the sample controls the story. In any extraordinary claim, fund the verification yourself, at the source, or assume the shavings.
