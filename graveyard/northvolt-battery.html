Why Did Northvolt Fail?

Europe's $15B Battery Sovereign Hope Ran Out of Scrap

2024YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $15B in capital; Chapter 11 filing in the USTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Founded by former Tesla executives Peter Carlsson and Paolo Cerruti, Northvolt was Europe's great industrial hope to break China's monopoly on electric vehicle batteries, backed by Volkswagen, BMW, Goldman Sachs, and the European Investment Bank with over $15 billion in equity and loans. It built the Ett gigafactory near the Arctic Circle in Sweden, powered by clean hydropower. But manufacturing lithium-ion battery cells at scale requires extreme precision. The factory suffered severe scrap rates, production delays, and equipment bottlenecks. In June 2024, BMW canceled a 2 billion euro battery contract due to missed delivery deadlines. Northvolt filed Chapter 11 in November 2024.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Underestimating the brutal manufacturing ramp learning curve of advanced battery chemistry while expanding to multiple international sites simultaneously.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Government subsidies and geopolitical goodwill cannot substitute for manufacturing yield. If your battery factory produces excessive scrap, political support will not keep your balance sheet solvent.
