Why Did The Beatles Fail?

🎶 The Manager Who Managed Them Apart · 1969 · 💸 The band itself

📜 What Happened

After manager Brian Epstein's death, the Beatles' finances were chaos (Apple Corps was bleeding money on boutiques and whims). John, George, and Ringo hired tough-guy manager Allen Klein; Paul wanted his father-in-law's firm. The 3-vs-1 split poisoned everything — Klein's aggressive deals and opaque accounting deepened the mistrust, and the money fights finished what creative tensions had started. Paul sued his own bandmates to escape Klein; the Beatles ended in courtrooms. Klein was later jailed for tax fraud.
