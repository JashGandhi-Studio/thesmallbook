Why Did HQ Trivia Fail?

Millions Watched, Then the Room Emptied

2020YEAR
TECHCAUSE
A live game phenomenon closed after its audience fadedTHE BURN

What happened

HQ Trivia turned a phone into a live game show, drawing huge simultaneous audiences with cash prizes and a charismatic host. The format was easy to copy, expensive to sustain and vulnerable to bots, technical glitches and novelty decay. Attempts to find a buyer failed in February 2020 and the company shut down, although the app later had brief attempts at revival under new ownership.

The fatal mistake

Confusing a scheduled media moment with a durable product loop that could retain users and advertisers between jackpots.

The lesson

Virality is rented attention. Unless each spike leaves behind habit, community or subscription value, the next show starts from zero.
