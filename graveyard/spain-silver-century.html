Why Did Spain's Silver Century Fail?

The Richest Country in Europe Went Bankrupt Six Times

1557YEAR
HISTORYCAUSE
Sovereign defaults in 1557, 1575, 1596, 1607, 1627 and 1647; a century of inflation while the silver lastedTHE BURN

What happened

After Potosí, Spanish galleons moved more silver than the world had ever seen - and Spain spent it on wars, not ships or farms. Prices rose across Europe for a hundred years (the 'price revolution') while Spanish industry atrophied: why make what silver buys? Each bankruptcies forced debt restructurings with Genoese bankers; by 1650 the empire's income was pledged decades forward and the silver mountain had produced the poorest great power in Europe.

The fatal mistake

Treating a resource windfall as income instead of capital - the silver bought consumption while every producer economy around it compounded.

The lesson

Windfalls spent are curses with interest. The test of a windfall economy is what it builds while the commodity flows; everything else is just scheduled decline.

The antidote book

The Intelligent InvestorBenjamin Graham · Money & Finance · 6 lessons

The investor's chief problem - and even his worst enemy - is likely to be himself. Buy value, demand a margin of safety, and use Mr. Market instead of obeying him.
