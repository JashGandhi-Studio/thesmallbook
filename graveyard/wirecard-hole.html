Why Did Wirecard Fail?

€1.9 Billion That Never Existed

2020YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 €24B market cap → insolvencyTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Germany's fintech champion — a DAX-30 member worth more than Deutsche Bank — claimed €1.9B in Philippine trustee accounts. Auditors finally checked: the money didn't exist; the banks had never heard of the accounts. Journalists (FT's Dan McCrum) had reported the fraud for YEARS — and German regulators responded by investigating the JOURNALISTS and banning short-selling. COO Jan Marsalek fled and remains one of Interpol's most wanted.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Not just the fraud — the ecosystem's refusal to check: auditors accepted confirmations for years without verifying, and regulators defended the company against its truthful critics.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

'Trust but verify' fails at scale without the verify. When a company attacks its questioners instead of answering the questions, the questions are the answer.
