Why Did Canoo EV Fail?

The Modular Skateboard That Ran Out of Runway

2024YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $600M+ burned; persistent going-concern warningsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Canoo developed a distinct cab-forward electric lifestyle vehicle on a proprietary flat skateboard chassis, backed by former BMW executives. Going public via SPAC at a $2.4 billion valuation in 2020, Canoo planned to offer vehicles purely on a subscription model without dealerships. But leadership clashes quickly dismantled the strategy: the subscription model was abandoned, engineering co-founders fled, and an engineering partnership with Hyundai dissolved. Despite promotional pilot deals with Walmart and NASA, Canoo burned cash with negligible revenue, leaving its shares trading for pocket change.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Pivoting business models three times in two years while burning millions on experimental lifestyle vehicle concepts.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Changing your customer model from subscription to direct sale to commercial fleet mid-stride destroys production momentum. In automotive manufacturing, focus is survival.
