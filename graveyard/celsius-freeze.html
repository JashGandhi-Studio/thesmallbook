Why Did Celsius Network Fail?

🧊 'Banks Are Not Your Friends' — Neither Was He · 2022 · 💸 $4.7B of customer crypto frozen

📜 What Happened

Alex Mashinsky's pitch was seductive: banks rob you, so give Celsius your crypto and earn 18% yield. 'Unbank yourself.' Weekly AMAs, endless confidence. The yield came from increasingly desperate bets with customer funds — and when markets turned, Celsius froze all withdrawals, revealing a $1.2B hole. 1.7 million users lost access to $4.7B. Mashinsky sold his own tokens before the freeze and was later convicted of fraud.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Chasing an 18% yield without asking the only question that matters: who exactly is paying this, and what happens when they stop?

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Yield is never free — it's payment for risk someone isn't explaining. If you can't identify where the return comes from, YOU are where the return comes from.
