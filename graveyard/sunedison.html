Why Did SunEdison Fail?

The solar empire eats itself

2016YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2016THE BURN

What happened

SunEdison grew from silicon roots into a solar development giant and invented financial structures to own renewable assets. Wall Street rewarded speed while sun power became serious infrastructure. Acquisitions and yieldco complexity piled debt faster than projects could produce trust. When markets questioned the machine, liquidity vanished and SunEdison filed for bankruptcy in 2016 as one of the era's major non bank collapses.

The fatal mistake

Using financial engineering to outrun project cash reality.

The lesson

Complex structures can hide risk from investors for a while. They cannot hide it from maturity dates.
