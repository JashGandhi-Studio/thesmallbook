Why Did Atari's E.T. Fail?

👽 The Game So Bad They Buried It in the Desert · 1983 · 💸 $536M loss; helped crash an industry

📜 What Happened

Atari paid $21M for E.T. movie rights in July 1982 and demanded the game ship for Christmas — giving one programmer FIVE WEEKS (normal: 6+ months). They manufactured 4 million cartridges for a game that was barely playable; an estimated 3.5M came back or never sold. Atari secretly dumped truckloads into a New Mexico landfill (excavated in 2014 — the legend was true). The debacle helped trigger the 1983 crash that nearly killed the entire video game industry.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The license and the deadline were the whole plan — the product itself got five weeks. Marketing calendar beat product reality.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

You can't schedule quality by decree. Shipping a bad product on time costs infinitely more than shipping a good one late — sometimes it costs the whole industry.
