Why Did Allbirds Fail?

The Silicon Valley Wool Sneaker That Strayed From Its Core

2023YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $4B IPO valuation collapsed 98% to penny-stock territoryTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Allbirds conquered Silicon Valley and Wall Street in 2016 with its ultra-comfortable, minimalist Wool Runner made from sustainable New Zealand merino wool and sugarcane soles, backed by Leonardo DiCaprio. In 2021, it completed a celebrated $4 billion IPO as a premier sustainable consumer brand. But after going public, Allbirds lost its focus: it opened dozens of expensive brick-and-mortar retail stores and launched bloated apparel lines (underwear, puffer jackets, leggings, running shorts) that nobody wanted. Meanwhile, its core shoes wore out quickly and lost their status appeal to On Running and Hoka. Allbirds suffered huge losses, forcing store closures and founder resignations.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Diluting a singular iconic product identity by expanding into generic apparel while neglecting core shoe durability.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Do not rush into brand extension before mastering the durability and defensibility of your hero product. When you make everything, your brand stands for nothing.
