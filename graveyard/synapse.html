Why Did Synapse Fail?

The Fintech Middleman That Lost Track of $160 Million

2024YEAR
FRAUDCAUSE
Tens of thousands of end users locked out of their accounts; the bankruptcy court still can't say whose money is whoseTHE BURN

What happened

Synapse was the plumbing of fintech: banking-as-a-service middleware connecting app accounts to partner banks. When it went bankrupt in April 2024, the industry discovered nobody had kept a unified ledger - Synapse's records, the banks' records and the apps' records disagreed about who owned the roughly $160 million sitting in the pipes. A court-appointed trustee had to freeze everything while reconciling by hand. Savings apps froze; end users paid the price.

The fatal mistake

Standing between banks and apps as the ledger of record while reconciling with spreadsheets - the one job of the middleman was the one job not done.

The lesson

In money movement, the reconciliation IS the company. Ask any intermediary for their daily three-way reconciliation before you ask for their pitch deck.
