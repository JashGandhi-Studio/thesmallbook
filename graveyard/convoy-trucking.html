Why Did Convoy Fail?

🚛 $3.8B 'Uber for Trucking' Hit a Wall at Full Speed · 2023 · 💸 $900M raised; near-zero return

📜 What Happened

Convoy — backed by Bezos, Gates, and top VCs at a $3.8B valuation — digitized freight matching between shippers and truckers. The tech worked. But trucking is a brutal, cyclical, wafer-thin-margin commodity market, and Convoy's software convenience never translated into pricing power. When the 2022-23 freight recession crushed rates, Convoy was still structurally unprofitable and mid-fundraise. Money markets slammed shut, and one of logistics' best-funded startups shut down in days, catching even employees off guard.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Assuming great software creates margin in a commodity market that has never awarded margin to anyone — then running out of runway exactly when the cycle turned.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Technology doesn't repeal an industry's economics. In commodity markets the winner is the lowest-cost survivor of the worst year — build for the trough, not the demo day.
