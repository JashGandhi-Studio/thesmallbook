Why Did Windows Vista Fail?

The Operating System That Made Everyone Wait for the Next One

2007YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Five years of development, then five years of brand damage; enterprises clung to XP until 2009's Windows 7THE BURN

What happened

Vista arrived in January 2007 after the longest gap in Windows history, and it asked the world for new hardware, new drivers and new patience. Programs broke, printers stopped, and a security prompt interrupted every third click until users trained themselves to approve anything. IT departments simply refused. Microsoft spent years apologising with 'Mojave' ads that secretly showed people Vista and watched them like it.

The fatal mistake

Shipping the platform's big rewrite before the ecosystem was ready - the OS was ahead of the drivers, the laptops and the users, and every gap was charged to the customer.

The lesson

A platform is only as good as its worst-supported peripheral. Never make early adopters pay the integration tax for your ambition; they are your evangelists, not your beta testers.
