Why Did Theranos Fail?

A Board of Generals With Not One Scientist

2018YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 A $9B valuation to zero; Holmes jailed for 11+ yearsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Theranos promised 200 blood tests from one drop of blood, and Elizabeth Holmes filled her board with two secretaries of state, two senators, generals and a former CDC director, prestige instead of expertise. The Edison machines rarely worked; most tests quietly ran on modified Siemens analysers, and results sent to real patients were sometimes dangerously wrong. John Carreyrou's 2015 Wall Street Journal investigation, and whistleblowers Tyler Shultz and Erika Cheung, pulled the thread. The SEC charged fraud in 2018, Theranos dissolved, and Holmes was convicted in 2022 and sentenced to over 11 years in prison.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Governance as decoration: a board hired to impress investors instead of directors able to test the one claim the company stood on.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Ask who in the room can say 'show me'. A board that cannot interrogate the core technology is not oversight, it is marketing.
