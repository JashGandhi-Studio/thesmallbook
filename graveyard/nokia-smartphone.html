Why Did Nokia Fail?

📞 'We Didn't Do Anything Wrong, But We Lost' · 2013 · 💸 50% market share → sold off

📜 What Happened

Nokia owned half the world's phone market and had a working touchscreen smartphone prototype YEARS before the iPhone — plus an internal report predicting exactly Apple's move. Middle managers, afraid of top management's tempers, softened bad news at every layer; engineers knew Symbian was doomed but saying so was career damage. At the sale to Microsoft, the CEO ended his speech: 'We didn't do anything wrong, but somehow, we lost' — and the room wept.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

They did do something wrong: they built a culture where fear filtered the truth into comfort, until reality arrived unfiltered.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

'We did nothing wrong' IS the wrong thing. In fast markets, standing still is a decision — and a culture where messengers get shot becomes a company that gets shot.
