Why Did Anil Ambani Fail?

📉 From World's 6th Richest to Court-Declared Broke · 2020 · 💸 $42B → 'net worth zero'

📜 What Happened

In 2008, Anil Ambani was the world's 6th richest man at $42 billion. A decade of debt-fueled bets — telecom price wars, power projects, defense ventures — collapsed one by one. Reliance Communications went bankrupt; in 2020 he told a London court his net worth was zero. His brother Mukesh, who took the boring cash-generating businesses in the family split, became Asia's richest man in the same decade.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Leverage stacked on leverage across capital-hungry industries — growth bought with debt in businesses that ate cash instead of producing it.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Debt is a time machine that borrows from your future self — and the future arrives. The brothers ran a 15-year controlled experiment: cash flow beat glamour, decisively.
