Why Did Elizabeth Holmes Fail?

Fake It Till You... Go to Prison

2018YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 $9B valuation → 11 years prisonTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Holmes promised 200+ blood tests from a single finger-prick. The technology never worked - Theranos secretly ran samples on Siemens machines while raising $945M from investors (Murdoch, Walton family, Kissinger on the board) who never saw audited financials. Real patients got wrong results about real diseases. Whistleblowers - two 20-something employees - brought down the $9B company. Holmes got 11 years.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

'Fake it till you make it' applied to MEDICINE - and a board of famous names chosen for prestige, none of whom could evaluate a blood test.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Vision without truth is fraud with better branding. And prestige is not due diligence: nobody on that legendary board asked to see the machine work.
