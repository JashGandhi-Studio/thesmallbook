Why Did Zynga Fail?

The FarmVille King That Rented Its Land

2013YEAR
☠️ TECHCAUSE
💸 About 85% of a $10B valuation in a single yearTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

FarmVille put 80 million farmers inside Facebook in 2009, and Zynga's 2011 IPO valued the company near $10 billion. But Zynga owned no land: its players, its payments and its free advertising all lived inside Facebook's feed. When Facebook changed the rules in 2012 and players moved to phones, where Zynga was late, the crops died in a season. The stock lost most of its value in a year, studios closed, and the king of social games became a tenant farmer with no farm.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Confusing distribution borrowed from a platform with a business of its own, then failing to follow its players to mobile.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If one partner controls your customers, your growth is a loan, not an asset. Build on rented land only while you buy your own.
