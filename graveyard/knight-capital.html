Why Did Knight Capital Fail?

🤖 The Algorithm That Lost $440M in 45 Minutes · 2012 · 💸 $440M before lunch

📜 What Happened

Knight Capital deployed new trading software to its eight servers — but a technician missed ONE server, which still ran retired code awakened by a reused feature flag. At market open, that server fired millions of unintended orders: $7B of accidental positions in 45 minutes, moving 150 stocks. No kill switch existed; staff debugged live while the machine traded. Loss: $440M — four times annual profit. The firm was sold within months.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Manual deployment to critical systems, a reused flag, and no emergency stop — the automation had more authority than any safeguard had coverage.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Automation without a kill switch is a loaded machine pointed at yourself. Deploy like it's surgery: checklists, verification on EVERY node, and a big red button you've actually tested.
