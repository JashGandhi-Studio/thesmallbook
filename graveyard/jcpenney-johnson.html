Why Did JCPenney Fail?

The CEO Who Banned Discounts Customers Loved

2013YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $4.3B in sales, 19,000 jobs in 17 monthsTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Ron Johnson had built Apple's stores — retail's golden boy. Hired to reinvent JCPenney, he abolished coupons and fake markdowns overnight for 'fair and square' everyday pricing, rebuilt stores as boutiques, and refused to test any of it ('we didn't test at Apple'). But JCPenney's customers LOVED the coupon hunt — the fake discount was the fun. Sales fell 25% in one year, a $4.3B evaporation. Johnson was out in 17 months; the coupons came back.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Deleting the ritual customers actually came for, refusing to test because it worked somewhere else for someone else.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

What worked at your last company was an answer to THAT company's question. Customers don't buy your logic — they buy their feelings. Test the medicine before prescribing it chain-wide.
