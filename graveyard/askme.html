Why Did AskMe Fail?

Local search, national burn

2016YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Shut in 2016THE BURN

What happened

AskMe tried to combine Indian local search, deals, classifieds and commerce into one consumer internet bazaar. Celebrity ads and acquisitions made it look like a group being assembled at speed. The pieces never became one habit strong enough to fund the spend. A major investor dispute and cash crunch shut operations in 2016, leaving vendors and employees chasing dues.

The fatal mistake

Buying adjacent features instead of deepening one repeated behaviour.

The lesson

A bundle is not synergy if each part needs its own reason to return.
