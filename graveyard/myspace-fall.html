From #1 Website on Earth to a Trivia Answer

📜 What Happened

In 2006 MySpace passed Google as America's most-visited website. After News Corp's $580M acquisition, the mandate became monetize NOW: ads carpeting every page, features chasing revenue targets, glittering profile chaos, while a clean blue-and-white site for college kids quietly ate its lunch. MySpace optimized quarterly numbers; Facebook optimized the product. By 2009 the race was over; MySpace later sold for $35M.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Harvesting the network before it was ripe — every ad dollar extracted was a user experience mortgaged to a competitor.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Networks are gardens: monetize before the roots are deep and you get one harvest. The product that respects users' experience longest usually owns them forever.
