Why Did Value America Fail?

Tomorrow's store, yesterday's cash

2000YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2000THE BURN

What happened

Value America promised an online superstore without inventory, connecting brands directly to shoppers. Founder Tom Via promotion and a huge IPO made it a symbol of internet retail ambition. The model confused orders, fulfilment broke down, returns and costs mounted, and confidence collapsed after restated expectations. It filed for bankruptcy in 2000, less than a year after peak hype.

The fatal mistake

Removing inventory while keeping all the hard parts of retail.

The lesson

Asset light is not problem light. Fulfilment still votes.
