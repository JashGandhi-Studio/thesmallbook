Why Did The Post Office Horizon Scandal Fail?

The Computer Was the Only Witness, and It Was Wrong

2015YEAR
TRUSTCAUSE
900+ sub-postmasters wrongly prosecuted; bankruptcies, prison terms and suicides; a 2024 law exonerated hundreds at onceTHE BURN

What happened

Britain's Post Office prosecuted hundreds of its own sub-postmasters for theft and false accounting because Fujitsu's Horizon system showed shortfalls in their tills. The shortfalls were often the software's. For two decades the institution defended the machine over the people - denying disclosure, crushing appeals, spending fortunes on lawyers - while the innocent went to prison. It is now called the widest miscarriage of justice in UK history; exonerations and compensation continue.

The fatal mistake

Institutional self-defence against its own members - once the first convictions were won, every later appeal threatened the institution's premise, so the appeals had to lose.

The lesson

When an organisation becomes the sole arbiter of its own systems' errors, it will defend the error before the person. Independent audit and disclosure are not bureaucracy; they are the exit from this exact spiral.
