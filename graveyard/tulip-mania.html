Why Did Tulip Mania Fail?

🌷 When a Flower Cost More Than a House · 1637 · 💸 The first recorded bubble

📜 What Happened

At the peak of Dutch tulip mania, a single Semper Augustus bulb traded for the price of an Amsterdam canal house. Farmers mortgaged land for bulbs; contracts changed hands ten times a day in tavern 'colleges'; everyone knew someone getting rich. In February 1637, at a routine auction, the bids simply... didn't come. Prices collapsed 99% in weeks. The bulbs still grew perfectly good tulips — it was only the story that died.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Buying not the thing but the greater fool expected behind you — and the fools, being finite, eventually ran out.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Every generation gets its tulips, renamed. When the asset's price has fully detached from its use, you're no longer investing — you're playing musical chairs and calling it vision.
