Why Did The Maginot Line Fail?

🧱 The Perfect Defense Against the Previous War · 1940 · 💸 France, in six weeks

📜 What Happened

France spent a decade and 3 billion francs building the world's most sophisticated fortification line against Germany — underground railways, air-conditioned bunkers, retractable turrets. It was genuinely impregnable. So Germany didn't attack it: panzers went AROUND, through the Ardennes forest (declared 'impassable' by French planners) and neutral Belgium. France fell in six weeks with its magnificent forts intact, facing the wrong direction.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Preparing perfectly for the last war's rules while the opponent rewrote them — the plan's strength became its blindfold.
