Why Did WhiteHat Jr Fail?

The white hat becomes a warning

2023YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Brand shut by Byju's in 2023THE BURN

What happened

WhiteHat Jr sold coding classes for children with aggressive promises about future jobs and fame. Byju's bought it for about 300 million dollars during the pandemic education rush. Parents and critics pushed back on sales tactics and claims, while the economics of one to one classes stayed hard. Byju's shut the brand in 2023 as its own crisis swallowed the empire that had bought it.

The fatal mistake

Using parental anxiety as fuel and calling it aspiration.

The lesson

Fear can convert a trial. It corrodes the trust a children's brand needs to survive.
