Why Did Montenegro Airlines Fail?

Small state, heavy wings

2020YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Grounded and bankruptTHE BURN

What happened

Montenegro Airlines carried a tiny nation's flag and tourism hopes with a fleet larger than the home market could feed. State support kept routes alive because connection felt national. Debt and European state aid rules closed around it. The government stopped funding at the end of 2020, flights ceased and bankruptcy followed, with a new airline later created under a different name.

The fatal mistake

Running a national symbol on commercial oxygen without deciding which it was.

The lesson

A country can buy connectivity openly or lose it expensively. Pretending it is profit helps neither.
