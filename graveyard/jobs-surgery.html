📜 What Happened

Jobs's pancreatic tumor was the RARE treatable kind — doctors called it lucky and urged immediate surgery. The man who bent reality in business tried to bend biology: nine months of fruit diets, acupuncture, juice fasts, and a psychic, against the pleading of family and physicians. When he finally operated, the cancer had spread. Biographer Isaacson: Jobs later understood the delay may have cost him his life — the reality distortion field's one fatal casualty was its owner.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Applying 'think different' to a domain where the rules aren't social constructs — biology doesn't negotiate, even with genius.
