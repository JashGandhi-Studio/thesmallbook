Why Did Kozmo.com Fail?

🚲 Free 1-Hour Delivery of a Single Candy Bar · 2001 · 💸 $280M

📜 What Happened

Kozmo promised free one-hour delivery of ANYTHING in cities — a DVD, a Snickers, one magazine — with no minimum order and no delivery fee. Couriers biked $1.50 items across Manhattan at a $10 cost per trip. Amazon invested $60M. The company added a minimum-order rule only in its final months — far too late — and died in the dot-com winter, a cautionary tale that took 20 years to become... every quick-commerce startup's pitch deck.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Trained customers on a promise (free, instant, no minimum) that could never coexist with profit — then tried to untrain them after the habit was set.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

The generosity you launch with becomes the entitlement you die by. Every 'free' has an invoice; decide who pays it BEFORE customers decide it's their birthright.
