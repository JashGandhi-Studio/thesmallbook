📜 What Happened

France's finance minister threw history's most magnificent housewarming at his château Vaux-le-Vicomte — fountains, gold plates, Molière premiering a play — to impress young King Louis XIV. Louis saw a subject living better than his king, concluded (correctly or not) it was funded by embezzlement, and arrested him within weeks. Fouquet died after 19 years in prison. Louis then hired Fouquet's architect, painter, and gardener to build... Versailles.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

He displayed superiority to the one man whose insecurity had an army. The party was an audit request.
