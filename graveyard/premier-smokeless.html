Why Did RJR's Smokeless Cigarette Fail?

🚬 $325M for a Cigarette Smokers Hated · 1989 · 💸 $325M, 4 months on shelves

📜 What Happened

RJ Reynolds spent $325M developing Premier — a 'smokeless' cigarette that heated instead of burned. Focus groups had warned everyone: smokers said it tasted 'like charcoal' and hissed when lit; it required instructions to smoke; and health groups attacked it anyway. The company's own CEO admitted it 'tasted like sh*t.' It launched anyway (sunk costs demanded it), died in four months, and became a Harvard case in ignoring your own research.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The research said no, the sunk costs said go — and $325M of internal momentum outvoted every smoker they'd asked.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Focus groups can't save you if you only hear the answers you paid to like. The most expensive words in product development: 'we've come too far to stop now.'
