Why Did QuadrigaCX Fail?

🔐 $190 Million Locked Forever — Because the CEO Took the Keys to His Grave · 2019 · 💸 $190M of customer crypto unrecoverable

📜 What Happened

QuadrigaCX was Canada's largest crypto exchange, and its 30-year-old CEO Gerald Cotten was the only custodian of the private keys to customer funds — $190 million worth. In December 2018, Cotten died suddenly in India of complications from Crohn's disease. The exchange collapsed: no one could access the cold wallets, and audits later suggested the exchange may have been trading customer funds without reserves all along. 115,000 customers lost their money — literally locked behind keys that died with one man. The crypto exchange that trusted everything to a single point of failure.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A single person holding the keys to everything — no backup, no succession, no transparency — the ultimate concentration of risk.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never let anything critical depend on one person, one password, one device. The 'irreplaceable' setup is a disaster waiting for a bad day — backups and redundancy aren't bureaucracy, they're survival.
