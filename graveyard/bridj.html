Why Did Bridj Fail?

Pop up buses, permanent costs

2017YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Shut in 2017THE BURN

What happened

Bridj tried data driven pop up bus routes in Boston and Kansas City, promising transit that learned where people actually wanted to go. Cities liked the experiment because it felt cheaper than building fixed systems. The model needed subsidy, density and patience at the same time. A hoped partnership did not close, funding failed and Bridj shut in 2017.

The fatal mistake

Needing public patience with private burn.

The lesson

If the buyer is a city, the sales cycle is part of the runway calculation.
