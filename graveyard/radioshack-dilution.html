Why Did RadioShack Fail?

📻 100 Years of 'The Electronics Store' — Diluted to Death · 2015 · 💸 From 7,000 stores and $4B revenue to bankruptcy twice

📜 What Happened

RadioShack was the neighborhood electronics authority for a century — the store where you bought your first soldering iron, your batteries, your CB radio. Then it tried to be everything: phones, kiosks inside other stores, own-brand gadgets, services, random accessories — a brand extension free-for-all that erased what it WAS in the customer's mind. By the time it realized, Amazon owned 'electronics' and Best Buy owned 'stores,' and RadioShack owned nothing. Bankruptcy in 2015; a zombie second bankruptcy by 2017; nearly all stores shuttered.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Diluting a clear position ('the electronics store') into a cluttered everything-store — the mind couldn't remember what RadioShack stood for, so it stopped choosing it.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A position is an asset you defend with your life. The moment you become 'the store that sells phones, batteries, kiosks and random gadgets,' you've become the store nobody needs. Narrow beats broad; clear beats busy.
