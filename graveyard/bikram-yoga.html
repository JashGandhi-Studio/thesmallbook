Why Did Bikram Yoga Fail?

The Hot-Yoga Empire That Melted

2017YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 $6.5M+ in judgments, an arrest warrant and bankruptcy — from 650 studios to a few dozenTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Bikram Choudhury built the most famous yoga franchise on earth: the same 26-posture sequence, the same 40°C room, and a legally enforced monopoly over its own method. At its peak, 650+ studios and millions of students swore by the practice. But the franchise ran on the guru's ego: he sued 21 studios in 2011-12 for using 'his' sequence, and from 2013 former students began coming forward with harassment claims. In 2016 he was hit with a default judgment of more than $6.5M; in 2017 another warrant was issued in Los Angeles while he remained abroad. The same year, his yoga college filed for bankruptcy. The empire that sold 'yoga, copyrighted' collapsed when the practice's own ethics caught up with its business — the first limb of yoga is how you treat people, not the temperature of the room.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Building a spiritual practice on legal control and personal ego — suing fellow teachers while the first yama (non-harm) was violated inside the school.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A practice that harms the people in it is a business, not a teaching. Bikram's lesson: whatever your craft, the moment you defend your 'method' harder than you serve your students, the enterprise is already melting — trust is the only hot asset that cools fastest.
