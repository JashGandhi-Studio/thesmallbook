Why Did Bre-X Minerals Fail?

⛏️ The $6B Gold Mine With No Gold In It · 1997 · 💸 $6B market value → $0

📜 What Happened

Tiny Canadian miner Bre-X announced a monster gold find in the Borneo jungle — estimates swelled from 2 million ounces to 70 million, the 'richest deposit ever.' The stock rocketed from pennies to a $6B valuation; pension funds piled in. The truth: a geologist had been shaving gold from his wedding ring and buying river gold to sprinkle into ('salt') the core samples. When an independent partner drilled its OWN holes: nothing. The geologist fell from a helicopter days before questioning; the stock hit zero within weeks.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Six billion dollars of belief built on samples only the company itself had ever tested — nobody independent drilled until the very end.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Extraordinary claims need independent verification, not bigger headlines. If the only proof comes from the person selling, you're not investing — you're donating with paperwork.
