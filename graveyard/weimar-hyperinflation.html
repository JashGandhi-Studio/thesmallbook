Why Did Weimar Hyperinflation Fail?

Prices Doubled Every Three Days

1923YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
The mark fell from 4.2 to 4.2 trillion per dollar; the savings of an entire middle class went to zeroTHE BURN

What happened

To fund the war and then the reparations, Germany printed. Inflation crept, then sprinted: by November 1923 the dollar bought a trillion paper marks, workers were paid twice daily and shopped at lunch before prices rose, and a loaf of bread cost billions. The Rentenmark stabilised the system in weeks - proving recovery was always possible - but the destruction of savings, pensions and trust had already radicalised a generation that would later listen to worse ideas.

The fatal mistake

Financing obligations by printing while claiming the currency was sound - the state taxed its own savers through inflation because it could not tax them honestly through law.

The lesson

Inflation is a fiscal fact before it is a monetary one. When a government's debts exceed its politics, the currency pays; savers are the collateral.
