Why Did HP TouchPad Fail?

The 49 day tablet

2011YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Discontinued after 49 daysTHE BURN

What happened

HP bought Palm for webOS and rushed the TouchPad into the tablet race. The system had elegant cards and multitasking, and HP had retail reach most rivals would envy. The TouchPad arrived heavier, slower and priced like an iPad without the app library. Seven weeks after launch HP killed the product, fired the strategy around webOS hardware, and cleared stock in a famous fire sale that proved demand existed only at a loss-making price.

The fatal mistake

Confusing ownership of a good operating system with a reason for customers to switch.

The lesson

Polish cannot rescue timing, price and ecosystem when all three point the wrong way.
