Why Did Luckin Coffee Fail?

☕ China's 'Starbucks Killer' Invented $310M of Sales · 2020 · 💸 $310M fabricated; stock -95%

📜 What Happened

Luckin opened coffee shops faster than any company in history — 4,500 stores in three years — and IPO'd on Nasdaq at a $4B+ valuation on a story of app-driven, discount-fueled hypergrowth. An anonymous 89-page short-seller report claimed the sales were staged; Luckin denied everything. Months later its own board investigation confirmed it: employees, directed from the top, had fabricated $310M in sales through fake orders. The stock collapsed 95%, Nasdaq delisted it, and executives were purged.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A growth story so aggressive that when reality couldn't keep pace, the company chose to manufacture the numbers rather than admit the story was wrong.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When a company's identity IS its growth rate, watch what happens the quarter growth slows — the pressure to fake one number is how every giant fraud starts. Stories don't audit themselves.
