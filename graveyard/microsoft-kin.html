Why Did Microsoft Kin Fail?

Dead in about two months

2010YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Axed weeks after launchTHE BURN

What happened

Microsoft built Kin One and Kin Two for teenagers who lived in social feeds. The phones looked friendly, synced photos to the cloud early, and were sold through Verizon with real marketing muscle. The launch landed between worlds. Kin had no app store, weak smartphone features, and data pricing that made parents compare it with real smartphones. Sales were so poor that Microsoft cancelled the line roughly two months after launch and folded the team into Windows Phone.

The fatal mistake

Selling a social phone that was neither cheap enough to be simple nor open enough to be smart.

The lesson

A narrow user does not excuse a narrow product. If buyers compare you with the stronger category beside you, you are in that category.
