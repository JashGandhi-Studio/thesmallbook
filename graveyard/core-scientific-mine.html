Why Did Core Scientific Fail?

The World's Biggest Bitcoin Miner Drowned in Debt

2022YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $1.3B in debt; Chapter 11 bankruptcyTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Core Scientific operated massive server farms in Texas, Georgia, and North Carolina, mining more Bitcoin than any competitor in North America. Riding the 2021 crypto bull run, it borrowed heavily to buy tens of thousands of specialized ASIC computers at peak prices. Then disaster struck from both sides: energy costs spiked due to international conflicts, while Bitcoin's price plunged from $69,000 to $16,000. Mining machines produced coins worth less than the electricity used to power them. Core Scientific filed Chapter 11 in December 2022 with $1.3 billion in debt.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Using short-term debt to buy cyclical commodity-mining hardware at the absolute top of the market cycle.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

In commodity production, low-cost operations survive; leveraged operators die. Never borrow fixed debt against an asset whose selling price can drop 75 percent in months.
