Why Did The McDonald Brothers Fail?

🍔 A Handshake Worth $100M a Year · 1961 · 💸 1% of McDonald's — forever

📜 What Happened

Dick and Mac McDonald invented the fast-food system; Ray Kroc franchised it, then bought them out for $2.7M. In the negotiation, the brothers asked for 0.5% of gross revenues in perpetuity — Kroc agreed, but asked to keep it out of the written contract (a handshake deal, for 'tax reasons'). The royalty was never paid. Today it would be worth well over $100 million a YEAR. Kroc even kept their name — and opened a McDonald's across from their original restaurant to bury it.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Trusting a handshake with a man whose ambition they'd already witnessed — the one term that mattered most was the one left unwritten.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If it matters, it goes in writing — especially with charming partners. Contracts aren't distrust; they're the price of keeping relationships intact when billions arrive.
