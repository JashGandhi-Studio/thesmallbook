☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 The telephone, offered for $100kTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Alexander Graham Bell offered his telephone patent to Western Union — the telegraph monopoly, the most powerful communications company on Earth — for $100,000. An internal committee's legendary assessment: 'This telephone has too many shortcomings to be seriously considered as a means of communication. The device is inherently of no value to us.' Within two years, Western Union was desperately (and unsuccessfully) fighting Bell in court. Bell Telephone became AT&T — for a century, the most valuable company alive.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Evaluating the new technology by how well it did the OLD job (formal record-keeping) instead of the new job (human conversation).

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Incumbents grade innovations against yesterday's exam. The question is never 'is it a better telegraph?' — it's 'what does this make possible that nothing else does?'
