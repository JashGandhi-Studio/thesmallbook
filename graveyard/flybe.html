Why Did Flybe Fail?

Two lives, same storm

2023YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Collapsed again in 2023THE BURN

What happened

Flybe connected British regions that trains and larger airlines neglected. It was easy to criticise and hard to replace, which kept rescuers interested. The first Flybe collapsed in 2020. A revived version launched in 2022 into high costs, weak demand and operational delays, then collapsed again in 2023. The map still needed the routes more than the model could fund them.

The fatal mistake

Believing a necessary service must therefore be a viable business.

The lesson

Social value can justify subsidy. It does not create margin by itself.
