Why Did Pan Am Fail?

✈️ The Airline That Invented Global Travel, Then Missed the Turn · 1991 · 💸 The world's most famous airline

📜 What Happened

Pan Am WAS international air travel — it launched the 747, glamorized flying, even took reservations for moon flights. But it bet everything on jumbo jets right before the 1973 oil crisis quadrupled fuel prices, and it had no domestic US network to feed its international routes when deregulation unleashed competition. It sold its iconic skyscraper, then its Pacific routes, then its Atlantic routes — eating itself to pay for one more year, until the Lockerbie tragedy and Gulf War fuel prices finished it in 1991.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A strategy built for one era — regulated routes, cheap fuel, no competition — flown at full speed into a completely different era.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Selling your best assets to fund a failing model isn't a turnaround; it's a slow-motion liquidation. When the environment changes permanently, change the model — not the fuel you burn to avoid changing it.
