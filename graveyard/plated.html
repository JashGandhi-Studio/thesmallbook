Why Did Plated Fail?

The meal kit gets shelved

2019YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Shut by Albertsons in 2019THE BURN

What happened

Plated was a polished meal kit pioneer that reached television fame and sold to Albertsons, which planned to put kits into grocery aisles. The acquisition looked like distribution solving customer acquisition. Meal kits remained costly to ship and easy to cancel. Albertsons shut Plated's subscription service in 2019 while keeping lessons for store brands, proof that shelf space could not rescue churn.

The fatal mistake

Believing distribution fixes a product people keep quitting.

The lesson

A new channel cannot cure a habit that leaks.
