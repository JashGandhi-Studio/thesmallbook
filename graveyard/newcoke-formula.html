Why Did New Coke Fail?

They Fixed the Thing That Wasn't Broken

1985YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 79 days of chaos, permanent lessonTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Losing taste tests to Pepsi, Coca-Cola changed its 99-year-old formula — backed by 200,000 taste tests showing people PREFERRED New Coke. The tests measured sips; they didn't measure identity. America revolted: 8,000 angry calls a day, hoarding, protest groups, lawsuits. Seventy-nine days later, 'Coca-Cola Classic' returned to national celebration — a senator announced it on the floor of Congress. Sales then surged past Pepsi anyway.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Perfect research answering the wrong question: they tested the liquid when the product was the meaning.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

People don't buy what things taste like; they buy what things MEAN. Data is only as good as the question — and no focus group can measure what customers don't know they'd grieve.
