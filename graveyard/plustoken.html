Why Did PlusToken Fail?

The $3 Billion Wallet That Paid Old Members With New Money

2018YEAR
FRAUDCAUSE
Up to $3 billion from millions of members across China and East AsiaTHE BURN

What happened

PlusToken pitched itself as a crypto wallet with a 'smart dog' trading bot guaranteeing monthly returns of 10-30%. It was a Ponzi of staggering size: investigators traced hundreds of thousands of bitcoin and millions of ethers from over two million members. When withdrawals jammed in mid-2019, members marched on offices; Chinese courts later sentenced the organisers, and its stolen hoard - dribbled onto exchanges - helped crush the 2019-20 market.

The fatal mistake

Running yield that could only be paid by deposits, in a market where every on-chain movement is public - the blockchain itself became the prosecution's ledger.

The lesson

Guaranteed high yield is the whole con, every time. If the return needs new deposits to exist, the audit is arithmetic, not forensics.
