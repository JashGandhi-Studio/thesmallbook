Why Did DaimlerChrysler Fail?

🚙 A $36B 'Merger of Equals' Where Nobody Was Equal · 2007 · 💸 $36B merger sold off for ~$7B

📜 What Happened

In 1998 Mercedes-maker Daimler merged with Chrysler in a $36B deal sold as a 'merger of equals' — the future of global carmaking. In reality German precision-engineering culture and Detroit's scrappy cost-cutting culture despised each other. Executives fought over everything from expense accounts to engineering philosophy; talent fled; the promised synergies never appeared. Daimler's CEO later admitted 'merger of equals' had always been a polite fiction. In 2007 Chrysler was offloaded for roughly $7B.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Modeling the spreadsheets perfectly and the humans not at all — assuming culture is a soft detail that sorts itself out after the signing ceremony.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Culture eats synergy for breakfast. Two companies are two tribes; if you can't explain how the tribes will actually work together on a Tuesday, the deal is a fantasy with lawyers.
