Why Did Jaypee Infratech Fail?

20,000 Unfinished Flats on the Yamuna Expressway

2017YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 9,800 crore INR default; 20,000 homebuyers strandedTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Jaypee Group was Uttar Pradesh's infrastructure kingpin, constructing the gleaming 165-kilometer Yamuna Expressway and India's Formula 1 Grand Prix circuit in Greater Noida. Along the expressway, Jaypee launched massive residential township projects, collecting thousands of crores from over 30,000 homebuyers. But Jaypee diverted customer booking advances into heavy cement, thermal power, and expressway construction that generated inadequate cash returns. When real estate markets cooled in 2015, construction ground to a halt. In August 2017, Jaypee Infratech was dragged to the NCLT in one of India's earliest landmark insolvency cases, leaving thousands of homebuyers waiting years for keys.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Diverting residential customer booking advances to finance speculative infrastructure and industrial projects.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Customer deposits for homes are not working capital loans for infrastructure. Siphoning customer advances into unrelated long-term capex leaves everyone with half-built concrete shells.
