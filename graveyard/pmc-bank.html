Why Did PMC Bank Fail?

The ₹4,355 Crore Scam in a 'Safe' Cooperative Bank

2019YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 ₹4,355 crore hidden in one borrower's account; 1.2M depositors trappedTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Punjab & Maharashtra Cooperative Bank looked like the safest thing in India — a small cooperative bank, government-insured, trusted by working-class depositors. In 2019, regulators discovered the horror: over 73% of the bank's loans — ₹4,355 crore — had gone to one real-estate company, hidden through thousands of fake accounts over years. The bank had 1.2 million depositors, many with life savings over the insured limit. Withdrawals were capped at ₹1,000; people queued for months to get their own money. The 'safest' bank in the neighborhood was the biggest fraud.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

One borrower, one connected promoter, and an auditor system that never looked — concentration risk at a criminal scale hiding behind a 'cooperative' label.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

'Safe' is not the same as 'checked'. A single point of failure in your money, your career, or your business is a bomb — diversify before you need to, because after the freeze, you can't.
