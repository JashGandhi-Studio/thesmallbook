Why Did Signature Bank Fail?

The Crypto Pivot That Sparked a 48-Hour Bank Run

2023YEAR
☠️ MONEYCAUSE

📜 What Happened

Signature Bank spent two decades as a quiet, relationship-driven commercial lender for New York real estate developers, law firms, and private businesses. In 2018, looking for low-cost deposits, it created Signet: a real-time blockchain payments network that attracted billions in deposits from crypto exchanges, hedge funds, and miners. When Silicon Valley Bank collapsed on Friday, March 10, 2023, panicking tech and crypto depositors initiated a synchronized digital run on Signature, withdrawing over $10 billion in hours. State regulators stepped in on Sunday, March 12, seizing the bank and wiping out shareholders in the third-largest bank failure in US history.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Replacing stable commercial deposits with volatile, interconnected, flight-prone crypto ecosystem funds.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Deposit stickiness matters more than deposit growth. Attracting hot, correlated deposits that can move via API over a single weekend turns a conservative lender into a liquidity casualty.
