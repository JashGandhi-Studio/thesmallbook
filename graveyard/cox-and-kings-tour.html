Why Did Cox & Kings Fail?

260 Years of Travel Vanished in Phantom Balances

2019YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE

📜 What Happened

Established in 1758, Cox & Kings was one of the oldest continuing companies on earth, having served as travel agent to the British Army, royalty, and generations of Indian holidaymakers. In the 2010s, promoter Ajay Ajit Peter Kerkar aggressively expanded overseas, buying European educational travel and camping brands on leverage. In June 2019, the company stunned markets by defaulting on a small 150 crore INR commercial paper repayment. Forensic audits soon revealed that over 3,000 crore INR in reported cash balances and sales invoices were completely fabricated with non-existent customers. The Enforcement Directorate arrested Kerkar in 2020.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Fabricating sales revenues and bank balances to cover up disastrous debt-fueled overseas travel acquisitions.

Age is not an audit. A corporate legacy spanning two centuries can be destroyed in 24 months when leadership begins manufacturing accounting receivables.
