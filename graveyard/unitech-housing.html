Why Did Unitech Real Estate Fail?

From 2G Telecom Scandals to Tihar Jail

2017YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 15,000+ stranded homebuyers; promoters imprisonedTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Unitech was once India's second-largest real estate developer, rivaling DLF with a market capitalization exceeding $12 billion in 2007, led by Ramesh Chandra and his sons Sanjay and Ajay. But Unitech took a disastrous detour into the capital-intensive 2G telecom sector through Uninor, which was swept up in the 2008 2G spectrum scam. The Supreme Court canceled Unitech's telecom licenses in 2012. Meanwhile, customer advances from residential townships were drained to service corporate debts, leaving 15,000 homebuyers in Gurgaon and Noida stranded in unfinished towers. In 2017, the Chandra brothers were arrested and sent to Tihar Jail.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Diverting core real estate funds into speculative telecom spectrum licenses and political corruption.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Venturing outside your core operational circle of competence into politically vulnerable licenses using borrowed customer funds will destroy both the new venture and your legacy business.
