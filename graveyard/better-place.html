Why Did Better Place Fail?

🔋 $850M to Swap Batteries Nobody Wanted Swapped · 2013 · 💸 $850M

📜 What Happened

Better Place built battery-SWAP stations for electric cars: drive in, robot exchanges your depleted battery, drive on. It required carmakers to standardize batteries (only Renault half-agreed, with one model), countries to build station networks (Israel and Denmark tried), and drivers to not own their batteries. Fewer than 1,500 cars ever used it. Tesla chose charging; physics and simplicity won.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A model that required EVERYONE (automakers, governments, drivers) to change simultaneously — maximum coordination risk on an unproven bet.

The best strategy needs the fewest permissions. Every actor who must cooperate before your model works is a veto you've volunteered to accept.
