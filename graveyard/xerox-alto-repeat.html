Why Did Kodak's Second Funeral Fail?

They Also Invented the Smartphone Camera's Future — OLED

📜 What Happened

Everyone knows Kodak invented the digital camera and shelved it. Fewer know they did it TWICE: Kodak researchers co-invented OLED display technology in the 1980s — the screens now on billions of phones. Kodak licensed and sold off its OLED business in 2009 for a fraction of its future worth, to raise cash while dying from... digital photography. The same organization that couldn't imagine cameras without film couldn't imagine screens as a business either.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

A culture so anchored to film chemistry that world-changing inventions were repeatedly priced as spare parts.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Your org chart determines what your inventions are worth to you. A breakthrough inside the wrong business model is worthless — build a home for new things or they'll make your competitors rich. Twice.
