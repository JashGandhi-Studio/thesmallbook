Why Did Hubble's Mirror Fail?

🔭 The $1.5B Telescope That Launched Blurry · 1990 · 💸 $1.5B + 3 years of ridicule

📜 What Happened

Hubble launched as humanity's greatest eye — and returned blurry images. The primary mirror was ground 2.2 MICRONS wrong (1/50th a hair) because the testing device itself was misassembled — and two cheaper tests that FLAGGED the error were dismissed, since the fancy instrument 'couldn't be wrong.' The fix (corrective optics installed by spacewalk in 1993) cost hundreds of millions. Post-fix, Hubble became history's most productive scientific instrument — the disaster was always in the unchecked confidence, not the glass.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

When cheap tests contradicted the expensive one, they trusted the expense — instrument prestige overrode disagreeing data.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

When two measurements disagree, that disagreement IS the finding — investigate it, never dismiss the cheaper voice. Redundancy only works if you listen to it.
