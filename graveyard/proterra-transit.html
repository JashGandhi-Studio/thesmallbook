Why Did Proterra Buses Fail?

The Electric Bus Leader Trapped by Fixed Contracts

2023YEAR
☠️ BUSINESSCAUSE
💸 $1B+ in capital burned; Chapter 11 bankruptcyTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Proterra was hailed as the pioneer of municipal transit: thousands of electric transit buses across major American cities and battery systems for commercial trucks. It went public via a $1.6 billion SPAC in 2021, praised by federal leaders. But municipal bus contracts are awarded via multi-year fixed-price tenders with customized specs for every transit agency. When inflation sent battery and steel costs up 40 percent in 2022, Proterra was legally bound to deliver buses at a massive loss on every unit. Supply chain shortages compounded delays, and Proterra filed Chapter 11 in August 2023.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Signing multi-year fixed-price government contracts without inflation escalator clauses during unstable supply chain cycles.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Government contracting with fixed pricing is deadly when supply chains fluctuate. High revenue volume under negative gross margins guarantees you run out of cash faster.
