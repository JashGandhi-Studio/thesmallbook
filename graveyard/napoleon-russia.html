Why Did Napoleon Bonaparte Fail?

❄️ The 400,000-Man Ego Trip · 1812 · 💸 ~500,000 soldiers, an empire

📜 What Happened

Master of Europe, undefeated in his own mind, Napoleon marched the Grande Armée — over 600,000 men — into Russia against every advisor's warning. The Russians retreated, burned their own cities (including Moscow), and let winter fight for them. Roughly 100,000 combat deaths; the rest starved and froze on the retreat. Fewer than 100,000 returned. Europe smelled weakness; two years later: Elba.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Twenty years of victories convinced him the rules — logistics, winter, overextension — applied to other people.
