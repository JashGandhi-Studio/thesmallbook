Why Did Adam Neumann Fail?

🏢 WeWork: $47B of Vibes · 2019 · 💸 $47B → $8B in six weeks

📜 What Happened

Neumann sold investors a real-estate subletting company as a tech platform to 'elevate the world's consciousness.' He trademarked 'We' personally and charged the company $5.9M for it, took loans against his own inflated stock, and flew a private jet with a cereal box of marijuana. The IPO filing exposed everything — losses of $1.6B, self-dealing throughout — and the valuation collapsed from $47B to $8B in weeks. He left with a nine-figure exit package while employees' options went to zero.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The story outran the spreadsheet by $40 billion, and a board drunk on the same story never enforced adult supervision.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Charisma is a multiplier on the underlying business — and the underlying business was renting desks. When the founder's mystique IS the moat, the IPO prospectus is the exorcism.
