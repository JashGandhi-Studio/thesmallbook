Why Did Quirky Fail?

💡 The Invention Machine That Invented Its Own Bankruptcy · 2015 · 💸 $185M

📜 What Happened

Quirky crowdsourced inventions: the community voted, Quirky manufactured — a new product every week, from egg-separating gadgets to the Wink smart hub. GE partnered; $185M flowed in. But shipping ~50 products a YEAR across unrelated categories meant mastering none: flopped products (a $80 Bluetooth speaker-cum-picnic-cooler), inventory pileups, and razor-thin retail margins. Bankruptcy in 2015.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Democratized WHAT to build but not WHETHER it should be built — votes measured enthusiasm, never willingness to pay.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

A thousand okay products lose to one great one. Focus isn't a constraint on innovation — it's the price of quality; and community applause is not a purchase order.
