Why Did Maplin Fail?

The electronics help desk closes

2018YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Administration in 2018THE BURN

What happened

Maplin served hobbyists and fixers who needed a cable, a component and someone who understood the question. Its stores were part workshop, part treasure hunt. Online prices undercut the shelves while the product range widened into general gadgets. Footfall and margins fell together, and Maplin entered administration in 2018.

The fatal mistake

Competing with the internet on stock while forgetting the advice was the moat.

The lesson

If expertise is the difference, make expertise the offer, not a free extra beside cheaper boxes.
