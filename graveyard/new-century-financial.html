Why Did New Century Financial Fail?

The first domino with a logo

2007YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
Bankrupt in 2007THE BURN

What happened

New Century was a star of subprime lending, making loans to borrowers traditional banks avoided and selling the risk into roaring markets. Profits made the model look like financial inclusion plus genius. Early payment defaults forced it to buy back bad loans just as warehouse lenders pulled funding. New Century filed for bankruptcy in April 2007, an early public warning that the housing machine was reversing.

The fatal mistake

Selling risk so fast that buyback clauses became invisible.

The lesson

If the exit door locks when quality falls, you never sold the risk. You rented confidence.
