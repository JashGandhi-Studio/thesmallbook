Why Did Samsung Galaxy Note 7 Fail?

🔋 The $17 Billion Battery That Caught Fire · 2016 · 💸 $17B lost; 2.5 million phones recalled and destroyed

📜 What Happened

The Galaxy Note 7 was Samsung's flagship — a masterpiece that reviewers loved. Then phones started catching fire. Samsung first recalled 2.5 million units, replaced them with 'safe' phones — which also caught fire. The replacement phones had the same flaw: rushed battery design in a race to beat Apple. Samsung recalled everything, destroyed 2.5 million devices, and lost $17 billion. The company survived — and its subsequent apology-and-fix culture became a case study — but the Note 7 remains the most expensive recall in tech history.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Rushing a battery design to hit a launch date — quality and safety were compressed to win a race nobody was timing.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Speed is worthless if the product can burn the customer. The rush to launch is almost never worth the cost of the flaw it ships — slow down the safety-critical parts, always.
