📜 What Happened

Whittaker was already a millionaire contractor when he won America's then-biggest single jackpot: $315 million. Within five years: robbed of $545,000 sitting in his car outside a strip club, sued over 400 times, family relationships destroyed, and personal tragedies that followed the money. 'I wish I'd torn that ticket up,' he told reporters — and meant it.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Sudden money with zero systems: no privacy, no boundaries, no plan — cash sitting in cars, every request answered with a check.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Money amplifies what exists; it fixes nothing. A windfall without a blueprint is an accelerant — the money blueprint matters more than the money.
