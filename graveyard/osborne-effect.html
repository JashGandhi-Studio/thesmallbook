Why Did Osborne Computer Fail?

💻 The Company That Announced Itself to Death · 1983 · 💸 Bankruptcy in months

📜 What Happened

Osborne made the first successful portable computer — $70M in year-two sales. Then founder Adam Osborne proudly pre-announced the next models: dramatically better, coming soon. Customers did the obvious: stopped buying the current model to wait. Inventory mountains, cash-flow collapse, bankruptcy within months. 'The Osborne Effect' is now the textbook term for announcing your own product's obsolescence.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Telling the market to stop buying his only source of revenue — enthusiasm without sequencing.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never kill today's sale with tomorrow's promise. Timing isn't just when to launch — it's when to speak.
