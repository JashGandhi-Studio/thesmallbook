Why Did Garden.com Fail?

Planted at the peak

2000YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Shut in 2000THE BURN

What happened

Garden.com sold plants and gardening advice online with editorial charm and real enthusiasm from home gardeners. It raised public money while ecommerce still felt like a land grab. Shipping live plants is hard, seasons are unforgiving and customer acquisition ate the margin. The company shut in 2000 after failing to secure more funding, a gentle brand inside a violent market lesson.

The fatal mistake

Ignoring that some categories fight physics, weather and seasonality every day.

The lesson

Nature is a supply chain partner that does not care about your burn multiple.
