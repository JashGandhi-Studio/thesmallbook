Why Did Lord and Taylor Fail?

America's oldest store closes

2020YEAR
HISTORYCAUSE
Liquidation began in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Lord and Taylor dressed American department store history from 1826, with holiday windows and a Fifth Avenue flagship that felt civic as much as commercial. Few retail names carried more memory. Ownership churn, department store decline and a failed attempt to become a fashion startup asset left it exposed. It entered bankruptcy in 2020 and the remaining stores were liquidated.

The fatal mistake

Treating heritage as a substitute for a customer proposition.

The lesson

A famous address can make decline look grander. It does not make it slower.

The antidote book

How the Mighty FallJim Collins · Business & Startups · 6 lessons

Decline is a staged disease: hubris → overreach → denial → grasping → death. It's self-inflicted, detectable, and - until stage 4 - reversible.
