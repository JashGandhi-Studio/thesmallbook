Why Did Mike Tyson Fail?

🥊 $300 Million to Bankruptcy · 2003 · 💸 $300M+ career earnings

📜 What Happened

The youngest heavyweight champion ever earned over $300 million — and filed for bankruptcy at 37, owing $23 million. The inventory of the burn: two Bengal tigers ($140k each, plus $125k/year care), a $2M bathtub, dozens of cars, mansions he barely visited, and an entourage of hundreds on payroll. He later admitted he never once looked at his own bank statements.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Total outsourcing of his own money to managers and moochers — earning like a champion, tracking like a child.

Income is not wealth. If you don't manage your money, someone else will — into their pockets. No fortune survives zero attention.
