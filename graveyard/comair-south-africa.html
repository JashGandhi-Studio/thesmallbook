Why Did Comair South Africa Fail?

The franchise loses the sky

2022YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Liquidation in 2022THE BURN

What happened

Comair operated British Airways branded flights and kulula.com in South Africa, surviving decades of aviation shocks. It entered business rescue during the pandemic hoping to return smaller and stronger. A funding plan failed after aircraft groundings and creditor pressure. Comair was liquidated in 2022, ending an unusually long run for an African airline group.

The fatal mistake

Assuming rescue time would be granted because history was long.

The lesson

In aviation, history does not pay for fuel while negotiators talk.
