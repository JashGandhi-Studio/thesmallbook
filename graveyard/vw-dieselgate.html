Why Did Volkswagen Dieselgate Fail?

🚗 11 Million Cars Programmed to Lie · 2015 · 💸 $35B+ in fines, buybacks, lawsuits

📜 What Happened

Volkswagen marketed 'clean diesel' as its weapon to become the world's #1 carmaker. The engineering truth: they couldn't hit US emissions limits at the promised cost and performance. Instead of admitting it, engineers installed software in 11 MILLION cars that detected laboratory testing and switched to clean mode — then emitted up to 40x the legal limit on real roads. Independent researchers with $70,000 of road tests exposed a conspiracy that cost VW $35B+, criminal convictions, and its reputation.

A culture where telling the CEO 'the target is impossible' was scarier than programming 11 million cars to commit fraud.
