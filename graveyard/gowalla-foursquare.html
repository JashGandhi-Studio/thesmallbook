Why Did Balaji's Law: Color Labs Fail?

🌈 $41M Pre-Launch for an App Without a Point · 2012 · 💸 $41M seed — record hype, zero fit

📜 What Happened

Color raised a legendary $41M BEFORE LAUNCH (Sequoia gave more than they'd given Google pre-launch) for 'implicit proximity-based social photo sharing.' Launch day: users opened it, saw empty feeds (it needed OTHER nearby users to function — a cold-start paradox), couldn't understand what it was for, and left one-star reviews. The team pivoted twice, returned some money, and shut down. The name became the era's shorthand for funding-round-as-product.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Raised on vision so abstract that even users who WANTED to like it couldn't find the point — and network-effect mechanics guaranteed emptiness at launch.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If you can't explain the value to a user standing alone in a parking lot, you don't have a product. Cold-start problems must be solved in design, not hoped away with funding.
