Why Did The Salad Oil Scandal Fail?

🛢️ The $175 Million Fake Salad Oil That Fooled Wall Street · 1963 · 💸 $175M fraud (≈ $1.8B today); banks and brokers wiped out

📜 What Happened

Tino De Angelis built Allied Crude Vegetable Oil Refining — and claimed to hold hundreds of millions of dollars of soybean oil in tanks in New Jersey. The collateral was verified... sort of. What the auditors missed: the tanks contained mostly water, with a thin layer of oil on top; some 'full' tanks were secretly connected by pipes so the same oil could be measured twice. When the fraud finally collapsed in 1963, the losses cascaded: a brokerage went under, banks took billions (today's dollars) in hits, and the 'verification' system that trusted a few men's word about some tanks was exposed as theater.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Trusting collateral that nobody actually verified — the entire financial system accepted 'the tanks are full' because it was inconvenient to check.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Audit the balance sheet, not the story. Due diligence is not a form you sign — it's a physical check of what actually exists. Reputation and paperwork are not proof; tanks can hold water.
