Why Did Gerald Ratner Fail?

💍 The £500M Joke · 1991 · 💸 £500M and his own name

📜 What Happened

Ratner built Britain's biggest jewelry chain — then, in one speech to 5,000 businesspeople, joked that his products were 'total crap' and that his earrings were 'cheaper than a prawn sandwich but probably wouldn't last as long.' The press feasted. Customers, humiliated for owning his products, vanished. £500 million of value evaporated; he was forced out of his own company; 'doing a Ratner' entered the dictionary.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Mocking his own customers to look clever in front of elites — one moment of ego at the podium versus decades of brand.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never make your customer the punchline. Your brand is what people feel owning your product — and one sentence can reprice that feeling to zero.
