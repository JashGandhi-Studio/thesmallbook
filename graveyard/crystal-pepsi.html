Why Did Crystal Pepsi Fail?

Clear Cola, Muddy Results

1992YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
A national launch, a Super Bowl ad, and a Van Halen jingle - killed within two yearsTHE BURN

What happened

Riding the early-90s clarity craze, Pepsi stripped the colour from cola and launched Crystal Pepsi with one of the biggest beverage campaigns of the decade. Curiosity sold the first bottles; the second bottle was the problem. People expected clear to mean lighter and healthier, but it tasted like regular cola with a aftertaste of expectation mismatch. Trial collapsed, Coke taunted with Tab Clear, and the sku was gone by 1994.

The fatal mistake

Buying massive trial with novelty while giving buyers no new reason to repeat - the benefit (clarity) belonged to the bottle, not the drink.

The lesson

Novelty is a loan against repeat purchase. If the product's actual experience does not match the story the package tells, scale only spreads the disappointment faster.
