Why Did JOANN Fail?

The Craft Shelf Lost Its Footing

2025YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
All stores closed after a second bankruptcyTHE BURN

What happened

JOANN served sewing and craft customers for decades and saw demand surge during the pandemic. That spike faded while the company carried debt and faced online competition for fabric, craft supplies and seasonal goods. It entered bankruptcy in 2024, emerged with a smaller footprint, then filed again in January 2025. An auction outcome led to the closure of all remaining stores.

The fatal mistake

Treating a temporary category surge as a permanent base while debt limited the time available to redesign the model.

The lesson

A loyal niche is valuable, but loyalty cannot refinance a balance sheet. Use boom years to remove fragility, not to declare victory.
