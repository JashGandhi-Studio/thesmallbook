Why Did AllAdvantage Fail?

Paying people to pretend

2001YEAR
STARTUPCAUSE
Closed in 2001THE BURN

What happened

AllAdvantage paid users to browse with an ad bar and rewarded referrals, turning attention into a multilevel money fountain. It grew explosively because joining felt like being paid by the internet itself. Advertisers would not fund the behaviour at the promised rate, and much of the attention was low quality or gamed. When ad markets fell, AllAdvantage shut in 2001.

The fatal mistake

Paying for the metric advertisers wished they were buying.

The lesson

If users come for the payout, the product is the payout. When it shrinks, so does the crowd.
