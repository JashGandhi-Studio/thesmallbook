Why Did LG's Phone Exit Fail?

Six Years of Losses, Then the Quiet Goodbye

2021YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
About $4.5 billion of accumulated losses; 60 years of phone-making ended with a statementTHE BURN

What happened

LG made genuinely inventive phones - the curved G Flex, the modular G5, the widescreen V30 loved by filmmakers. None of it translated: six consecutive years and roughly $4.5 billion of losses against Samsung above and Xiaomi below. In April 2021 LG announced it was quitting phones entirely, redeploying to TVs and batteries where it actually wins. Some 60 years after making its first telephone, it stopped mid-call.

The fatal mistake

Funding a decade of engineering marvels in a segment where scale economics had already decided the winners - every brilliant phone lost money at LG's volume.

The lesson

Innovation that does not change your margin is art, not strategy. Know the volume your economics require, and exit the segment the day that volume becomes unreachable.
