Why Did IL&FS Fail?

🏗️ The Lender That Froze India's Credit System · 2018 · 💸 ₹91,000 crore in defaults; a nationwide credit freeze

📜 What Happened

IL&FS was India's infrastructure financing giant — the lender behind roads, ports and power projects, rated AAA by every agency. In 2018 it defaulted on a small commercial paper, and the truth cascaded: ₹91,000 crore of debt, a labyrinth of 348 subsidiaries, and governance that let management hide losses for years. The default froze India's entire shadow-banking system — mutual funds holding IL&FS paper froze redemptions, other NBFCs got starved of credit, and the economy felt the chill for years. One AAA-rated company's rot froze the country's lending.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Complexity as camouflage — 348 entities and opaque accounts let losses hide until they were system-sized.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

If you can't understand the structure, the structure is hiding something. Complexity is the tax that honesty pays to survive — simplify until you can see the whole thing, or don't touch it.
