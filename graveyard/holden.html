Why Did Holden Fail?

Australia stops making its own

2020YEAR
HISTORYCAUSE
Brand retired in 2020THE BURN

What happened

Holden was more than a car badge in Australia. The Commodore became a national object, and local manufacturing made the brand part of the country's industrial identity. GM ended Australian production in 2017 as costs and market shifts made local manufacturing hard to defend. Without local cars the badge lost its centre, and GM retired Holden in 2020.

The fatal mistake

Letting identity depend on a factory economics could no longer carry.

The lesson

When place is the brand, moving production is not a supply decision. It is a brand decision.
