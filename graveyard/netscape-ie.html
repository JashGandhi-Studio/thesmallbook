Why Did Netscape Fail?

🌐 Rewrote the Code, Lost the War · 1998 · 💸 90% browser share → AOL fire sale

📜 What Happened

Netscape owned 90% of the browser market. Facing Microsoft's bundled-free IE, it made the fatal engineering call: throw away the entire working codebase and rewrite from scratch. The rewrite took THREE YEARS — an eternity during which Netscape shipped almost nothing while IE improved relentlessly with Windows' distribution behind it. By the time anything emerged, the war was over. Joel Spolsky's verdict became doctrine: 'the single worst strategic mistake any software company can make.'
