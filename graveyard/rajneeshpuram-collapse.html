Why Did Rajneeshpuram: The Commune That Became a Crime Scene Fail?

The Commune That Became a Crime Scene

1985YEAR
TRUSTCAUSE
751 poisoned, a city dissolved, a guru deportedTHE BURN

What happened

In 1981 the followers of Bhagwan Shree Rajneesh bought a 64,000 acre ranch in Oregon and built a city: airport, hotels, restaurants, a police force, thousands of residents dressed in red, and a guru who owned some ninety Rolls-Royces. Within four years the commune's leadership, centred on secretary Ma Anand Sheela, had bused in thousands of homeless people to swing a county election, attempted to register them while quietly drugging the uncooperative, plotted to murder the United States Attorney and a journalist, and committed the largest bioterror attack in American history, poisoning salad bars in The Dalles with salmonella in 1984, sickening 751 people in a test run for suppressing election day turnout. Wiretaps, immigration fraud and attempted murder charges followed. Sheela fled, was arrested in West Germany and served prison time. Rajneesh himself pleaded guilty to immigration fraud in a plea deal, paid a large fine and was deported in 1985; the city was dissolved and the ranch later became a Christian youth camp. Ma Anand Sheela went to prison and later ran nursing homes near Basel. The guru, renamed Osho, died in India in 1990, having always denied knowledge of the crimes built in his name.

The fatal mistake

The commune fused spiritual authority with total institutional control and no external audit: land, police, immigration sponsorship and information all answered to one office, which concentrated in Ma Anand Sheela with no check. Charisma was treated as constitution, so questioning a decision became spiritual failure. The movement also chose enemies as strategy, provoking the county and state politically instead of negotiating coexistence, which militarised internal culture. Finally, the guru either directed the crimes or built a structure where he could not see them; for governance purposes those are the same failure wearing different robes.

The lesson

Any community where the leader cannot be audited will eventually be run by whoever controls access to the leader. Separate spiritual authority from money, security and law, or expect the offices to conspire. Institutions that define dissent as sickness lose their early warning system first and their innocence later. And when a movement's response to a hostile county is to import voters and poison salad bars, the problem is no longer public relations. It is the doctrine that ends justify, which always ends the same way.
