Why Did Zume Pizza Fail?

$445 Million for Robots That Couldn't Keep Cheese On

2020YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $445M (mostly SoftBank)THE BURN

📜 What Happened

Zume's vision: robots make pizza in trucks that BAKE EN ROUTE, arriving fresh. SoftBank valued it at $2.25B. Reality: cheese slid off during cornering (physics doesn't take funding rounds), the ovens-in-trucks concept hit regulatory walls, and the robots were slower than humans. Pivoted to compostable packaging, burned through the rest, shut the pizza business entirely.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

The core physics problem — melted cheese + moving vehicle — was discoverable with one van and one weekend, before the $445M.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Test the riskiest assumption FIRST, at minimum cost. If your business dies when cheese slides, spend day one on cheese, not on the robot fleet.
