Why Did Argo AI Fail?

$3.6B for Self-Driving Cars That Arrived on Time — Never

2022YEAR
☠️ STARTUPCAUSE
💸 $3.6B from Ford & VW — shut downTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Ford and Volkswagen poured $3.6B into Argo AI to win the self-driving race — full autonomy was 'a couple of years away,' as the whole industry kept saying, every year, for a decade. Argo's tech was genuinely respected; the problem was the last 1% of driving reality (weird intersections, rain, humans) devouring years and billions. In 2022 Ford concluded profitable robotaxis were 'a long way off,' both giants stopped funding, and Argo — 2,000 employees — closed almost overnight.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Burning at a pace only justified by a timeline nobody could actually promise — the business plan required the future to arrive on schedule.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Never bet the company on a breakthrough arriving by a specific date. The last 1% of a hard problem routinely costs more than the first 99% — fund milestones you control, not miracles you hope for.
