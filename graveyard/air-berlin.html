Why Did Air Berlin Fail?

The second airline runs out of patrons

2017YEAR
BUSINESSCAUSE
Insolvent in 2017THE BURN

What happened

Air Berlin grew into Germany's second airline through acquisitions and a hybrid promise between holiday and business travel. Abu Dhabi Etihad's money kept the ambition airborne. Losses persisted, strategy blurred and Etihad stopped funding after its own expansion bets soured. Air Berlin filed for insolvency in 2017 and its final flight became a national farewell.

The fatal mistake

Depending on one shareholder's patience to fund a model that never stood alone.

The lesson

A patron can delay the reckoning. Only a working route network can cancel it.
