Why Did IndyMac Fail?

The televised bank run

2008YEAR
MONEYCAUSE
Seized by regulators in 2008THE BURN

What happened

IndyMac grew from Countrywide roots into a major mortgage bank focused on Alt A loans. It looked modern and specialised while housing credit kept expanding. A senator's public letter about its health helped trigger withdrawals, and regulators seized IndyMac in 2008 in one of the largest US bank failures then seen. The mortgage quality underneath made rescue hard to imagine.

The fatal mistake

Holding shaky assets while confidence became the only liquid thing left.

The lesson

Banks die from doubt before arithmetic finishes them. Trust is the real capital buffer.
