Why Did Centra Tech Fail?

A Fake Banker, a Fake CEO, and a $25 Million ICO

2017YEAR
FRAUDCAUSE
$25 million raised from thousands of investors; founders federally convictedTHE BURN

What happened

Centra sold 'Centra Cards' that would let you spend any cryptocurrency anywhere Visa runs - the pitch fronted by celebrity endorsements from Mayweather and DJ Khaled. The product did not exist. The claimed MIT-grad CFO did not exist. Founder Sohrab Sharma had fabricated executives, fake team photos and invented partnerships while the 2017 ICO mania did the fundraising. US prosecutors called it a classic pump: prison terms of up to eight years followed.

The fatal mistake

The entire product was the pitch deck - a payments company with no banking partner, no licenses and no card, sold on celebrity reach while the token market looked up.

The lesson

When the money arrives before the product, the temptation to become the lie is structural. Verify teams, licenses and banking rails; celebrity is not diligence.
