Why Did Nikola Fail?

The Truck That Rolled Downhill to a $34B Valuation

2020YEAR
☠️ FRAUDCAUSE
💸 $34B peak → founder convictedTHE BURN

📜 What Happened

Nikola's promo video showed its hydrogen truck cruising — actually it was TOWED to a hilltop and ROLLED down in neutral, filmed at an angle to look self-propelled. At peak, the pre-revenue company was briefly worth more than Ford. Short-seller Hindenburg's report unraveled it; GM's partnership collapsed; founder Trevor Milton was convicted of securities fraud in 2022.

☠️ The Fatal Mistake

Faking the core capability on camera — betting the future on catching up to a lie before anyone filmed the hill.

🧠 The Lesson (Free for You)

Demos are promises with witnesses. In the age of forensic internet, every staged inch becomes discovery evidence — build the truck before the video, always.
